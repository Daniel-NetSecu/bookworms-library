Exercises

A Checking your understanding

Chapter 1–5 How much can you remember? Check your answers.

1 Where was the sheep farm that Gabriel rented?

2 How much was the toll that Bathsheba refused to pay?

3 What was the name of Bathsheba's aunt?

4 How many of Gabriel's sheep died in the chalk-pit?

5 Name two of Bathsheba's farm workers at Weatherbury.

6 What was the name of the missing maid?

7 What were the words on the seal Bathsheba used for the valentine?


Chapter 6–9 Are these sentences true (T) or false (F)?

1 Gabriel had a shepherd's hut at Weatherbury.

2 Boldwood believed that Troy would marry Fanny.

3 Fanny could have married Troy if she had arrived at the church on time.

4 Boldwood declared his love to Bathsheba as soon as he received the valentine.

5 Gabriel always gave honest advice.

6 Seventy of Bathsheba's sheep died from eating clover.

7 Bathsheba first met Sergeant Troy in her garden.

8 Troy had sometimes helped Bathsheba's uncle on the farm.


Chapter 10–19 Write answers to these questions.

1 How did Gabriel know that Troy never went to church?

2 Why did Bathsheba go to Bath?

3 Why did Boldwood offer Troy money?

4 Why did none of the farm workers help Gabriel save the wheat during the storm?

5 Why did Fanny die?

C Activities

1 You are a newspaper reporter. Write a report either of the shooting at Boldwood's farmhouse, or of Boldwood's murder trial.

2 Write Bathsheba's diary either for the day she watched Troy's sword practice (Chapter 10) or for the day she discovered the truth about her husband and Fanny (Chapter 14).

3 What does this story tell you about farming life in England in the last century? Prepare a talk on this, with as many details as possible.

4 Compare the characters of the three men in Bathsheba's life, Boldwood, Troy and Gabriel.
