THE ENGINE.  

火车头  

Friday, 10th.

星期五，10日

Precossi came to our house Today with Garrone. I do not think that two sons of princes would have been received with greater delight. This is the first time that Garrone has been here, because he is rather shy, and then he is ashamed to show himself because he is so large, and is still in the third grade. We all went to open the door when they rang. Crossi did not come, because his father has at last arrived from America, after an absence of seven years. My mother kissed Precossi at once. My father introduced Garrone to her, saying:— 
今天泼来可西和卡隆一起来我家了。我觉得就算是两位王子的儿子来了，我也不会那么开心地去接待。这是卡隆第一次来我家，因为他相当地害羞，他为自己这么高大还在上四年级而感到羞愧。一听到他们按门铃，我们一家人就都去开门。克洛西没有来，因为他父亲在离开七年之后从美国回来了。我母亲立刻亲吻了泼来可西。我父亲把卡隆介绍给她说：
"Here he is. This lad is not only a good boy; he is a man of honor and a gentleman." 
“就是他。这小伙子不仅仅是个好孩子，还是个高尚的人，是个绅士。”
And the boy dropped his big, shaggy head, with a sly smile at me. Precossi had on his medal, and he was happy, because his father has gone to work again, and has not drunk anything for the last five days, wants him to be always in the workshop to keep him company, and seems quite another man. We began to play, and I brought out all my things. Precossi was enchanted with my train of cars, with the engine that goes of itself on being wound up. He had never seen anything of the kind. He devoured the little red and yellow cars with his eyes. I gave him the key to play with, and he knelt down to his amusement, and did not raise his head again. I have never seen him so pleased. He kept saying, "Excuse me, excuse me," to everything, and motioning to us with his hands, that we should not stop the engine; and then he picked it up and replaced the cars with a thousand precautions, as though they had been made of glass. He was afraid of tarnishing them with his breath, and he polished them up again, examining them top and bottom, and smiling to himself. We all stood around him and gazed at him. We looked at that slender neck, those poor little ears, which I had seen bleeding one day, that jacket with the sleeves turned up, from which projected two sickly little arms, which had been upraised to ward off blows from his face. Oh! at that moment I could have cast all my playthings and all my books at his feet, I could have torn the last morsel of bread from my lips to give to him, I could have divested myself of my clothing to clothe him, I could have flung myself on my knees to kiss his hand. "I will at least give you the train," I thought; but—was necessary to ask permission of my father. At that moment I felt a bit of paper thrust into my hand. I looked; it was written in pencil by my father; it said: 
那男孩低着他那颗大大的、头发蓬乱的脑袋，朝我狡猾地笑了笑。泼来可西戴着他的奖牌，他很高兴，因为他父亲又回去工作了，并且五天来滴酒未沾，还一直让他呆在车间里陪着他，就像变了个人似的。我们开始玩游戏，我拿出了我所有的东西。泼来可西很喜欢我的一列火车，它有火车头，上了发条就可以自己开走。他从没见过这种东西。他目不转睛地看着这些红黄相间的小车厢。我把上发条的钥匙给他，让他自己来，然后他头也不抬地跪在地上玩。我从没见他这么高兴过。他还是一直说“不好意思，不好意思”，并打手势示意我们不要让火车头停下来；玩完后，他把它拿起来，小心翼翼地把车厢放回原位，就好像它们是玻璃做的似的。他生怕他呼出的气弄脏了它们，就又擦了一遍，然后从头到尾仔细检查完，对自己笑了笑。我们都站在他旁边看着他。我们看着他细长的脖子，那对我有天看到在流血的可怜的小耳朵，还有那件卷着袖子的夹克，袖子里伸出两只无力的小手臂，而那双手曾举起来挡住打在脸上的巴掌。哦！当时我真想把我所有的玩具和书本放在他脚下，真想撕下嘴里最后一口面包给他，真想脱下我的衣服给他穿上，真想跪下来亲吻他的双手。“我至少要把火车给你。”我想，可是——我得得到父亲的允许。我正想着，一张纸条塞进了我手里。我看了看，那是我父亲用铅笔写的，上面写着：
"Your train pleases Precossi. He has no playthings. Does your heart suggest nothing to you?" 
“你的火车让泼来可西很开心。他没有玩具。你不想做点儿什么吗？”
Instantly I seized the engine and the cars in both hands, and placed the whole in his arms, saying:— 
我马上两手拿起火车头和车厢，都放在他怀里，说：
"Take this; it is yours." 
“拿着，这是你的了。”
He looked at me, and did not understand. "It is yours," I said; "I give it to you." 
他看了看我，没明白。“这是你的了。”我说，“我把它送给你。”
Then he looked at my father and mother, in still greater astonishment, and asked me:— 
他更加惊讶地看了看我的父母，问我：
"But why?" 
“可是为什么呢？”
My father said to him:— 
我父亲对他说：
"Enrico gives it to you because he is your friend, because he loves you—to celebrate your medal." 
“安利柯把这送给你是因为他是你的朋友，因为他爱你——为了庆祝你得到奖牌。”
Precossi asked timidly:— 
泼来可西羞怯地问：
"I may carry it away—home?" 
“我能把它拿走吗——拿回家？”
"Of course!" we all responded. He was already at the door, but he dared not go out. He was happy! He begged our pardon with a mouth that smiled and quivered. Garrone helped him to wrap up the train in a handkerchief, and as he bent over, he made the things with which his pockets were filled rattle.
“当然可以！”我们一起回答。他已经走到门口了，但他不敢出去。他太高兴了！他嘴角微笑并颤抖着又问了我们一遍。卡隆帮他把火车包在手帕里，他弯腰的时候，口袋里嘎嘎作响。
"Some day," said Precossi to me, "you shall come to the shop to see my father at work. I will give you some nails." 
泼来可西对我说：“你哪天到车间来看看我父亲工作吧。我会送你些钉子。”
My mother put a little bunch of flowers into Garrone's buttonhole, for him to carry to his mother in her name. Garrone said, "Thanks," in his big voice, without raising his chin from his breast. But all his kind and noble soul shone in his eyes.
我母亲把一小束花插在卡隆的纽扣孔里，让他以她的名义把花带给他的母亲。卡隆仍把下巴抵在胸前，大声说：“谢谢。”但是他善良、高尚的灵魂都闪现在他的眼睛里。
