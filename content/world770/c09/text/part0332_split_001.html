"What does it matter to me, sir? Oh! very well, what does it matter to me? Did any one ever hear the like! And the provisions and all that and—” 
“和我有什么关系，先生？很好，和我有什么关系？可从来没人对我说这样的话！这关系到我采购用品，还有其他很多事，还有——”
At any other time she would have overwhelmed her master with reproaches for his breach of trust, but now she followed him into the kitchen before the torrent of words had come to an end. She had guessed that there was a prospect of a boarder, and was eager to see Genestas, to whom she made a very deferential courtesy, while she scanned him from head to foot. A thoughtful and dejected expression gave a harsh look to the soldier's face. In the dialogue between master and servant the latter had appeared to him in the light of a nonentity; and although he regretted the fact, this revelation had lessened the high opinion that he had formed of the man whose persistent efforts to save the district from the horrors of cretinism had won his admiration.
要是其他时候，她肯定会不停地抱怨主人不信任她，但这次她话没说完就跟着主人进了厨房。她猜到应该是有个寄宿病人来了，所以很想见一下。她向热奈斯塔行屈膝礼，很有礼貌地打了招呼，把他上下打量了一番。军人一脸的沉思和忧郁，看上去很严肃。从主人与女仆的对话中可以听出，主人没什么权威。军人对此感到愦憾，也让他曾经给予贝纳西的高评价大打折扣。他曾经很钦佩贝纳西先生，因为贝纳西先生通过不懈努力，让这个地区从克汀病的笼罩中走了出来。
"I do not like the looks of that fellow at all!" said Jacquotte to herself.
“我一点儿不喜欢这个人的样子！”雅科特暗想。
"If you are not tired, sir," said the doctor to his supposed patient, "we will take a turn round the garden before dinner." 
“如果您不觉得累的话，先生，那么晚饭前，我可以带您到花园走走。”医生对他这个所谓的病人说。
"Willingly," answered the commandant.
“很乐意。”指挥官回答道。
They went through the dining-room, and reached the garden by way of a sort of vestibule at the foot of the staircase between the salon and the dining-room. Beyond a great glass door at the farther end of the vestibule lay a flight of stone steps which adorned the garden side of the house. The garden itself was divided into four large squares of equal size by two paths that intersected each other in the form of a cross, a box edging along their sides. At the farther end there was a thick, green alley of hornbeam trees, which had been the joy and pride of the late owner. The soldier seated himself on a worm-eaten bench, and saw neither the trellis-work nor the espaliers, nor the vegetables of which Jacquotte took such great care. She followed the traditions of the epicurean churchman to whom this valuable garden owed its origin; but Benassis himself regarded it with sufficient indifference.
他们穿过饭厅，经过楼梯下面的前厅，前厅位于饭厅和客厅之间，便来到了花园。前厅的玻璃门外是一段石头台阶，台阶把房子靠花园的那面装饰得很漂亮。花园被两条相互交叉的十字形小路分成四块，小路旁种着黄杨。花园的尽头是前主人喜欢且引以为荣的一条林阴小道，那里种着浓密、绿色的鹅耳枥树。军人坐在一条虫蛀的长木椅上，他既没有看葡萄架子，也没有注意那枝杈盘绕的树木，也没有观注雅科特细心耕种的蔬菜。幸好她遵循宗教人士讲究美食的传统，才会有这片园子的蔬菜。不过，贝纳西对园子却绝对漠不关心。
The commandant turned their talk from the trivial matters which had occupied them by saying to the doctor: 
指挥官想结束讨论那些鸡毛蒜皮的事，换个话题，于是问医生：
"How comes it, sir, that the population of the valley has been trebled in ten years? There were seven hundred souls in it when you came, and to-day you say that they number more than two thousand.” 
“先生，这个山谷里的人在十年内是怎么增加到以前的三倍的？您来的时候才七百人，而今天这里已超过两千人了呀。”
"You are the first person who has put that question to me," the doctor answered. "Though it has been my aim to develop the capabilities of this little corner of the earth to the utmost, the constant pressure of a busy life has not left me time to think over the way in which (like the mendicant brother) I have made 'broth from a flint' on a large scale. M. Gravier himself, who is one of several who have done a great deal for us, and to whom I was able to render a service by re-establishing his health, has never given a thought to the theory, though he has been everywhere over our mountain sides with me, to see its practical results.” 
“您是第一个问这个问题的人。”医生回答，“虽然我一直努力想提高本地区的粮食产量，然而，忙碌的生活带来了源源不断的压力，让我根本没有时间去想大规模‘煮石头汤’的方法（像乞丐兄弟那样）。我曾帮格拉维耶先生恢复了健康，他也是大力帮助过我们的人之一。他曾陪我一起翻山越岭查看实践的结果，却从没想过有关这样的理论。”
There was a moment's silence, during which Benassis followed his own thoughts, careless of the keen glance by which his guest friend tried to fathom him.
两人沉默了一会儿，贝纳西先生自己在思考着什么，完全没有注意到他的客人朋友正在密切地打量他，试图看穿他的心思。
"You ask how it came about, my dear sir?" the doctor resumed. "It came about quite naturally through the working of the social law by which the need and the means of supplying it are correlated. Herein lies the whole story. Races who have no wants are always poor. When I first came to live here in this township, there were about a hundred and thirty peasant families in it, and some two hundred hearths in the valley. The local authorities were such as might be expected in the prevailing wretchedness of the population. The mayor himself could not write, and the deputy-mayor was a small farmer, who lived beyond the limits of the Commune. The justice of the peace was a poor devil who had nothing but his salary, and who was forced to relinquish the registration of births, marriages, and deaths to his clerk, another hapless wretch who was scarcely able to understand his duties. The old cure had died at the age of seventy, and his curate, a quite uneducated man, had just succeeded to his position. These people comprised all the intelligence of the district over which they ruled.
“您问我怎么做到的，亲爱的先生？”医生接着说，“很自然，就是依靠需求与满足需求的方法是相互联系的这一社会法则来实现的。这就是奥秘所在。没有任何需求的民族永远都是贫穷的。我刚来镇上时，这儿大概有一百三十户农民，山谷里另外住着二百户人家。当地政府人员的情况与贫穷的村民倒挺相配。区长本人连字都不会写，副区长是个小农场主，住得很远。治安法官是个除了薪水之外一无所有的穷光蛋。无奈之下，他还把出生、结婚和死亡登记的工作交给助手去做。而这名助手也是个连自己的职责都弄不清楚的可怜家伙。以前的神父在七十岁时去世了，他的继任者是一个没什么文化的人，就接替了他的位置。当时，就是这样一群人还被当成精英来管理着这个地方。
"Those who dwelt amidst these lovely natural surroundings groveled in squalor and lived upon potatoes, milk, butter, and cheese. The only produce that brought in any money was the cheese, which most of them carried in small baskets to Grenoble or its outskirts. The richer or the more energetic among them sowed buckwheat for home consumption; sometimes they raised a crop of barley or oats, but wheat was unknown. The only trader in the place was the mayor, who owned a sawmill and bought up timber at a low price to sell again. In the absence of roads, his tree trunks had to be transported during the summer season; each log was dragged along one at a time, and with no small difficulty, by means of a chain attached to a halter about his horse's neck, and an iron hook at the farther end of the chain, which was driven into the wood. Any one who went to Grenoble, whether on horseback or afoot, was obliged to follow a track high up on the mountain side, for the valley was quite impassable. The pretty road between this place and the first village that you reach as you come into the canton (the way along which you must have come) was nothing but a slough at all seasons of the year.
“住在这赏心悦目的自然环境里，本地居民整天却只知道在烂泥里刨食，主要吃土豆、牛奶、黄油和奶酪。唯一能拿去卖钱的就是奶酪，大部分人会用小篮子装点儿奶酪到格勒诺布尔或周边地区去卖。富一点儿的人，或是勤快一点儿的会种些荞麦自己吃，有时也会种些大麦或燕麦，而小麦他们则听都没听过。本地唯一的生意人就是区长，他有一个锯木厂，低价买进一些树，然后再锯成木板出售。由于没有公路，他的木材只能在夏季运送。一般将链子的一头拴在马笼头上，另一头系在钩子上，然后将钩子钩住木头，就这样一根一根地把木头运出去，很是费劲。任何人不管是骑马，还是走路去格勒诺布都得走山上那条路，因为山谷里没法走。从这里到你来时见到的第一个村子之间，这条漂亮的大路（你应该是沿着这条路来的）在当时却是一条一年四季都泥泞不堪的小路。
"Political events and revolutions had never reached this inaccessible country—it lay completely beyond the limits of social stir and change. Napoleon's name, and his alone, had penetrated hither; he is held in great veneration, thanks to one or two old soldiers who have returned to their native homes, and who of evenings tell marvelous tales about his adventures and his armies for the benefit of these simple folk. Their coming back is, moreover, a puzzle that no one can explain. Before I came here, the young men who went into the army all stayed in it for good. This fact in itself is a sufficient revelation of the wretched condition of the country. I need not give you a detailed description of it.
“任何政治事件和革命都不曾影响到这个不可及的地方——因为它完全处于社会动荡和变革之外。只有拿破仑的名字深入人心，受到格外尊重。这要归功于几个退伍回乡的老兵。他们在晚上闲聊时，会对这些朴实的人，讲述有关拿破仑以及他的军队的一些惊险故事。不过，没人知道这几个兵为什么会回乡。在我来之前，这里参军的年轻人一般都留在部队里了。这也充分说明，这个地方有多穷。也不需要我再详细介绍了。
"This, then, was the state of things when I first came to the canton, which has several contented, well-tilled, and fairly prosperous communes belonging to it upon the other side of the mountains. I will say nothing about the hovels in the town; they were neither more nor less than stables, in which men and animals were indiscriminately huddled together. As there was no inn in the place, I was obliged to ask the curate for a bed, he being in possession, for the time being, of this house, then offered for sale. Putting to him question after question, I came to have some slight knowledge of the lamentable condition of the country with the pleasant climate, the fertile soil, and the natural productiveness that had impressed me so much.
“这就是我刚来这个区时的情况。现在，山的另一边有几片地种得很不错，几个区也相当繁华。根本就不用提当时人们住的破茅屋棚了，比马厩好不到哪里去。村民和牲畜差不多都混住在一起了。当时，这里没有旅馆，所以我来的时候只得找助理神父帮忙,给我安排个住处。他当时恰好住在这房子里，因为房子处于待售中。我当时问了他不少问题，对本地的悲惨境地有了些许了解。同时，这里宜人的气候、肥沃的土地和丰富的自然资源也给我留下了深刻的印象。
"At that time, sir, I was seeking to shape a future for myself that should be as little as possible like the troubled life that had left me weary; and one of those thoughts came into my mind that God gives us at times, to enable us to take up our burdens and bear them. I resolved to develop all the resources of this country, just as a tutor develops the capacities of a child. Do not think too much of my benevolence; the pressing need that I felt for turning my thoughts into fresh channels entered too much into my motives. I had determined to give up the remainder of my life to some difficult task. A lifetime would be required to bring about the needful changes in a canton that Nature had made so wealthy, and man so poor; and I was tempted by the practical difficulties that stood in the way. As soon as I found that I could secure the cure's house and plenty of waste land at a small cost, I solemnly devoted myself to the calling of a country surgeon —the very last position that a man aspires to take. I determined to become the friend of the poor, and to expect no reward of any kind from them. Oh! I did not indulge in any illusions as to the nature of the country people, nor as to the hindrances that lie in the way of every attempt to bring about a better state of things among men or their surroundings. I have never made idyllic pictures of my people; I have taken them at their just worth—as poor peasants, neither wholly good nor wholly bad, whose constant toil never allows them to indulge in emotion, though they can feel acutely at times. Above all things, in fact, I clearly understood that I should do nothing with them except through an appeal to their selfish interests, and by schemes for their immediate well-being. The peasants are one and all the sons of St. Thomas, the doubting apostle—they always like words to be supported by visible facts.
“先生，那时候我正在寻找一种新的生活，因为之前的生活已经让我很疲倦。我当时突然有这样的想法：上帝有时会赋予我们使命，让我们有能力去承担并忍受负担。于是，我决定要像一个家庭教师开发孩子的能力一样，开发这个地区的资源。您千万不要过多认为我是多么有善心。其实我做这些，更主要的是为了找到新渠道，实现自己的想法。我决定用我的余生去完成一项艰巨的任务。要改变这个地区需要投入毕生精力，虽然自然赐予了这个地方富饶的资源，但却使这里的人们变得十分贫穷。前进的路上肯定是困难重重，但我却义无反顾。当我发现我可以低价买下神父的房子和大片荒地时，我便决心要在这里为做一名乡村医生而奉献终生——这是一个大家最不愿从事的职业。我决定要成为穷人的朋友，而且并不期待他们给予我任何回报。哦！我对乡下人的本性不抱任何幻想，也不指望在试图提高他们，或改善他们所处环境时不会遇到困难。我从不会把这些人理想化，就按照他们原来的样子接受他们——他们就是贫苦的农民，不能说十全十美，但也不会坏透顶。长期的辛劳让他们无暇沉溺于情感，尽管他们有时也会有所感触。事实上，我最清楚的一点是，我只有满足他们的个人利益，给他们带来立竿见影的好处，才能打动他们。所有的农民都一样，他们都是持怀疑态度的圣托马斯的后代——他们总是相信用事实说话更有说服力。
"Perhaps you will laugh at my first start, sir," the doctor went on after a pause. "I began my difficult enterprise by introducing the manufacture of baskets. The poor folks used to buy the wicker mats on which they drain their cheeses, and all the baskets needed for the insignificant trade of the district. I suggested to an intelligent young fellow that he might take a lease on a good-sized piece of land by the side of the torrent. Every year the floods deposited a rich alluvial soil on this spot, where there should be no difficulty in growing osiers. I reckoned out the quantity of wicker-work of various kinds required from time to time by the canton, and went over to Grenoble, where I found a young craftsman, a clever worker, but without any capital. When I had discovered him, I soon made up my mind to set him up in business here. I undertook to advance the money for the osiers required for his work until my osier-farmer should be in a position to supply him. I induced him to sell his baskets at rather lower prices than they asked for them in Grenoble, while, at the same time, they were better made. He entered into my views completely. The osier-beds and the basket-making were two business speculations whose results were only appreciated after a lapse of four years. Of course, you know that osiers must be three years old before they are fit to cut.
“也许您会觉得我开始做的事好笑，先生。”医生停顿片刻后，继续说道，“我是从引进篮子加工企业开始这项艰巨任务的。这里的穷人都会买晾干酪用的篮子，以及其他做小买卖用的各种篮子。我给一个聪明的年轻人出主意，让他租下河岸边的大片土地。每年洪水过后，在这片区域里冲积形成的淤泥特别肥沃，很适合种柳树。我粗略计算了一下这个地方对柳条制品的需求量，然后去格勒诺布尔找了一名好手艺的年轻工匠，不过他没有资金。当我找到他时，我就下定决心给他提供启动资金。我借钱给他买柳条，直到我的柳条种植者能给他供应柳条为止。我让他一定要比格勒诺布尔的柳条篮子卖得便宜，而且保证质量更好。他完全理解了我的用意。种值柳林和柳筐编织是两项投机生意，因此四年后才见成效。当然，您肯定知道柳树要种三年以上才能砍成柳条。
"At the commencement of operations, the basket-maker was boarded and lodged gratuitously. Before very long he married a woman from Saint Laurent du Pont, who had a little money. Then he had a house built, in a healthy and very airy situation which I chose, and my advice was followed as to the internal arrangements. Here was a triumph! I had created a new industry, and had brought a producer and several workers into the town. I wonder if you will regard my elations as childish?
“自打从事柳筐编织生意以来，我请来的那位工匠的生活大大改善了。前不久，他还娶了个来自圣洛朗迪的女人做老婆，这个女人有点儿积蓄。后来，他还建了座房子，房子建在我给他们选的一个既卫生又通风的地方，而且在房子内部布局上，他也采纳了我的建议。这可真是一次胜利啊！我创办了新企业，而且引进了生产者和几个工人。不知道您会不会把我的沾沾自喜看作孩子气？
"For the first few days after my basket-maker had set up his business, I never went past his shop but my heart beat somewhat faster. And when I saw the newly-built house, with the green-painted shutters, the vine beside the doorway, and the bench and bundles of osiers before it; when I saw a tidy, neatly-dressed woman within it, nursing a plump, pink and white baby among the workmen, who were singing merrily and busily plaiting their wicker-work under the superintendence of a man who but lately had looked so pinched and pale, but now had an atmosphere of prosperity about him; when I saw all this, I confess that I could not forego the pleasure of turning basket-maker for a moment, of going into the shop to hear how things went with them, and of giving myself up to a feeling of content that I cannot express in words, for I had all their happiness as well as my own to make me glad. All my hopes became centered on this house, where the man dwelt who had been the first to put a steady faith in me. Like the basket-maker's wife, clasping her first nursling to her breast, did not I already fondly cherish the hopes of the future of this poor district?
“在编筐工匠的作坊开办初期，每次经过门口时，我都会心跳加速。当我看到这幢新房子绿色的窗板，门口边种着葡萄树，门前放着长凳和几捆柳条时；当我看到屋里一位穿戴整洁的女人正在给一个白白胖胖的、粉嫩的婴儿喂奶，而工人们则在工匠的指导下编着筐，快乐地哼着歌，那位工匠之前还愁眉苦脸，现在却容光焕发时；当我看到所有这些时，我承认，我总会不自觉走进去亲自体验一下编筐的快乐，打听他们的经营状况，让自己沉浸于那种难以言表的满足感之中，因为他们的幸福像是我自己的幸福一样，令我快乐。我所有的希望都集中在这户人家，因为住在这里的那个人是我的第一个忠实支持者。正如工匠的妻子将第一个孩子抱在怀里一样，我不正也怀抱着这贫困地区的未来与希望吗？
"I had to do so many things at once," he went on, "I came into collision with other people's notions, and met with violent opposition, fomented by the ignorant mayor to whose office I had succeeded, and whose influence had dwindled away as mine increased. I determined to make him my deputy and a confederate in my schemes of benevolence. Yes, in the first place, I endeavored to instil enlightened ideas into the densest of all heads. Through his self-love and cupidity I gained a hold upon my man. During six months as we dined together, I took him deeply into my confidence about my projected improvements. Many people would think this intimacy one of the most painful inflictions in the course of my task; but was he not a tool of the most valuable kind? Woe to him who despises his axe, or flings it carelessly aside! Would it not have been very inconsistent, moreover, if I, who wished to improve a district, had shrunk back at the thought of improving one man in it?
“我还有很多事得马上去做。”贝纳西接着说，“由于和其他人的观念不同，我遭到了强烈的抵制，这都是那无知的前区长发起的。当然，后来随着他影响力的减弱，以及我的影响力的扩大，我最终取代了他。我决定让他做我的帮手和盟友，一起为大家做好事。是的，首先，我竭力给这个最顽固的脑子灌输一些启蒙思想。通过满足他的自尊心和贪念，我掌控了这个人。半年里，我们每天都一起吃饭，最终我让他完全赞成我的改良工作。很多人可能会认为这种亲密关系是我工作中最痛苦的事情之一，但是难道他不是最有价值的工具吗？那些看不起自己的斧头，甚至把斧头随便抛弃的人可真是自作自受！更何况，如果我想改变这个地方，却又不愿去改变这里的人的话，那岂不是自相矛盾吗？
"A road was our first and most pressing need in bringing about a better state of things. If we could obtain permission from the Municipal Council to make a hard road, so as to put us in communication with the highway to Grenoble, the deputy-mayor would be the first gainer by it; for instead of dragging his timber over rough tracks at a great expense, a good road through the canton would enable him to transport it more easily, and to engage in a traffic on a large scale, in all kinds of wood, that would bring in money—not a miserable six hundred francs a year, but handsome sums which would mean a certain fortune for him some day. Convinced at last, he became my proselytizer.
“当时我们最急需的就是修一条公路，有了公路，其他问题才能迎刃而解。如果市镇会议能通过这项提议同意修路，就可以把这里同通向格勒诺布尔的高速连接起来，那么副区长将是第一个受益的。有了这条公路，他做起木材生意就更方便，不用费那么大的劲将木村一根一根往外拖。他可以扩大生意规模，经营各种木材，从而赚更多钱——不是像现在每年只赚区区六百法郎，而是某一天，能让他发大财的大笔钱。最终我说服了副区长，他便成了我的新信徒。
"Through the whole of one winter the ex-mayor got into the way of explaining to our citizens that a good road for wheeled traffic would be a source of wealth to the whole country round, for it would enable every one to do a trade with Grenoble; he held forth on this head at the tavern while drinking with his intimates. When the Municipal Council had authorized the making of the road, I went to the prefect and obtained some money from the charitable funds at the disposal of the department, in order to pay for the hire of carts, for the Commune was unable to undertake the transport of road metal for lack of wheeled conveyances. The ignorant began to murmur against me, and to say that I wanted to bring the days of the corvee back again; this made me anxious to finish this important work, that they might speedily appreciate its benefits. With this end in view, every Sunday during my first year of office I drew the whole population of the township, willing or unwilling, up on to the mountain, where I myself had traced out on a hard bottom the road between our village and the highway to Grenoble. Materials for making it were fortunately to be had in plenty along the site.
“整个冬天，前区长都在向居民解释一条好路将成为当地财富之源，因为公路能够方便他们去格勒诺布尔做买卖。他还经常去酒馆同好友推杯换盏。市政议会通过修路决议后，我又去找省长，从该部门管理之下的慈善基金中获得了一些钱，用来支付租用卡车的费用，因为本区缺少车辆，无力担任运输的任务。有些无知的人又在议论我、反对我、抱怨我又让他们回到服劳役的日子。因此，我一定得快些完成这项重要的工程，让他们尽早看到公路带来的好处。就这样，在我任区长的第一年里，每个星期天我都带领全镇老小去山上修路，也不管他们愿不愿意。在那里，我发现村子与通向格勒诺布尔的高速公路之间的那段基石很结实。而且，幸好，沿途的石料也很丰富。
"The tedious enterprise called for a great deal of patience on my part. Some who were ignorant of the law would refuse at times to give their contribution of labor; others again, who had not bread to eat, really could not afford to lose a day. Corn had to be distributed among these last, and the others must be soothed with friendly words. Yet by the time we had finished two-thirds of the road, which in all is about two leagues in length, the people had so thoroughly recognized its advantages that the remaining third was accomplished with a spirit that surprised me. I added to the future wealth of the Commune by planting a double row of poplars along the ditch on either side of the way. The trees are already almost worth a fortune, and they make our road look like a king's highway. It is almost always dry, by reason of its position, and it was so well made that the annual cost of maintaining it is a bare two hundred francs. I must show it to you, for you cannot have seen it; you must have come by the picturesque way along the valley bottom, a road which the people decided to make for themselves three years later, so as to connect the various farms that were made there at that time. In three years ideas had rooted themselves in the common sense of this township, hitherto so lacking in intelligence that a passing traveler would perhaps have thought it hopeless to attempt to instil them. But to continue.
“这项乏味的工程需要我付出极大的耐心。有些不懂法律的人有时会拒绝出工，而其他有些连一日三餐都没着落的人也实在不可能丢下手里的活来修路。所以，只能给这些人分一些谷物，对其他人只能苦口婆心地好言相劝。当路修到三分之二，大约两里格时，这些人彻底意识到了公路给他们带来的好处。于是个个干劲十足，剩下的部分很快就完工了，他们的干劲着实让我感到惊讶。为了给区里创收，我又安排在公路两旁种了两排白杨。这些树已经成材了，同时也让这条路看上去像条王道。由于公路的地理位置较好，能够长期保持干燥，所以每年只需要花两百法郎来保养。我一定要领您去参观一下这条路，您来时肯定没看到。估计您走的是谷底那条沿途风景不错的路，那是人们三年后自己修的，当时是为了让各个农场之间相通。在三年的时间里，这些本没有知识的居民终于接受了一些常识性的想法。要是在以前，他们是绝不可能接受一个外乡人给他们灌输的任何思想。当然，事情还没完。
"The establishment of the basket-maker was an example set before these poverty-stricken folk that they might profit by it. And if the road was to be a direct cause of the future wealth of the canton, all the primary forms of industry must be stimulated, or these two germs of a better state of things would come to nothing. My own work went forward by slow degrees, as I helped my osier farmer and wicker-worker and saw to the making of the road.
“引进柳条制品企业给这些穷苦的居民树了一个典型，让他们尝到了甜头。如果一条路都可以成为未来能给本区带来财富的直接原因的话，那么就更应该鼓励创办各种主要企业。不然的话，之前的两个良好开端就没有任何意义。在帮助我的柳树种植户和柳条制品匠的同时，筑路过程中，我自己的事业也缓慢地进展。
"I had two horses, and the timber merchant, the deputy-mayor, had three. He could only have them shod whenever he went over to Grenoble, so I induced a farrier to take up his abode here, and undertook to find him plenty of work. On the same day I met with a discharged soldier, who had nothing but his pension of a hundred francs, and was sufficiently perplexed about his future. He could read and write, so I engaged him as secretary to the mayor; as it happened, I was lucky enough to find a wife for him, and his dreams of happiness were fulfilled.
“我拥有两匹马。那个木材商，也就是副区长已经有三匹了。他每次给马钉掌都还得跑去格勒诺布尔，所以我请来一位蹄铁匠到这里定居，而且想办法给他找活儿做。也就在那天，我碰到一位退伍老兵。他当时除了一百法郎退伍补助以外，什么都没有，对未来也很迷茫。不过他能读会写，于是我让他做了区长秘书。后来，很幸运的是，我还帮他找了个老婆，终于圆了他的幸福梦。
"Both of these new families needed houses, as well as the basket-maker and twenty-two others from the cretin village, soon afterwards twelve more households were established in the place. The workers in each of these families were at once producers and consumers. They were masons, carpenters, joiners, slaters, blacksmiths, and glaziers; and there was work enough to last them for a long time, for had they not their own houses to build when they had finished those for other people? Seventy, in fact, were build in the Commune during my second year of office. One form of production demands another. The additions to the population of the township had created fresh wants, hitherto unknown among these dwellers in poverty. The wants gave rise to industries, and industries to trade, and the gains of trade raised the standard of comfort, which in its turn gave them practical ideas.
“这两个新家庭和编织匠以及二十二户从克汀病村搬出的人家都需要房子。不久后，又有十二户人家在这儿安家了。这些人家的劳力既是生产者也是消费者。他们中有泥匠、木匠、细木匠、瓦匠、铁匠、玻璃匠。虽然他们长期都有活儿做，但是难道他们给别人建了房子后，不应该给自己建所房子吗？事实上，在我上任的第二年，本区就建起了七十幢房子。一种生产方式往往会带动另一种生产方式。镇上人口的增多推动了新的需求，而这些需求是这些穷人以前听都没有听过的。需求带动了企业的发展，从而促进了贸易的繁荣，使居民的生活水平大大提高。反过来，他们对生活也有了更多实际的思考。
"The various workmen wished to buy their bread ready baked, so we came to have a baker. Buckwheat could no longer be the food of a population which, awakened from its lethargy, had become essentially active. They lived on buckwheat when I first came among them, and I wished to effect a change to rye, or a mixture of rye and wheat in the first instance, and finally to see a loaf of white bread even in the poorest household. Intellectual progress, to my thinking, was entirely dependent on a general improvement in the conditions of life. The presence of a butcher in the district says as much for its intelligence as for its wealth. The worker feeds himself, and a man who feeds himself thinks. I had made a very careful study of the soil, for I foresaw a time when it would be necessary to grow wheat. I was sure of launching the place in a very prosperous agricultural career, and of doubling the population, when once it had begun to work. And now the time had come.
“各行各业的工人希望能吃到现成的烤面包，所以我们又请了一位面包师傅。从萎靡不振中走出来的这些人变得格外积极，他们不想再吃荞麦了。我刚来这儿时，他们主要吃荞麦。后来我想改进一下，先让他们吃上黑麦或黑麦与小麦的混合麦，最终希望他们中最穷的人都能吃上白面包。在我看来，思想的进步完全依赖于生活条件的普遍改善。一个地方有了屠户，说明当地人的思想和经济状况都比较好。一个人靠劳动吃饭，吃饱饭就会去思考。我细心研究了一下这里的土质，我做了一个预测，何时有必要种上小麦。我坚信，只要肯干，一定能让这里的农业发展兴旺，让人口翻一倍。而现在良好时机已到。
"M. Gravier, of Grenoble, owned a great deal of land in the commune, which brought him in no rent, but which might be turned into corn-growing land. He is the head of a department in the Prefecture, as you know. It was a kindness for his own countryside quite as much as my earnest entreaties that won him over. He had very benevolently yielded to my importunities on former occasions, and I succeeded in making it clear to him that in so doing he had wrought unconsciously for his own benefit. After several days spent in pleadings, consultation, and talk, the matter was thrashed out. I undertook to guarantee him against all risks in the undertaking, from which his wife, a woman of no imagination, sought to frighten him. He agreed to build four farmhouses with a hundred acres of land attached to each, and promised to advance the sums required to pay for clearing the ground, for seeds, ploughing gear, and cattle, and for making occupation roads.
“家住格勒诺布尔的格拉维耶先生，在这个区拥有大片土地。他没靠这些地收租，但是应该可以改种麦子。正如您所了解的，他是省政府的一名局长。由于他热爱自己的家乡，同时也抵制不住我苦口婆心的劝说，最终他接受了我的提议。当然，以前他已经很和善地接受过我多次请求。我也让他意识到，这样做在不经意间也会让他获益。经过多天的请求、商量和探讨，事情终于谈妥了。因为他那没什么远见的妻子曾拿风险说事，所以我向他保证所有风险都由我来承担。最后，他同意建四个农场，每个农场占地一百英亩，相互毗邻。而且承诺预付开支，用来垦地，购买种子、农具和牲畜，以及修路。
"I myself also started two farms, quite as much for the sake of bringing my waste land into cultivation as with a view to giving an object-lesson in the use of modern methods in agriculture. In six weeks' time the population of the town increased to three hundred people. Homes for several families must be built on the six farms; there was a vast quantity of land to be broken up; the work called for laborers. Wheelwrights, drainmakers, journeymen, and laborers of all kinds flocked in. The road to Grenoble was covered with carts that came and went. All the countryside was astir. The circulation of money had made every one anxious to earn it, apathy had ceased, the place had awakened.
“我自己也开了两个农场，一方面为了利用一下买来的荒地，另一方面也是想用实例证明给村民看，让他们看一下如何运用现代化技术来发展农业。在短短的六周时间里，村里人口增至三百。六个农场都得给几家住户建房子，大片土地要开垦，这些都需要大量劳力。车轮匠、管道工、熟练工人及各行业的劳力都大量涌入本地。通向格勒诺布尔的大路，车水马龙。整个乡村一派欣欣向荣的景象。金钱的流通让每个人都产生了挣钱的念头，他们不再萎靡不振，整个地区都苏醒了。
"The story of M. Gravier, one of those who did so much for this canton, can be concluded in a few words. In spite of cautious misgivings, not unnatural in a man occupying an official position in a provincial town, he advanced more than forty thousand francs, on the faith of my promises, without knowing whether he should ever see them back again. To-day every one of his farms is let for a thousand francs. His tenants have thriven so well that each of them owns at least a hundred acres, three hundred sheep, twenty cows, ten oxen, and five horses, and employs more than twenty persons.
“格拉维诺布尔是为本地区做出大贡献的人之一，他的事迹可以用几句话简单地概括。作为一个在省城政府部门就职的人，处事谨慎不足为怪。不管怎样，他最终还是相信我，预付了四万多法郎，虽然也不知道这钱能否收回。现在，他的每个农场以一千法郎的价格租出去了。租户把农场经营得很不错。每个农场至少有一百英亩土地、三百只羊、二十头奶牛、十头耕牛、五匹马，还请了二十多个人帮忙打理农场。
"But to resume. Our farms were ready by the end of the fourth year. Our wheat harvest seemed miraculous to the people in the district, heavy as the first crop off the land ought to be. How often during that year I trembled for the success of my work! Rain or drought might spoil everything by diminishing the belief in me that was already felt. When we began to grow wheat, it necessitated the mill that you have seen, which brings me in about five hundred francs a year. So the peasants say that 'there is luck about me' (that is the way they put it), and believe in me as they believe in their relics. These new undertakings—the farms, the mill, the plantations, and the roads —have given employment to all the various kinds of workers whom I had called in. Although the buildings fully represent the value of the sixty thousand francs of capital, which we sunk in the district, the outlay was more than returned to us by the profits on the sales which the consumers occasioned. I never ceased my efforts to put vigor into this industrial life which was just beginning. A nurseryman took my advice and came to settle in the place, and I preached wholesome doctrine to the poor concerning the planting of fruit trees, in order that some day they should obtain a monopoly of the sale of fruit in Grenoble.
“我接着讲。在我上任的第四年年末，农场建好了。这一年小麦获得了丰收，让当地人大吃一惊，跟地里的头一茬庄稼应出的产量一样高。整整一年时间，我为这项工作的成败操了多少心啊！洪涝灾害可能会毁掉一切，也会让我已有的信心消失殆尽。开始种植小麦后，也就有了您所看到的那间磨坊，它每年可以给我创收五百法郎。农民们都说‘我运气好’（他们是这样说的），因此就像信奉圣物一样相信我。这几项新的工程——农场、磨坊、林场和公路——让我引进的各行各业工人都有事可做。虽然我们在这里建设的这几项工程投入了六万法郎，但消费者给我们带来的收益却远不止这个数。我一直都致力于推动刚刚起步的工业继续发展下去，从未间断过。一个花圃工采纳了我的建议，在这里安家了。我还鼓励穷人种果树，希望有一天，他们能够垄断格勒诺布尔的水果市场。
“'You take your cheeses there as it is,' I used to tell them, 'why not take poultry, eggs, vegetables, game, hay and straw, and so forth?' All my counsels were a source of fortune; it was a question of who should follow them first. A number of little businesses were started; they went on at first but slowly, but from day to day their progress became more rapid; and now sixty carts full of the various products of the district set out every Monday for Grenoble, and there is more buckwheat grown for poultry food than they used to sow for human consumption. The trade in timber grew to be so considerable that it was subdivided, and since the fourth year of our industrial era, we have had dealers in firewood, squared timber, planks, bark, and later on, in charcoal. In the end four new sawmills were set up, to turn out the planks and beams of timber.
“‘你们在那儿卖奶酪，’我常跟他们讲，‘那为什么不去卖家禽、鸡蛋、蔬菜、野味、干草这样的东西呢？’我的建议都会成为他们赚钱的门路，问题只在于谁愿意第一个去尝试。就这样，很多小生意都发展起来了，起初发展较慢，后来的发展一天比一天快。现在，每周一都有六十多辆装满各种产品的车辆开往格勒诺布尔。而种来用以喂家禽的荞麦比以前人们自己吃的还要多。木材业已经发展到相当大规模，开始进行细分。从创立企业的第四年起，我们已经有了各种专营商，比如木柴商、方木商、木板商、树皮商及木炭商。最后，还新建了四家锯木厂，专门将木头加工成木板和梁木。
"When the ex-mayor had acquired a few business notions, he felt the necessity of learning to read and write. He compared the prices that were asked for wood in various neighborhoods, and found such differences in his favor, that he secured new customers in one place after another, and now a third of the trade in the department passes through his hands. There has been such a sudden increase in our traffic that we find constant work for three wagon-builders and two harness-makers, each of them employing three hands at least. Lastly, the quantity of ironware that we use is so large that an agricultural implement and tool-maker has removed into the town, and is very well satisfied with the result.
“前区长掌握了一些做生意的知识后，开始觉得有必要学会读写。他比较了一下附近各地区的木材价格，发现自己有价格优势。随后在各地不断地抓住新的客户群，到现在，木材市场上，他已经占有三分之一的贸易额。由于交通的迅猛发展，三个马车师傅和两个马具师傅的生意也很红火，他们每个人至少还雇了三个帮手。最后，由于铁器的需求量大增，一位制造农业器具的铁匠师傅也搬到镇上开铺子，对自己的生意很满意。
"The desire of gain develops a spirit of ambition, which has ever since impelled our workers to extend their field from the township to the canton, and from the canton to the department, so as to increase their profits by increasing their sales. I had only to say a word to point out new openings to them, and their own sense did the rest. Four years had been sufficient to change the face of the township. When I had come through it first, I did not catch the slightest sound; but in less than five years from that time, there was life and bustle everywhere. The gay songs, the shrill or murmuring sounds made by the tools in the workshops rang pleasantly in my ears. I watched the comings and goings of a busy population congregated in the clean and wholesome new town, where plenty of trees had been planted. Every one of them seemed conscious of a happy lot, every face shone with the content that comes through a life of useful toil.
“对财富的追求必然激发人们的野心，它激励着工匠们将自己的生意范围从小镇扩大到区，又从区走向全省，通过增加销量获得更多利润。我只需要给他们指一指新的赚钱门路，然后他们按自己的意愿去落实就行了。四年的时间足以让这个镇子焕然一新。我第一次经过这儿时，听不到任何轻微的响动。然而不到五年的时间，小镇到处充满了生气，热闹非凡。快活的歌声，还有从工厂里传出的尖锐的或低低的工具发出的声音都回荡在我耳畔，令人愉快。我看到新镇的居民们快乐地忙碌着，新镇干净卫生，还种满了各种树。每个人似乎都感受到幸福，每张脸上都流露出对有意义的生活无比满足的神情。
"I look upon these five years as the first epoch of prosperity in the history of our town," the doctor went on after a pause. "During that time I have prepared the ground and sowed the seed in men's minds as well as in the land. Henceforward industrial progress could not be stayed, the population was bound to go forward. A second epoch was about to begin. This little world very soon desired to be better clad. A shoemaker came, and with him a haberdasher, a tailor, and a hatter. This dawn of luxury brought us a butcher and a grocer, and a midwife, who became very necessary to me, for I lost a great deal of time over maternity cases. The stubbed wastes yielded excellent harvests, and the superior quality of our agricultural produce was maintained through the increased supply of manure. My enterprise could now develop itself; everything followed on quite naturally.
“我把这五年看作小镇繁荣史上的第一个阶段。”医生停顿了一下，接着说，“在这段时间里，我整顿好地，播下了种子，同时也在人们的头脑中种下了思想的种子。从此，这里的企业蓬勃发展起来了，人口必然地在不断增长。第二个阶段即将到来。不久后，这个小社会就开始讲究，希望穿得好些。于是陆续有鞋店、缝纫用品店、裁缝店和帽子店开业。小镇初步的繁荣使这里有了间肉铺、杂货铺和一个接生婆。接生婆的到来对我来说非常有必要，因为接生耗费了我大量的时间。开垦荒地收成不错，肥料供应量的增加保证了农业的高产。我的事业也不断发展，一切顺理成章地进行。
"When the houses had been rendered wholesome, and their inmates gradually persuaded to feed and clothe themselves better, I wanted the dumb animals to feel the benefit of these beginnings of civilization. All the excellence of cattle, whether as a race or as individuals, and, in consequence, the quality of the milk and meat, depends upon the care that is expended upon them. I took the sanitation of cowsheds for the text of my sermons. I showed them how an animal that is properly housed and well cared for is more profitable than a lean neglected beast, and the comparison wrought a gradual change for the better in the lot of the cattle in the Commune. Not one of them was ill treated. The cows and oxen were rubbed down as in Switzerland and Auvergne. Sheep-folds, stables, byres, dairies, and barns were rebuilt after the pattern of the roomy, well-ventilated, and consequently healthy steadings that M. Gravier and I had constructed. Our tenants became my apostles. They made rapid converts of unbelievers, demonstrating the soundness of my doctrines by their prompt results. I lent money to those who needed it, giving the preference to hardworking poor people, because they served as an example. Any unsound or sickly cattle or beasts of poor quality were quickly disposed of by my advice, and replaced by fine specimens. In this way our dairy produce came, in time, to command higher prices in the market than that sent by other communes. We had splendid herds, and as a consequence, capital leather.
“在让居民有健康的居住环境，吃穿条件也逐渐有所改善后，我希望这里的牲畜也能感受到文明带来的好处。牛的质量好坏问题，无论是一个品种还是单个牛，牛奶和肉的品质问题都取决于人们的照料。我倡议大家把牛棚打扫干净。我向他们展示，一头所处环境不错又被精心照顾的牲口能够带来多么丰厚的收入，而照顾不好的却只能带来微薄的收入。通过比较，人们渐渐地开始改进饲养方法。从此，没有一头牲口再受到虐待了。本区的奶牛和耕牛都被打理得干净整洁，就像瑞士和奥弗涅地区的一样。按照格拉维耶和我家的标准，所有村民家的羊圈、马厩、牛棚、奶制品作坊和谷仓都进行了重建，都变得更宽敞、透气而卫生。我们的佃户也成了我积极的宣传者。他们拿已经取得的成果向那些怀疑者证明了我的主张是多么明智。我还借钱给急需用钱的人，特别是那些勤劳的穷人，因为他们可以成为大家的典型。我还建议村民把自家生病的、质量不好的牲口都处理掉，换一些好牲口。这样一来，我们区产的奶制品的价格比其他地区供应的都高。由于有了很好的牲口，也就有了上等的皮革。
"This step forward was of great importance, and in this wise. In rural economy nothing can be regarded as trifling. Our hides used to fetch scarcely anything, and the leather we made was of little value, but when once our leather and hides were improved, tanneries were easily established along the waterside. We became tanners, and business rapidly increased.
“这一改进意义非凡，实属明智之举。农村经济无小事。以前这里的树皮基本没用，皮革也不值什么钱。可自从皮革和树皮质量提高后，这里很快就在河边建起了制革厂。也有了制革工人，生意很快就红火起来。
"Wine, properly speaking, had been hitherto unknown; a thin, sour beverage like verjuice had been their only drink, but now wineshops were established to supply a natural demand. The oldest tavern was enlarged and transformed into an inn, which furnished mules to pilgrims to the Grand Chartreuse who began to come our way, and after two years there was just enough business for two innkeepers.
“可以说，这里的人以前都不知道葡萄酒为何物，他们只喝过又稀又酸的类似果汁的东西。而现在大家已习惯喝葡萄酒，卖酒的店铺也陆续开张。最老的那家小酒馆扩大规模，并改成了客栈，还给那些经过我们这儿去大沙特勒斯山朝圣的人提供骡子。两年后由于这里生意往来频繁，住店的人也很多，对两个旅店老板来说足够了。
"The justice of the peace died just as our second prosperous epoch began, and luckily for us, his successor had formerly been a notary in Grenoble who had lost most of his fortune by a bad speculation, though enough of it yet remained to cause him to be looked upon in the village as a wealthy man. It was M. Gravier who induced him to settle among us. He built himself a comfortable house and helped me by uniting his efforts to mine. He also laid out a farm, and broke up and cleaned some of the waste land, and at this moment he has three chalets up above on the mountain side. He has a large family. He dismissed the old registrar and the clerk, and in their place installed better-educated men, who worked far harder, moreover, than their predecessors had done. One of the heads of these two new households started a distillery of potato-spirit, and the other was a wool-washer; each combined these occupations with his official work, and in this way two valuable industries were created among us.
“在我们的发展刚进入第二个繁荣期时，本区的治安法官去世了。不过幸好，接替者是格勒诺布尔的前任公证人。他在投机活动中亏损了不少，不过剩下的那些钱也足够他在这里做个富人。正是格拉维耶先生劝他来这里定居的。他建了幢舒适的房子，也和我共同努力，做好这里的工作。他也开了一个农场，开垦了一些荒地。同时，他还在山上建了三幢房子。他拥有了一个大家庭。他解雇了以前的户籍员和文书，另外找了两个人来做。这两个人文化程度要比前任的更高，工作也更卖力。这新来的两家中有一家开了一个土豆酿酒厂，另一家开了个毛织物洗涤厂。他们都是一边搞好本职工作，一边办好自己的厂子。这样一来，我们又有了两个很有价值的产业。
"Now that the Commune had some revenues of its own, no opposition was raised in any quarter when they were spent on building a town-hall, with a free school for elementary education in the building and accommodation for a teacher. For this important post I had selected a poor priest who had taken the oath, and had therefore been cast out by the department, and who at last found a refuge among us for his old age. The schoolmistress is a very worthy woman who had lost all that she had, and was in great distress. We made up a nice little sum for her, and she has just opened a boarding-school for girls to which the wealthy farmers hereabouts are beginning to send their daughters.
“现在区里自己有了一定的财政收入，我用其中一部分建了一座公所，并在那里开了一所免费就读的小学，以及教师公寓，这都没有遇到任何异议。我选了一位宣誓派的穷教士来担任教师这个重要职务，其他地方没人收留这个可怜的人。他晚年时，终于在我们这儿找到了落脚的地方。另外还有一位女教师曾是位有钱人，不过现在已经一无所有了，也极度困苦。我们给她提供了一小笔可观的资金，她就办了所女子寄宿学校。本地有钱的佃农都开始把女儿送到那儿上学。
"If so far, sir, I have been entitled to tell you the story of my own doings as the chronicle of this little spot of earth, I have reached the point where M. Janvier, the new parson, began to divide the work of regeneration with me.  He has been a second Fenelon, unknown beyond the narrow limits of a country parish, and by some secret of his own has infused a spirit of brotherliness and of charity among these folk that has made them almost like one large family. M. Dufau, the justice of the peace, was a late comer, but he in an equal degree deserves the gratitude of the people here.
“先生，虽然我已经有资格向您讲述我为这个小地方所做的一切，但我还是不得不提让维埃先生——新一任教区神父。他帮我分担工作，为本地的发展做出了巨大贡献。他是第二个费讷隆，虽然教区之外没人知道他这个人，但在本地，他自己却成功地使兄弟情谊、慈善友爱的精神得以传播，让这里几乎成了一个和睦的大家庭。杜弗先生，也就是这里的治安法官，他来得很晚，但也同样值得大家感激。
"I will put the whole position before you in figures that will make it clearer than any words of mine. At this moment the Commune owns two hundred acres of woodland, and a hundred and sixty acres of meadow. Without running up the rates, we give a hundred crowns to supplement the cure's stipend, we pay two hundred francs to the rural policeman, and as much again to the schoolmaster and schoolmistress. The maintenance of the roads costs us five hundred francs, while necessary repairs to the townhall, the parsonage, and the church, with some few other expenses, also amount to a similar sum. In fifteen years' time there will be a thousand francs worth of wood to fell for every hundred francs' worth cut now, and the taxes will not cost the inhabitants a penny. This Commune is bound to become one of the richest in France. But perhaps I am taxing your patience, sir?" said Benassis, suddenly discovering that his companion wore such a pensive expression that it seemed as though his attention was wandering.
“我将用数字来说明一切，这样应该比我说的更有说服力。现在，本区已有二百英亩树林，一百六十英亩牧地。在不征收附加税的情况下，我们可以给神父一百克朗的补助，付给乡村警察二百法郎的工资，而且也付给小学男女教师各两百法郎。还可以有五百法郎的养路费，另外有五百法郎是必要时用来修理区公所、神父住宅，教堂和支付其他小笔开支。十五年后，这里种的树也可以带来十万法郎的收入，因此居民都不用自己掏钱缴税。这个区将成为法国最富有的区之一。不过，我说这么多一定让您觉得烦了吧，先生？”贝纳西说，因为他突然发现，他的朋友一脸若有所思的表情，好像有点心不在焉。

"At a certain point our prosperity came to a standstill. I wanted a post-office, and sellers of tobacco, stationery, powder and shot. The receiver of taxes had hitherto preferred to live elsewhere, but now I succeeded in persuading him to take up his abode in the town, holding out as inducements the pleasantness of the place and of the new society. As time and place permitted I had succeeded in producing a supply of everything for which I had first created a need, in attracting families of hardworking people into the district, and in implanting a desire to own land in them all.  So by degrees, as they saved a little money, the waste land began to be broken up; spade husbandry and small holdings increased; so did the value of property on the mountain.
“当到了一定程度，这种繁荣可能会中止。我想要间邮局、烟草店、文具店和香粉店。税收员原本喜欢住在乡下，但是我成功地游说他到镇上来落户，告诉他居在这里可以享受到新社会的乐趣。一旦时机和地点允许，我就能成功地进行生产，来满足我起初创造出的各种需求。我总会把勤劳的人家吸引到这里来，让他们产生垦地致富的念头。就这样，渐渐的，当他们攒了些钱后，就开始开发荒地。小规模的耕种者和土地所有者大量出现，也涌现了一批靠开发山林创造财富的人。
"Those struggling folk who, when I knew them first, used to walk over to Grenoble carrying their few cheeses for sale, now made the journey comfortably in a cart, and took fruit, eggs, chickens and turkeys, and before they were aware of it, everyone was a little richer. Even those who came off worst had a garden at any rate, and grew early vegetables and fruit. It became the children's work to watch the cattle in the fields, and at last it was found to be a waste of time to bake bread at home. Here were signs of prosperity!
“当我刚来这儿时，那些为生计奔波的人曾经步行去格勒诺布尔卖点儿干酪，而现在却能舒适地用卡车运送水果、鸡蛋、鸡和火鸡去卖。在他们意识到条件有所改善前，每一个人都比从前富裕些。现在，境况最差的人家都有片自己的园子，种着各种蔬菜和水果。现在都是让小孩在田地里放牛，而且最后发现，自己在家烤面包也是浪费时间。这些可都是繁荣的迹象啊！
"But if this place was to be a permanent forge of industry, fuel must be constantly added to the fire. The town had not as yet a renascent industry which could maintain this commercial process, an industry which should make great transactions, a warehouse, and a market necessary. It is not enough that a country should lose none of the money that forms its capital; you will not increase its prosperity by more or less ingenious devices for causing this amount to circulate, by means of production and consumption, through the greatest possible number of hands. That is not where your problem lies. When a country is fully developed and its production keeps pace with its consumption, if private wealth is to increase as well as the wealth of the community at large, there must be exchanges with other communities, which will keep a balance on the right side of the balance-sheet. This thought has let states with a limited territorial basis like Tyre, Carthage, Venice, Holland, and England, for instance, to secure the carrying trade. I cast about for some such notion as this to apply to our little world, so as to inaugurate a third commercial epoch. Our town is so much like any other, that our prosperity was scarcely visible to a passing stranger; it was only for me that it was astonishing. The folk had come together by degrees; they themselves were a part of the change, and could not judge of its effects as a whole.
“如果这个地方的企业想长久地发展下去，就必须不断地添加新燃料，注入活力。目前，这里还没有一个有活力的企业能够长期维持下去，能够完成大型交易，拥有大型货仓和必要的市场。一个地方仅仅做到不损失资金是远远不够的，没有一些别出心裁的方法保障资金流动是不可能增加财富的，得通过生产和消费活动，让资金在更多人的手里流通。这还不是问题的关键。当一个地区有了充分发展，且生产能够满足消费需求之后，个人和地区要想要增加财富，就必须与其他地区有贸易往来，这样才能实现贸易顺差。这一想法是一些领土基础有限的国家能够保障运输业的关键因素，比如推罗、迦太基、威尼斯、荷兰和英国。我把这一理念也运用到这个小地方，以此来创造第三个商业繁荣期。和其他城镇一样，过路人是感觉不到这里的繁荣的。只有我知道这里的繁荣和变化是多么惊人。居民渐渐到镇上来定居，他们本身都身处在变化之中，所以对变革的影响不能做出全局的评价。
"Seven years had gone by when I met with two strangers, the real benefactors of the place, which perhaps some day they will transform into a large town. One of them is a Tyrolese, an exceedingly clever fellow, who makes rough shoes for country people's wear, and boots for people of fashion in Grenoble as no one can make them, not even in Paris itself. He was a poor strolling musician, who, singing and working, had made his way through Italy; one of those busy Germans who fashion the tools of their own work, and make the instrument that they play upon. When he came to the town he asked if any one wanted a pair of shoes. They sent him to me, and I gave him an order for two pairs of boots, for which he made his own lasts. The foreigner's skill surprised me. He gave accurate and consistent answers to the questions I put, and his face and manner confirmed the good opinion I had formed of him. I suggested that he should settle in the place, undertaking to assist him in business in every way that I could; in fact, I put a fairly large sum of money at his disposal. He accepted my offer. I had my own ideas in this. The quality of our leather had improved; and why should we not use it ourselves, and before very long make our own shoes at moderate prices?
“七年前，我遇到了两个陌生人，他们是我们这个地方真正的福音。也许有一天，他们将把这里变成一个大城市。其中一个是蒂罗尔人，相当聪明。他会做乡下人穿的鞋子，也会做格勒诺布尔的时髦人穿的靴子。那手艺无人能比，就是在巴黎也找不到有这样好手艺的人。他是位可怜的流浪音乐家，边做鞋边卖唱，一路经过意大利来到了这里。他是德国人，既会制作自己用的工具，还会制作乐器。刚来镇上时，他到处问有没有人买鞋。别人让他来找我，我订购了两双靴子，样式由他来设计。他的手艺让我很吃惊。对于我提出的问题，他都回答得既准确又有条理，而且他的表情和态度让我更加肯定之前对他的好印象没错。我建议他在这里安家，而且承诺会尽力在生意上给他提供帮助。事实上，我也确实借了一大笔钱给他。他接受了我的帮助。对于这个生意，我有自己的想法。既然我们的皮革质量提高了，那么为什么不能由我们自己来使用这些皮革，不久之后就能为我们自己做些便宜的皮鞋呢？
"It was the basket-maker's business over again on a larger scale. Chance had put an exceedingly clever hard-working man in my way, and he must be retained so that a steady and profitable trade might be given to the place. There is a constant demand for foot-gear, and a very slight difference in price is felt at once by the purchaser.
“这一次比编筐生意做得更大。上天把这样一个勤劳而格外聪明的人带到我这里。只要留住他，就可能给这里开发了一个稳定又赚钱的行业。人们对鞋子的需求是持续不断的，消费者对价格上的一点点差异都很敏感。
"This was my reasoning, sir, and fortunately events have justified it. At this time we have five tanyards, each of which has its bark-mill. They take all the hides produced in the department itself, and even draw part of their supply from Provence; and yet the Tyrolese uses more leather than they can produce, and has forty work-people in his employ!
“这是我的推理，先生。幸运的是，最终结果证实了我的推理是正确的。现在我们有五个鞋坊，每个鞋坊都配有一个制皮坊。全省的皮革都用上了，有时还需要去其他省购买。可是蒂罗尔已经请了四十个工人，生产的皮革已经不够用了。
"I happened on the other man after a fashion no whit less strange, but you might find the story tedious. He is just an ordinary peasant, who discovered a cheaper way of making the great broad-brimmed hats that are worn in this part of the world. He sells them in other cantons, and even sends them into Switzerland and Savoy. The prefect, amazed at our industrial progress, lent his aid in obtaining the royal ordinance which authorized them, and last year we held our three fairs. They are known as far as Savoy as the Shoe Fair and the Hat Fair.
“我遇到的另一个人也挺不可思议的，但您可能觉得这个故事没什么意思。他是个普通的农民，他发现了一种方法，能够用低廉的价格制作当地人戴的大宽沿帽。他还把帽子拿到其他地区去卖，甚至卖到了瑞士和萨瓦地区。由于他的帽子物美价廉，所以给区里创造了源源不断的财富。这让我产生了每年在这里举办三次展销会的想法。鉴于我们在做企业方面取得了优异的成绩，省长帮我们取得了国王批准举办展销会的指令。去年我们举办了三次展销会。主要是鞋帽展销会，名声已远扬至萨瓦地区。
"The head clerk of a notary in Grenoble heard of these changes. He was poor, but he was a well-educated, hardworking young fellow, and Mlle. Gravier was engaged to be married to him. He went to Paris to ask for an authorization to establish himself here as a notary, and his request was granted. As he had not to pay for his appointment, he could afford to build a house in the market square of the new town, opposite the house of the justice of the peace. "Next year a druggist surely ought to come among us, and next we want a clockmaker, a furniture dealer, and a bookseller; and so, by degrees, we shall have all the desirable luxuries of life. Who knows but that at last we shall have a number of substantial houses, and give ourselves all the airs of a small city? Education has made such strides that there has never been any opposition made at the council-board when I proposed that we should restore our church and build a parsonage; nor when I brought forward a plan for laying out a fine open space, planted with trees, where the fairs could be held, and a further scheme for a survey of the township, so that its future streets should be wholesome, spacious, and wisely planned.
“明年这里应该会来个药剂师，接下来还需要一名钟表师、一个家具商和一个书商。可以说，渐渐地，在这里，人们生活中的各种需求都能得到满足。说不定最后这里还会有许多豪宅，甚至有小城市的样子，谁知道呢？对这里的教化取得了巨大的进步，因此在我推出一些举措时并没有遭到市政府议会的任何反对。我提议要修葺教堂，给神父建一所住宅；提出建一个露天广场，用来开展览会，还要种上树；我还提出对小镇进行规划，让以后的街道更干净、宽敞，布局更为合理。
"This is how we came to have nineteen hundred hearths in the place of a hundred and thirty-seven; three thousand head of cattle instead of eight hundred; and for a population of seven hundred, no less than two thousand persons are living in the township, or three thousand, if the people down the valley are included. There are twelve houses belonging to wealthy people in the Commune, there are a hundred well-to-do families, and two hundred more which are thriving. The rest have their own exertions to look to. Every one knows how to read and write, and we subscribe to seventeen different newspapers.
“就是这样，这里的住户由一百三十七户增至一千九百户，牲口从八百增至三千，镇上的人口由七百增到两千多。如果算上山谷里的居民，那门人口就达到三千了。这个区已经有十二户富人、一百户较殷实的人家和二百多户兴旺的家庭。其他人也都在努力致富。这里的每个人都会读会写，我们还订了十七种不同的报纸。
"We have poor people still among us—there are far too many of them, in fact; but we have no beggars, and there is work enough for all. I have so many patients that my daily round taxes the powers of two horses. I can go anywhere for five miles round at any hour without fear; for if any one was minded to fire a shot at me, his life would not be worth ten minutes' purchase. The law is uniform, but not so the land and the minds and customs of those who dwell in it; and the administration of the law is the art of carrying it out in such a manner that no injury is done to people's interests. Every place must be considered separately.
“后来，我又把目光转向了另一种生产，以此来增加这些穷人的福利，而不仅仅是财富。我不会从外界带进来任何这个地区没有的东西。我仅仅只是鼓励这些人突破局限性，从而为自己的产品打开市场，还鼓励他们探索一种能够让他们自己感觉很快就能繁荣的方法。这些人也许都没意识到自己成了我的拥护者，在工作中宣扬了我的理论方针。于是，我又有了别的想法。我们距离格勒诺布尔只有五里格远。而大城市对各种产品的需求量都很大，这种得天独厚的地理优势并不是每个区都有的。在开展每项类似的工作之前，必须把地区特点、所处的环境和当地资源情况都考虑在内，当然还得充分考察当地的风土人情、土地情况及其他各种因素。我们不可能指望在诺曼底地区种植葡萄。管理工作比其他任何事情都变化多样，也没有什么普遍原则可循。法律可以制定一个统一标准，但居民所拥有的土地、思想及风俗却不尽相同。因此，行政管理的艺术在于执行了法律，同时又不损害任何人的利益。所以，对于不同地区，我们必须区别对待。
"On the other side of the mountain at the foot of which our deserted village lies, they find it impossible to use wheeled plows, because the soil is not deep enough. Now if the mayor of the commune were to take it into his head to follow in our footsteps, he would be the ruin of his neighborhood. I advised him to plant vineyards; they had a capital vintage last year in the little district, and their wine is exchanged for our corn.
“山那边废弃的村庄，位于山脚下，由于土壤厚度不够，所以不能使用带轮子的犁。如果那个地区的区长效仿我们的做法，无疑只会给居民带来灾难。我建议他开辟葡萄园。这个小地区在去年就获得了葡萄大丰收，他们用葡萄酒换我们的粮食。
"Then, lastly, it must be remembered that my words carried a certain weight with the people to whom I preached, and that we were continually brought into close contact. I cured my peasants' complaints; an easy task, for a nourishing diet is, as a rule, all that is needed to restore them to health and strength. Either through thrift, or through sheer poverty, the country people starve themselves; any illness among them is caused in this way, and as a rule they enjoy very fair health.
“总的来说，我的话在那些被教化的人心中是有一定分量的，我们也一直保持着紧密联系。我把很多农民的病都治好了。给他们治病并不难，关键就是让他们吃有营养的食物来增强体质，恢复健康。因为过于节俭或贫困，乡下人吃都吃不饱。他们的病也都是这个原因造成的，其实他们本身的身体状况通常是很不错的。
"When I first decided to devote myself to this life of obscure renunciation, I was in doubt for a long while whether to become a cure, a country doctor, or a justice of the peace. It is not without reason that people speak collectively of the priest, the lawyer, and the doctor as 'men of the black robe’—so the saying goes. They represent the three principal elements necessary to the existence of society—conscience, property, and health. At one time the first, and at a later period the second, was all-important in the State. Our predecessors on this earth thought, perhaps not without reason, that the priest, who prescribed what men should think, ought to be paramount; so the priest was king, pontiff, and judge in one, for in those days belief and faith were everything. All this has been changed in our day; and we must even take our epoch as we find it. But I, for one, believe that the progress of civilization and the welfare of the people depend on these three men. They are the three powers who bring home to the people's minds the ways in which facts, interests, and principles affect them. They themselves are three great results produced in the midst of the nation by the operation of events, by the ownership of property, and by the growth of ideas. Time goes on and brings changes to pass, property increases or diminishes in men's hands, all the various readjustments have to be duly regulated, and in this way principles of social order are established. If civilization is to spread itself, and production is to be increased, the people must be made to understand the way in which the interests of the individual harmonize with national interests which resolve themselves into facts, interests, and principles. As these three professions are bound to deal with these issues of human life, it seemed to me that they must be the most powerful civilizing agencies of our time. They alone afford to a man of wealth the opportunity of mitigating the fate of the poor, with whom they daily bring him in contact.
“当初，在我下定决心要默默地克制己欲、奉献出自己一生的时候，我曾犹豫了许久，不知道到底是做一名神父、乡村医生还是治安法官。人们往往把神父、法律工作者和医生归为‘穿黑袍’的一类人，这样做是有道理的——而且大家也都是这么认为的。这类人代表了社会存在的三个必要的关键因素——良心、财产和健康。开始是神父，后来是法律工作者，他们相继在国家中占据极其重要的地位。我们的前辈们这样认为也不是没有道理，他们认为指导人们应该如何思考的神父是至高无上的，他是国王、教皇和法官的集合体，因为在那个年代信仰就是一切。然而，当今的时代已经变了，我们只能欣然接受它现在的样子。但是，我仍然相信一个社会文明程度的提高和人民的福利要靠这三种人。这三股力量让人们认识到事实、利益和原则是如何影响他们的。他们自己本身也是国家在处理重大事件、聚集财富和不断发展思想这三种重要过程中的产物。时间流逝带来了变化，人们手中的财富变多了或减少了。根据这些变化，社会的其他很多方面都需要重新调整，于是维持社会秩序的准则就产生了。文明要发展，生产力要提高，人民就必须懂得个人利益和国家利益怎样才能统一起来，而国家利益正是靠实实在在的业绩、利益和原则来保障的。因为这三种职业就是处理人类生活中的这些问题的，所以我认为它们是我们这个时代最强有力的文明机构。只有它们才能给富人提供机会，让其去帮助穷人改变命运，是它们使富人在日常生活中和这些穷人打交道。
"The peasant is always more willing to listen to the man who lays down rules for saving him from bodily ills than to the priest who exhorts him to save his soul. The first speaker can talk of this earth, the scene of the peasant's labors, while the priest is bound to talk to him of heaven, with which, unfortunately, the peasant nowadays concerns himself very little indeed; I say unfortunately, because the doctrine of a future life is not only a consolation, but a means by which men may be governed. Is not religion the one power that sanctions social laws? We have but lately vindicated the existence of God. In the absence of a religion, the Government was driven to invent the Terror, in order to carry its laws into effect; but the terror was the fear of man, and it has passed away.
“相对于劝诫人们去拯救灵魂的神父而言，农民总是更愿意听取给他们医治身体疾病的医生提出的建议。医生可以跟他们谈论他们自己耕作的土地，而神父注定只能和他们谈论天堂。而不幸的是，农民现在很少关心天堂的事。我之所以说‘不幸’，因为有关未来生活的教义不仅是一种宽慰，而且还是一种统治的手段。难道宗教不是唯一有权规定社会准则的力量吗？最近，我们论证了上帝的存在是合理的问题。如果没有宗教，政府就不得不制造恐怖，为的是让制定的法律生效。但这只是人世间的恐怖，而且也已经过去了。
"When a peasant is ill, when he is forced to lie on his pallet, and while he is recovering, he cannot help himself, he is forced to listen to logical reasoning, which he can understand quite well if it is put clearly before him. This thought made a doctor of me. My calculations for the peasants were made along with them. I never gave advice unless I was quite sure of the results, and in this way compelled them to admit the wisdom of my views. The people require infallibility. Infallibility was the making of Napoleon; he would have been a god if he had not filled the world with the sound of his fall at Waterloo. If Mahomet founded a permanent religion after conquering the third part of the globe, it was by dint of concealing his deathbed from the crowd. The same rules hold good for the great conqueror and for the provincial mayor, and a nation or a commune is much the same sort of crowd; indeed, the great multitude of mankind is the same everywhere.
“当农民病了，不得不躺在简陋的小床上，而他在恢复过程中，又无能为力的时候，他就不得不听从别人的逻辑推理。只要我们讲得很清楚，他们就能够理解。正是这种想法让我决定做名医生。我和农民们一起想法子。只有当我对结果很有把握时，我才会给他们提供建议，因此他们都认为我的看法总是很明智的。人们需要的是绝对可靠的东西。而正是万无一失造就了拿破仑，要不是滑铁卢那次震惊世界的惨败，他也许会被当作神来崇拜。如果说穆罕默德在征服了三分之一的世界后，创造了一种长久的宗教信仰，那是因为他没有让世人看到他临死之时的样子。对于伟大的征服者和地方的管理人员而言，这些规则同样适用。一个国家或一个地区是同样性质的群体，确实，不管是哪里的民众都是一样的。
"I have been exceedingly firm with those whom I have helped with money; if I had not been inflexible on this point, they all would have laughed at me. Peasants, no less than worldlings, end by despising the man that they can deceive. He has been cheated? Clearly, then, he must have been weak; and it is might alone that governs the world. I have never charged a penny for my professional advice, except to those who were evidently rich people; but I have not allowed the value of my services to be overlooked at all, and I always make them pay for medicine unless the patient is exceedingly poor. If my peasants do not pay me in money, they are quite aware that they are in my debt; sometimes they satisfy their consciences by bringing oats for my horses, or corn, when it is cheap. But if the miller were to send me some eels as a return for my advice, I should tell him that he is too generous for such a small matter. My politeness bears fruit. In the winter I shall have some sacks of flour for the poor. Ah! sir, they have kind hearts, these people, if one does not slight them, and to-day I think more good and less evil of them than I did formerly.” 
“我对那些我曾资助的人态度非常坚决。如果我不坚持这一原则的话，他们可能就不会敬重我。同其他凡人一样，农民最终也会瞧不起那些被他们欺骗的人。他被人欺骗了吗？明显，说明这个人软弱无能。只有靠力量才能统治这个世界。我给人看病向来都分文不收，当然，特别有钱的人除外。但是，我也不会让别人看轻了我的劳动，所以除了特别穷的病人之外，其他病人是要付给我药费的。如果农民没有付钱，他们会意识到欠我一个人情。为了心安，他们有时会给我送来喂马的燕麦，或者小麦便宜时也会送来小麦。但如果磨坊工人送些鳗鱼给我当作酬劳，我会跟他讲为了一点儿小事，他太客气了。我的礼貌是有回报的。冬天，我会从他那儿得到几袋面粉，用来救济穷人。啊！先生，如果别人不看轻这些人，他们还是很善良的。比起以前，现在我把他们想得更好一些。”
"What a deal of trouble you have taken!" said Genestas.
“您可真是费了不少心啊！”热奈斯塔说。
"Not at all," answered Benassis. "It was no more trouble to say something useful than to chatter about trifles; and whether I chatted or joked, the talk always turned on them and their concerns wherever I went. They would not listen to me at first. I had to overcome their dislikes; I belonged to the middle classes—that is to say, I was a natural enemy. I found the struggle amusing. An easy or an uneasy conscience—that is all the difference that lies between doing well or ill; the trouble is the same in either case. If scoundrels would but behave themselves properly, they might be millionaires instead of being hanged. That is all.” 
“一点儿都不费心。”贝纳西说，“其实，给他们讲些有用的话并不比拉家常费劲。不管是闲聊还是开玩笑，我都会跟他们谈谈他们所关心的事。一开始他们也不听我的。我必须克服他们对我的抵触情绪。我属于中产阶级——也就是说，我是他们的天敌。我觉得这场较量挺有意义的。问心无愧与问心有愧——这便是行善与作恶的唯一区别，其中所经历的麻烦都是一样的。如果恶棍愿意改邪归正，他们也可以成为百万富翁，而不会被处以绞刑。道理就是这样的。”
"The dinner is growing cold, sir!" cried Jacquotte, in the doorway.
“晚餐都快放凉了，先生！”雅科特站在门口大声喊。
Genestas caught the doctor's arm.
热奈斯塔拉住医生的胳膊。
"I have only one comment to offer on what I have just heard," he remarked. "I am not acquainted with any account of the wars of Mahomet, so that I can form no opinions as to his military talents; but if you had only watched the Emperor's tactics during the campaign in France, you might well have taken him for a god; and if he was beaten on the field of Waterloo, it was because he was more than mortal, it was because the earth found his weight too heavy to bear, and sprang from under his feet! On every other subject I entirely agree with you, and tonnerre de Dieu! whoever hatched you did a good day's work.” 
“对于刚才您所说的那些，我只有一点看法。”热奈斯塔说。“我不了解穆罕默德的战争经历，所以关于他的军事才能也发表不了什么看法。但如果您看过拿破仑大帝在法国战争中所运用的战术，您可能就会把他当神看了。他在滑铁卢战场上之所以失败，是因为他极为不寻常，他的力量太重以至于地球都难以承受，所以才在他脚下爆发了！对于其他问题，我绝对认同您的看法，亲爱的先生！今天可真是让您家的管家忙坏了吧！”
"Come," exclaimed Benassis with a smile, "let us sit down to dinner." 
“快过来，坐下吃饭吧！”贝纳西大声说，面带微笑。
The walls of the dining-room were paneled from floor to ceiling, and painted gray. The furniture consisted of a few straw-bottomed chairs, a sideboard, some cupboards, a stove, and the late owner's celebrated clock; there were white curtains in the window, and a white cloth on the table, about which there was no sign of luxury. The dinner service was of plain white earthenware; the soup, made after the traditions of the late cure, was the most concentrated kind of broth that was ever set to simmer by any mortal cook. The doctor and his guest had scarcely finished it when a man rushed into the kitchen, and in spite of Jacquotte, suddenly invaded the dining-room.
饭厅的墙壁，从地板到天花板，都镶了木板，并刷成了灰色。餐厅里有几把铺了草垫的椅子、一个碗柜、几个柜子、一只炉子和已故主人那架有名的钟。窗帘是白色的，餐桌布也是白色的，完全没有奢华的迹象。吃饭用的碗碟全是朴素的白粘土制成的。根据已故主人的习惯，第一道菜是浓汤，没有谁会像这位女厨一样把汤煮得这么浓。医生和客人刚把汤喝完，突然一个男子不顾雅科特的阻挠冲进了厨房，径直来到了饭厅。
"Well, what is it?" asked the doctor.
“哎，你有什么事吗？”医生问道。
"It is this, sir. The mistress, our Mme. Vigneau, has turned as white as white can be, so that we are frightened about her." 
“先生，事情是这样的。我家女主人维尼奥太太，脸色突然变得很惨白，我们都被她吓着了。”
"Oh, well, then," Benassis said cheerfully, "I must leave the table," and he rose to go.
“哦，那好吧，看来我得去看一下。”贝纳西很爽快地回答，随即起身准备离开。
In spite of the doctor's entreaties, Genestas flung down his table-napkin, and swore in a soldierly fashion that he would not finish his dinner without his host. He returned indeed to the salon; and as he warmed himself by the fire, he thought over the troubles that no man may escape, the troubles that are found in every lot that it falls to man to endure here upon earth.
尽管医生再三请求热奈斯塔不要离席，后者还是放下餐巾离开了饭桌。他以军人的方式发誓，如果主人离开了，他不可能一个人吃下去。他回到客厅，一边烤火，一边想着在这世上，每个人都会遇到麻烦，没人能逃得掉，而我们只能去忍受。
Benassis soon came back, and the two future friends sat down again.
贝纳西很快就回来了，于是两位未来的朋友又重新回到餐桌旁。
"Taboureau has just come up to speak to you," said Jacquotte to her master, as she brought in the dishes that she had kept hot for them.
“塔布罗刚才来过，说有话跟您说。”雅科特端来一直在加热的菜时，对主人说。
"Who can be ill at his place?" asked the doctor.
“他家里谁病了呢？”医生问。
"No one is ill, sir. I think from what he said that it is some matter of his own that he wants to ask you about; he is coming back again." 
“先生，他家没有人生病。听他说话的意思，好像是想谈谈他自己的事，想找你出出主意。他还会再来的。”
"Very good. This Taboureau," Benassis went on, addressing Genestas, "is for me a whole philosophical treatise; take a good look at him when he comes, he is sure to amuse you. He was a laborer, a thrifty, hard-working man, eating little and getting through a good deal of work. As soon as the rogue came to have a few crowns of his own, his intelligence began to develop; he watched the progress which I had originated in this little district with an eye to his own profit. He had made quite a fortune in eight year's time, that is to say, a fortune for our part of the world. Very likely he may have a couple of score thousand francs by now. But if I were to give you a thousand guesses, you would never find out how he made the money. He is a usurer, and his scheme of usury is so profoundly and so cleverly based upon the requirements of the whole canton, that I should merely waste my time if I were to take it upon myself to undeceive them as to the benefits which they reap, in their own opinion, from their dealings with Taboureau. When this devil of a fellow saw every one cultivating his own plot of ground, he hurried about buying grain so as to supply the poor with the requisite seed. Here, as everywhere else, the peasants and even some of the farmers had no ready money with which to pay for seed. To some, Master Taboureau would lend a sack of barley, for which he was to receive a sack of rye at harvest time, and to others a measure of wheat for a sack of four. At the present day the man has extended this curious business of his all over the department; and unless something happens to prevent him, he will go on and very likely make a million. Well, my dear sir, Taboureau the laborer, an obliging, hard-working, good-natured fellow, used to lend a helping hand to any one who asked him; but as his gains have increased Monsieur Taboureau has become litigious, arrogant, and somewhat given to sharp practice. The more money he makes, the worse he grows. The moment that the peasant forsakes his life of toil pure and simple for the leisured existence of the landowning classes, he becomes intolerable. There is a certain kind of character, partly virtuous, partly vicious, half-educated, half-ignorant, which will always be the despair of governments. You will see an example of it in Taboureau. He looks simple, and even doltish; but when his interests are in question, he is certainly profoundly clever.” 
“好的。贝纳西接着对热奈斯塔说，“对我而言，这个塔布罗简直就是一部哲学论著。等会儿他来的时候您仔细看看他，您肯定会觉得很好笑的。他原来是个做工的，生活节俭，工作卖力，做很多事，但吃得少。这个家伙自己有了几个克朗后，脑袋就变灵光了。他看到我在这个小地方取得了一些成绩，于是就开始学我，想办法赚钱。他在八年时间里大赚了一笔，也就是说，他在这个小地方的确算是发财了。他现在身价可能有四万法郎。不过，即便我给您一千次机会让您猜，您也不会想到他是如何发家致富的。他是放高利贷的，根据本区人们的需求，他制定了极为周密的放贷计划，而且放贷手段极其巧妙。所以，如果我跟那些和塔布罗打交道的人说，他们实际上都吃亏上当了，那完全就是白费力气。这个可恶的家伙看到大家都开始种地，于是赶忙买了些谷物回来，提供给那些需要买种子的穷人。跟其他地方一样，这里的农民甚至是一些佃农，有时都会没钱买种子。对于这些农民，塔布罗老板会借给他们一袋大麦，到了收获季节就从他们那儿收回一袋黑麦；有时会借出一袋小麦，收回一袋面粉。现在，他已经把这精明的生意扩大到了全省范围。不出意外的话，他会一直做下去，很可能挣上一百万。可是，亲爱的先生，塔布罗打工者曾经乐于助人，干活卖力，性格也很好，还总是帮那些向他求助的人。可是，有了钱之后，这位塔布罗先生就变得爱打官司、傲慢，而且总喜欢和人吵架。钱赚得越多，这个人就变得越坏。当一个农民放弃了简单淳朴的劳动生活，过上了有产阶级的安逸生活之后，他就会变得不可理喻了。世上有一类人，他们不邪恶，却也不高尚；他们不无知，却称不上有文化，这类人往往让政府束手无策。塔布罗就是这种人。他看上去似乎很单纯，甚至有些傻乎乎的，但一旦涉及利益问题，他就显出无比的精明和老练。”
A heavy footstep announced the approach of the grain lender.
听到一阵沉重的脚步声，便知道这位谷种放贷人来了。
"Come in, Taboureau!" cried Benassis.
“快请进吧，塔布罗。”贝纳西大声喊道。
Thus forewarned by the doctor, the commandant scrutinized the peasant in the doorway. Taboureau was decidedly thin, and stooped a little. He had a bulging forehead, covered with wrinkles, and a cavernous face, in which two small gray eyes with a dark spot in either of them seemed to be pierced rather than set. The lines of the miser's mouth were close and firm, and his narrow chin turned up to meet an exaggeratedly hooked nose. His hair was turning gray already, and deep furrows which converged above the prominent cheek-bones spoke of the wily shrewdness of a horse-dealer and of a life spent in journeying about. He wore a blue coat in fairly clean condition, the square side-pocket flaps stuck out above his hips, and the skirts of the coats hung loose in front, so that a white-flowered waistcoat was visible. There he stood firmly planted on both feet, leaning upon a thick stick with a knob at the end of it. A little spaniel had followed the grain-dealer, in spite of Jacquotte's efforts, and was crouching beside him.
由于之前听了医生的提醒，指挥官仔细观察起站在门口的这位农民。塔布罗很瘦，有一点驼背。他的前额向前突，额头上布满了皱纹。陷下去的脸上长着双灰色的小眼睛，眼窝深陷，就像是在脸上扎的两个洞。这位守财奴长着张瘪嘴，尖下巴高高翘起，都快碰到那长得很夸张的鹰钩鼻子了。他的头发已经变成灰白色，突出的颧骨上深深的皱纹显示出马商般的精明和奔波生活中的艰辛。他穿着件很干净的蓝色外套，两侧的方形口袋在腰间鼓起，外套敞着，可以看到里面的白花马甲。他稳稳地站着，还拄着一根底端带球形柄的拐杖。一只小长耳狗紧跟在这个谷种贩子后面，尽管雅科特想赶狗出去，但最后它还是趴在主人身边。
"Well, what is it?" Benassis asked as he turned to this being.
“你找我有什么事吗？”贝纳西转身问塔布罗。
Taboureau gave a suspicious glance at the stranger seated at the doctor's table, and said: 
塔布罗疑惑地看了看坐在饭桌旁的陌生人，说：
"It is not a case of illness, M. le Maire, but you understand how to doctor the ailments of the purse just as well as those of the body. We have had a little difficulty with a man over at Saint-Laurent, and I have come to ask your advice about it.” 
“我来这儿不是为了看病，区长先生，我知道您 不仅治病，而且也很善于处理钱这方面的麻烦事。因为我们和圣洛朗的一个人发生了点儿生意上的纠纷，所以过来向您求教。”
"Why not see the justice of the peace or his clerk?" 
“为什么不去找治安法官或他的文书呢？”
"Oh, because you are so much cleverer, sir, and I shall feel more sure about my case if I can have your countenance." 
“哦，因为我觉得您比他们更高明。如果能得到您的指点，我心里就更有底了。”
"My good Taboureau, I am willing to give medical advice to the poor without charging for it; but I cannot look into the lawsuits of a man who is as wealthy as you are for nothing. It costs a good deal to acquire that kind of knowledge." 
“我亲爱的塔布罗，我愿意无偿为穷人出诊，但对于你这样一个有钱人，我可不能免费给你打官司、出谋划策。获得这方面的知识可是要花大价钱的。”
Taboureau began to twist his hat about.
塔布罗开始转动手上的帽子。
"If you want my advice, in order to save the hard coin you would have to pay to the lawyer folk over in Grenoble, you must send a bag of rye to the widow Martin, the woman who is bringing up the charity children." 
“如果我给你提建议，你就省了一笔在格勒诺布尔请律师的开支，所以你得送一袋黑麦给马丁大嫂，就是那位从孤儿院领养孤儿的寡妇。”
"Dame! I will do it with all my heart, sir, if you think it necessary. Can I talk about this business of mine without troubling the gentleman there?" he added, with a look at Genestas.
“没问题！先生，只要您觉得有必要，我一定照办。如果不影响这位先生的话，我可以开始谈谈我的事情了吗？”他看了看热奈斯塔，补充道。
The doctor nodded, so Taboureau went on.
看到医生点了点头，塔布罗就接着说下去了。
"Well, then, sir, two months ago a man from Saint-Laurent came over here to find me. 'Taboureau,' said he to me, 'could you sell me a hundred and thirty-seven measures of barley?' 'Why not?' say I, 'that is my trade. Do you want it immediately?'  'No,' he says, 'I want it for the beginning of spring, in March.' So far, so good. Well, we drive our bargain, and we drink a glass, and we agree that he is to pay me the price that the barley fetched at Grenoble last market day, and I am to deliver it in March. I am to warehouse it at owner's risk, and no allowance for shrinkage of course. But barley goes up and up, my dear sir; the barley rises like boiling milk. Then I am hard up for money, and I sell my barley. Quite natural, sir, was it not?” 
“先生，事情是这样的，两个月前有个来自圣洛朗的人找到我。‘塔布罗，’他对我说，‘你能卖一百三十七塞蒂埃大麦给我吗？’我跟他说：‘怎么会不卖呢？我做的就是这种生意！你马上就要货吗？’他回答说：‘没那么急要，大概三月份开春的时候要。’就这样，我们说定了。接下来我们讨价还价，还一起喝酒，最后我们谈妥了，他按格勒诺布尔最近一次集市日上的大麦价付钱，到三月份时再交货。交货前还是放在我仓库里保管，不过风险由他承担，当然损耗也算他的。可是，亲爱的先生，大麦的价格一直在涨，就像煮沸的牛奶一样。后来因为急着用钱，我就把大麦卖掉了。先生，这是很自然的事情啊，不是吗？”
"No," said Benassis, "the barley had passed out of your possession, you were only warehousing it. And suppose the barley had gone down in value, would you not have compelled your buyer to take it at the price you agreed upon?" 
“当然不是，”贝纳西说，“大麦已经不是你的，你只是负责保管。假如大麦价格降了，你不是还会让买家以当初定的价格付款吗？”
"But very likely he would not have paid me, sir. One must look out for oneself! The seller ought to make a good profit when the chance comes in his way; and, after, all the goods are not yours until you have paid for them. That is so, Monsieur l'Officier, is it not? For you can see that the gentleman has been in the army.” 
“但很可能这个人不会付，先生。每个人都是为自己考虑的！要是有机会，生意人就应该想办法赚钱。只有付了款，东西才是你的。事情就应该是这样呀，是吗？军官先生？看得出来，这位先生在部队里呆过。”
"Taboureau," Benassis said sternly, "ill luck will come to you. Sooner or later God punishes ill deeds. How can you, knowing as much as you do, a capable man moreover, and a man who conducts his business honorably, set examples of dishonesty to the canton? If you allow such proceedings as this to be taken against you, how can you expect that the poor will remain honest people and will not rob you? Your laborers will cheat you out of part of their working hours, and every one here will be demoralized. You are in the wrong. Your barley was as good as delivered. If the man from Saint-Laurent had fetched it himself, you would not have gone there to take it away from him; you have sold something that was no longer yours to sell, for your barley had already been turned into money which was to be paid down at the stipulated time. But go on.” 
“塔布罗，”贝纳西严厉地说，“你会倒霉的。上帝迟早都会惩罚做坏事的人。你本是个见过世面的人，能力出众，在生意场上也有良好的口碑，怎么可以在这里给大家树立不诚信的榜样呢？如果你因此而吃了官司，你还能指望这里的穷人保持诚信而不抢你的吗？你的工人们将会在工作时间偷懒，这里的每个人也都会变得不遵守道德规范。错全在你。你的大麦本来就已经卖出去了。如果当时圣洛朗的那个人把大麦运走了，你是不可能从他那儿再拿回来的。你是把不属于你的东西给卖了，那个人会在商定的时间付款，也就是说你已经用大麦换了钱。你接着说。”
Genestas gave the doctor a significant glance, to call his attention to Taboureau's impassive countenance. Not a muscle had stirred in the usurer's face during this reprimand; there was no flush on his forehead, and no sign of emotion in his little eyes.
热奈斯塔给医生递了个眼色，让他看看塔布罗面无表情的样子。医生在说这番责备话的时候，这位高利贷者脸上任何反应都没有。他没有脸红，小眼睛里也没流露出什么情感。
"Well, sir, I am called upon to supply the barley at last winter's price. Now I consider that I am not bound to do so.” 
“先生，照您的意思，我该以去年冬天的价格卖给他大麦。可是现在我不想这么做。”
"Look here, Taboureau, deliver that barley and be very quick about it, or make up your mind to be respected by nobody in the future. Even if you gained the day in a case like this, you would be looked upon as an unscrupulous man who does not keep to his word, and is not bound by promises, or by honor, or—” 
“你听清楚了，塔布罗，赶紧把麦子给人家，否则以后不会再有人敬重你了。即便你打赢了官司，大家以后也会认为你是个不道德的人，是个既没信用，也不要尊严的人，而且——”
"Go on, there is nothing to be afraid of; tell me that I am a scamp, a scoundrel, a thief outright. You can say things like that in business without insulting anybody, M. le Maire. Tis each for himself in business, you know." 
“您接着说下去，不用顾忌什么，直接说我是个小人、恶棍或强盗都行。在生意场上，这种话不会伤人的，区长先生，生意人向来都是无利不起早的，你知道的。”
"Well, then, why deliberately put yourself in a position in which you deserve to be called by such names?" 
“可是，你为什么故意给自己这样一个定位，让自己背上这些骂名呢？”
"But if the law is on my side, sir?" 
“但是也有可能，法律对我有利呢，先生？”
"But the law will certainly not be on your side." 
“法律绝对不会站在你那边的。”
"Are you quite sure about it, sir? Certain sure? For you see it is an important matter." 
“你就这么确定吗，先生？有十足把握吗？这可不是件小事。”
"Certainly I am. Quite sure. If I were not at dinner, I would have down the code, and you should see for yourself. If the case comes on, you will lose it, and you will never set foot in my house again, for I do not wish to receive people whom I do not respect. Do you understand? You will lose your case." 
“当然，我非常确信。要不是因为在吃饭，我会把法典拿给你，让你自己看看。只要打官司，你必输无疑，那么以后你也不要再来我家了。我可不愿意接待我瞧不起的那些人。你听明白了吗？这场官司你肯定输。”
"Oh! no, not at all, I shall not lose it, sir," said Taboureau. "You see, sir, it is this way; it is the man from Saint-Laurent who owes me the barley; I bought it of him, and now he refuses to deliver it. I just wanted to make quite certain that I should gain my case before going to any expense at court about it.” 
“哦！不，不可能，我不会输的，先生。”塔布罗说，“先生您看，实际上，事情是这样的，是来自圣洛朗的那个人欠我的大麦。我从他那儿买了大麦，但他现在拒绝交付。我只是想在交诉讼费之前，确定一下我有把握打赢这场官司。”
Genestas and the doctor exchanged glances; each concealed his amazement at the ingenious device by which the man had sought to learn the truth about this point of law.
热奈斯塔和医生相互看了一眼，两人都没料到，塔布罗竟然精心设计了这样的方法来了解法律对于此事的处理方案。不过他们都没表现出这种惊讶。
"Very well, Taboureau, your man is a swindler; you should not make bargains with such people." 
“那就好办，塔布罗，看来是那个人不守信用，其实你不应该和这种人做生意。”
"Ah! sir, they understand business, those people do." 
”啊！先生，他们对做生意很精通，的确是很老练。”
"Good-bye, Taboureau.” 
“再见啊，塔布罗。”
"Your servant, gentlemen." 
“区长先生，军官大人，再见了啊！”
"Well, now," remarked Benassis, when the usurer had gone, "if that fellow were in Paris, do you not think that he would be a millionaire before very long?" 
高利贷者走后，贝纳西问军人：“依你所见，如果这个人在巴黎，你难道不认为他很快就能变成百万富翁吗？”
After dinner, the doctor and his visitor went back to the salon, and all the rest of the evening until bedtime they talked about war and politics; Genestas evincing a most violent dislike of the English in the course of conversation.
晚饭后，医生和这位拜访者回到了客厅。回房睡觉之前，他们一直在谈论战争、政治。谈话中热奈斯塔表现出对英国人极度讨厌的情绪。
"May I know whom I have the honor of entertaining as a guest?" asked the doctor.
“不知是否有幸知道这位客人的大名？”医生问道。
"My name is Pierre Bluteau," answered Genestas; "I am a captain stationed at Grenoble." 
“我叫皮埃尔·布吕托，”热奈斯塔回答，“格勒诺布尔的一名骑兵上尉。”
"Very well, sir. Do you care to adopt M. Gravier's plan? In the morning after breakfast he liked to go on my rounds with me. I am not at all sure that you would find anything to interest you in the things that occupy me—they are so very commonplace. For, after all, you own no land about here, nor are you the mayor of the place, and you will see nothing in the canton that you cannot see elsewhere; one thatched cottage is just like another. Still you will be in the open air, and you will have something to take you out of doors.” 
“哦，是这样啊，先生。您愿意像格拉维耶先生那样做吗？吃过早饭后，他都乐意跟着我到处跑一跑。我也不知道你对我做的事感不感兴趣——因为都是些很平常的事。毕竟您在这里没有地，也不是区长，而且在这个区，您也看不到其他区没有的东西。一个个茅屋都是一样的。不过，您可以到外面呼吸一下新鲜空气，也算是有点儿事做。”
"No proposal could give me more pleasure. I did not venture to make it myself, lest I should thrust myself upon you." 
“您的建议再好不过了。因为怕麻烦您，我才没敢向您提。”
Commandant Genestas (who shall keep his own name in spite of the fictitious appellation which he had thought fit to give himself) followed his host to a room on the first floor above the salon.
热奈斯塔指挥官（尽管他给自己编了个事先考虑好的假名，我们还是保留用这个名字）跟在主人后面，来到客厅上面，一楼的一个房间。
"That is right," said Benassis, "Jacquotte has lighted a fire for you. If you want anything, there is a bell-pull close to the head of the bed.” 
“很不错，”贝纳西说，“雅科特给您生了火炉。如果您有什么需要的话，可以拉一下床头边的拉铃绳。”
"I am not likely to want anything, however small, it seems to me," exclaimed Genestas. "There is even a boot-jack. Only an old trooper knows what a boot-jack is worth! There are times, when one is out on a campaign, sir, when one is ready to burn down a house to come by a knave of a boot-jack. After a few marches, one on the top of another, or above all, after an engagement, there are times when a swollen foot and the soaked leather will not part company, pull as you will; I have had to lie down in my boots more than once. One can put up with the annoyance so long as one is by oneself.” 
“在我看来，没什么需要的了。”热奈斯塔大声说，“连鞋拔子这么小的东西都有了。只有老骑兵知道鞋拔子有多么重要！先生，以前打仗的时候，为了找到一只鞋拔子，可能要烧掉一幢房子呢。有时连续行军，或者是参加一场战斗之后，脚会肿起来，无论怎么扯，浸湿的鞋子都脱不掉。所以很多时候，我就只能穿着鞋睡。当然，单身的时候，这种情况还勉强可以忍受。”
The commandant's wink gave a kind of profound slyness to his last utterance; then he began to make a survey. Not without surprise, he saw that the room was neatly kept, comfortable, and almost luxurious.
这位指挥官在说最后一句话时，朝医生使了个眼色，眼神中带着一丝狡黠。然后他开始仔细观察这个房间。房间看上去干净整洁、舒适宜人，几乎可以称得上豪华，这让他很吃惊。
"What splendor!" was his comment. "Your own room must be something wonderful." 
“真豪华呀！”他感叹道，“您自己的房间也应该很不错吧。”
"Come and see," said the doctor; "I am your neighbor, there is nothing but the staircase between us." 
“那就过来看看吧。”医生说，“我的房间就在您旁边，我们之间只隔着楼梯而已。”
Genestas was again surprised when he entered the doctor's room, a bare-looking apartment with no adornment on the walls save an old-fashioned wall-paper of a yellowish tint with a pattern of brown roses over it; the color had gone in patches here and there. There was a roughly painted iron bedstead, two gray cotton curtains were suspended from a wooden bracket above it, and a threadbare strip of carpet lay at the foot; it was like a bed in a hospital. By the bed-head stood a rickety cupboard on four feet with a door that continually rattled with a sound like castanets. Three chairs and a couple of straw-bottomed armchairs stood about the room, and on a low chest of drawers in walnut wood stood a basin, and a ewer of obsolete pattern with a lid, which was kept in place by a leaden rim round the top of the vessel. This completed the list of the furniture.
走进医生的房间后，热奈斯塔又一次感到吃惊，不过这次是因为他发现这个房间很简陋。除了墙上贴着老式的、黄底带棕色玫瑰花的墙纸外，几乎没有什么装饰了，而且墙纸上的有些地方已经褪色了。房间里放着张随便漆了一下的铁床，床上面的木架上挂着两块灰色的棉布床幔，床脚下则铺着一条破烂的毛毯，这简直就像医院里的一张破床。床头立着一个快要散架的四脚床头柜，打开柜门就会不停地发出像响板一样的嘎吱声音。房间里面还有三张椅子和两张铺着草垫的扶手椅，一张胡桃木矮屉柜上放着只脸盆和一只老式带盖水罐，水罐顶端四周镶了铅边。这些便是房间里的所有家具。
The grate was empty. All the apparatus required for shaving lay about in front of an old mirror suspended above the painted stone chimney-piece by a bit of string. The floor was clean and carefully swept, but it was worn and splintered in various places, and there were hollows in it here and there. Gray cotton curtains bordered with a green fringe adorned the two windows. The scrupulous cleanliness maintained by Jacquotte gave a certain air of distinction to this picture of simplicity, but everything in it, down to the round table littered with stray papers, and the very pens on the writing-desk, gave the idea of an almost monastic life—a life so wholly filled with thought and feeling of a wider kind that outward surroundings had come to be matters of no moment. An open door allowed the commandant to see the smaller room, which doubtless the doctor seldom occupied. It was scarcely kept in the same condition as the adjoining apartment; a few dusty books lay strewn about over the no less dusty shelves, and from the rows of labeled bottles it was easy to guess that the place was devoted rather to the dispensing of drugs than scientific studies.
壁炉里面空空的，没生火。漆过的石炉台上面用一小根线挂着一面旧镜子，镜子前散放着些刮胡子用的工具。地板是经过细心打扫的，很干净，不过好几个地方已经磨损了，有些地方都出现了小洞。两扇窗户上挂着镶了绿色流苏的灰色棉布窗帘。雅科特把房间收拾得干干净净，给这个简朴的房间增添了一份别致。不过房间里的一切，比如说散放着纸张的圆桌、躺在书桌上的钢笔，都让人感觉主人过着隐士般的生活——一种充满思想和情感，却又淡薄名利的生活。房间里还有扇门敞开着，指挥官因此看到里面是个小房间，很显然医生很少用这个小房间。小房间看上去很少被打理，和医生卧室的情况差不多。满是灰尘的书架上散放着几本同样布满灰尘的书。从几排带标签的瓶子很容易看出，这个架子更多是用来放药品而不是用来做科学研究的。
"Why this difference between your room and mine, you will ask?" said Benassis. "Listen a moment. I have always blushed for those who put their guests in the attics, who furnish them with mirrors that distort everything to such a degree that any one beholding himself might think that he was smaller or larger than nature made him, or suffering from apoplectic stroke or some other bad complaint. Ought we not to do our utmost to make a room as pleasant as possible during the time that our friend can be with us? Hospitality, to my thinking, is a virtue, a pleasure, and a luxury; but in whatever light it is considered, nay, even if you regard it as a speculation, ought not our guest or our friend to be made much of? Ought not every refinement of luxury to be reserved for him?
“您可能会问，为什么我的房间和您的完全不一样？”贝纳西说，“您听我说。有些人让客人住在阁楼里，给客人房间里装了能把人照变形的镜子，结果把人照小了或照大了，甚至让客人中风了或是病倒了，我真为这种人感到羞耻。难道我们不应该尽全力让客人和我们在一起的时候，住得更舒服些吗？在我看来，好客是一种美德、一种快乐、一种难得的享受。不管如何去看待这个问题，哪怕把它当作一种投机行为，难道不应该让我们的客人或朋友住得更惬意一些吗？难道不应该把装修豪华而精美的房间留给他们住吗？
"So the best furniture is put into your room, where a thick carpet is laid down; there are hangings on the walls, and a clock and wax candles; and for you Jacquotte will do her best, she has no doubt brought a night-light, and a pair of new slippers and some milk, and her warming-pan too for your benefit. I hope that you will find that luxurious armchair the most comfortable seat you have ever sat in, it was a discovery of the late cure's; I do not know where he found it, but it is a fact that if you wish to meet with the perfection of comfort, beauty, or convenience, you must ask counsel of the Church. Well, I hope that you will find everything in your room to your liking. You will find some good razors and excellent soap, and all the trifling details that make one's own home so pleasant. And if my views on the subject of hospitality should not at once explain the difference between your room and mine, to-morrow, M. Bluteau, you will arrive at a wonderfully clear comprehension of the bareness of my room and the untidy condition of my study, when you see all the continual comings and goings here. Mine is not an indoor life, to begin with. I am almost always out of the house, and if I stay at home, peasants come in at every moment to speak to me. My body and soul and house are all theirs. Why should I worry about social conventions in these matters, or trouble myself over the damage unintentionally done to floors and furniture by these worthy folk? Such things cannot be helped. Luxury properly belongs to the boudoir and the guest-chamber, to great houses and chateaux. In short, as I scarcely do more than sleep here, what do I want with superfluities of wealth? You do not know, moreover, how little I care for anything in this world.” 
“所以，我把最好的家具放到您的房间了，地上铺的是厚厚的地毯，墙上还挂有装饰品，还有时钟、烛台。雅科特会尽全力把您照顾好，很显然她已经给您点上了夜灯，拿了双新拖鞋、牛奶，当然还拿了暖床炉给您用。我希望您坐在那张豪华的扶手椅上能感到无比舒适，它的确是已故神父的一大发现。我也不知道他从哪儿弄到这张椅子的，不过事实就是，如果想找到一件舒适、漂亮又实用的完美物品，就得找教会帮忙。当然，我希望您能喜欢房间里的一切。您会看到房间里有优质的刮胡刀、上等的肥皂，以及其他一些能让日常起居更舒适的小东西。如果我对于好客之道的理解还不足以解释我们两人的房间差异的话，那么，布吕托先生，您明天看看进出我们家的那些人之后，您就可以很好地理解为什么我的房间那么简陋，而且书房又那么乱。首先，我并不经常呆在家里。我总是在外出诊，即便呆在家里，也总会有农民来找我。这样的话，我整个人和这房间就属于他们了。我为什么要自找麻烦去操心一些礼节方面的事，去担心这些好人可能无意中会损坏我的地板或是家具呢？这些都是不可避免的。奢华只属于姑娘的闺房、客人的房间或是大宅和豪华的别墅。总之，我也就在房间里睡个觉，装修得再豪华又有什么用呢？而且，您不知道，对于尘世间的这些东西我看得有多淡。”
They wished each other a friendly good-night with a warm shake of the hand, and went to bed. But before the commandant slept, he came to more than one conclusion as to the man who hour by hour grew greater in his eyes.
他们亲切地握了握手，互相道了声晚安，然后回房休息了。但指挥官对这位在他眼里形象变得越来越高大的医生，反复思考后，才入睡。
