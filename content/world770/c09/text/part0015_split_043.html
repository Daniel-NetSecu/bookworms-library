HOPE.  

希望  

Sunday, 29th.

星期日，29日

Very beautiful, Enrico, was the impetuosity with which you flung yourself on your mother's heart on your return from your lesson of religion. Yes, your master said grand and consoling things to you. God threw you in each other's arms; he will never part you. When I die, when your father dies, we shall not speak to each other these despairing words, "Mamma, papa, Enrico, I shall never see you again!" We shall see each other again in another life, where he who has suffered much in this life will receive compensation; where he who has loved much on earth will find again the souls whom he has loved, in a world without sin, without sorrow, and without death. But we must all render ourselves worthy of that other life. Reflect, my son. Every good action of yours, every impulse of affection for those who love you, every courteous act towards your companions, every noble thought of yours, is like a leap towards that other world. And every misfortune, also, serves to raise you towards that world; every sorrow, for every sorrow is the expiation of a sin, every tear blots out a stain. Make it your rule to become better and more loving every day than the day before. Say every morning, "Today I will do something for which my conscience will praise me, and with which my father will be satisfied; something which will render me beloved by such or such a comrade, by my teacher, by my brother, or by others." And beseech God to give you the strength to put your resolution into practice. "Lord, I wish to be good, noble, courageous, gentle, sincere; help me; grant that every night, when my mother gives me her last kiss, I may be able to say to her, 'You kiss this night a nobler and more worthy boy than you kissed last night.'" Keep always in your thoughts that other superhuman and blessed Enrico which you may be after this life. And pray. You cannot imagine the sweetness that you experience,—how much better a mother feels when she sees her child with hands clasped in prayer. When I behold you praying, it seems impossible to me that there should not be some one there gazing at you and listening to you. Then I believe more firmly that there is a supreme goodness and an infinite pity; I love you more, I work with more ardor, I endure with more force, I forgive with all my heart, and I think of death with serenity. O great and good God! To hear once more, after death, the voice of my mother, to meet my children again, to see my Enrico once more, my Enrico, blessed and immortal, and to clasp him in an embrace which shall nevermore be loosed, nevermore, nevermore to all eternity! Oh, pray! let us pray, let us love each other, let us be good, let us bear this celestial hope in our hearts and souls, my adored child!
安利柯，你上完宗教课回来，扑向母亲怀抱时的热切之情真是太美了。是啊，你的老师跟你们讲了伟大而又令人宽慰的事情。上帝让你们互相拥抱，他永远不会让你们分开的。在我死的时候，或者你父亲死的时候，我们不会对彼此说这些令人绝望的话：“妈妈，爸爸，安利柯，我再也见不到你们了！”我们会在另一个世界见面。我们在这个世界遭受的磨难，会在另一个世界得到补偿；我们会在那个没有罪恶、没有痛苦、没有死亡的世界里重新找到在人世间所爱的人们。但是我们都必须让自己成为有资格生活在那个世界的人。好好想想，我的儿子。你做的每件好事、对那些爱你的人的每一次情感的宣泄、对你的伙伴的每一次善举，还有你每一个高尚的念头，都像是朝向那个世界的一次飞跃。而每次不幸也能让你离那个世界更近；痛苦也是一样，因为每次遭受痛苦就是在赎罪，每一滴泪水都能抹去一个污点。你要越来越优秀，每天都要比前一天更有爱心，如此坚持下去。每天早晨对自己说：“今天我要做些无愧于心的、并能让父亲满意的事情；要做些能让我的同学、老师、兄弟或者其他人爱我的事情。”并恳求上帝赐予你把决心付诸行动的力量。“上帝，我想成为一个善良、高尚、勇敢、有礼貌、真诚的人，请帮助我；当每天晚上母亲给我晚安吻的时候，请让我能够对她说：‘今晚您亲吻的男孩比昨晚更高尚、更可敬。’”你要谨记，你此生之后将会成为另一个非凡的、深受祝福的安利柯。为此祈祷吧。你想象不到你所经历的事是多么美妙——当一个母亲看到儿子双手合十祈祷时，感觉好极了。当我看着正在祈祷的你，我似乎觉得那里不可能没有人在看着你、听着你。于是我更加坚信，真有一位至高无上的上帝，而且他有无尽的怜悯；我更加爱你，更加热情地工作，更努力地坚持下去，我会全心全意地饶恕别人，并平静地面对死亡。哦，伟大、善良的上帝！请让我在死后能再一次听到我母亲的声音，再一次看到我的孩子们，再一次见到我的安利柯，我那蒙福的、永生的安利柯，让我紧紧拥抱他绝不再松开，绝不、绝不，直到永远！哦，祈祷吧！我亲爱的孩子，让我们祈祷，让我们彼此相爱，让我们做个好人，让我们一直怀着内心和灵魂深处的这份崇高的希望！
Thy Mother.
你的母亲
