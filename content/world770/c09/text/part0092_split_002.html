The Father  

父亲  

I

一

As he lived at Batignolles and was a clerk in the Public Education Office, he took the omnibus every morning, when he went to the center of Paris, sitting opposite a girl with whom he fell in love.
他是公众教育部的一名职员，住在巴蒂诺尔街。每天早上他乘公共马车去巴黎市中心时，都面对着一个他所爱慕的姑娘坐着。
She went to the shop where she was employed, at the same time every day. She was a little brunette, one of those dark girls whose eyes are so dark that they look like spots, and whose complexion has a look like ivory. He always saw her coming at the corner of the same street, and she generally had to run to catch the heavy vehicle, and sprang upon the steps before the horses had quite stopped. Then she got inside, rather out of breath, and sitting down, she looked round her.
她每天都是同一个时间去她所受雇的店里。这是一个娇小、头发浅黑的女子，这种浅黑色使得她的眼珠子看起来如同两颗黑球，皮肤像象牙一样白。他总是看见她从同一条街的拐角处出来，而且通常她不得不跑去赶那辆笨重的车子，在马车没完全停止之前就跳上踏板。然后上气不接下气地走进车内，随后坐下，瞧瞧周围。
The first time that he saw her, François Tessier felt that her face pleased him extremely. One sometimes meets one of those women whom one longs to clasp madly in one's arms immediately, without even knowing her. That girl answered to his inward desires, to his secret hopes, to that sort of ideal of love which one cherishes in the depths of the heart, without knowing it.
弗朗索瓦·泰西耶第一次看见她的时候，便觉得她的脸蛋非常讨他喜欢。有时候，人们会碰到那种连她们的名字都不知道，但立刻就令人渴望疯狂拥抱的女人。这个女孩符合他的内心愿望、秘密期盼，符合那种内心深处不知不觉所怀有的爱情理想。
He looked at her intently, in spite of himself, and she grew embarrassed at his looks and blushed. He saw it and tried to turn away his eyes; but he involuntarily fixed them upon her again every moment, although he tried to look in another direction, and in a few days they knew each other without having spoken. He gave up his place to her when the omnibus was full, and got outside, though he was very sorry to do it. By this time, she had got so far as to greet him with a little smile; and although she always dropped her eyes under his looks, which she felt were too ardent, yet she did not appear offended at being looked at in such a manner.
他不由自主地专心地看着她。他的样子让她觉得很不安，脸都羞红了。他意识到了，并且想移开眼睛。尽管他设法向另一方向望去，但不知不觉又时刻注视起她来。几天之后，他们还没说话就相互认识了。当车子满座时，他就把座位让给她，自己站到外边去，尽管他非常不愿意这样。这个时候，她只是冲他微微一笑，以示招呼。她觉得他的目光过于热烈，虽然她总是垂下眼睛，但并没有表现出被那种注视所冒犯的意思。
They ended by speaking. A kind of rapid intimacy had become established between them, a daily intimacy of half an hour, and that was certainly one of the most charming half hours in his life, to him. He thought of her all the rest of the time, saw her continually during the long office hours, for he was haunted and bewitched by that floating and yet tenacious recollection which the image of a beloved woman leaves in us, and it seemed to him that the entire possession of that little person would be maddening happiness to him, almost above human realization.
最后，他们说话了。他们之间很快建立了一种亲密关系。对于他来说，每天这半个钟头的亲密无疑是令他毕生最陶醉的半个钟头。此外的所有时间，他都在想着她。在漫长的办公时间，他也总是看见她，被这个飘忽而又固执的心爱女人留在我们心中的形象的回忆所缠扰、着迷。对于他来说，能够完全拥有这个小女人似乎会让他幸福得发疯，几乎在人类现实之上。
Every morning now she shook hands with him, and he preserved the feeling of that touch, and the recollection of the gentle pressure of her little fingers, until the next day, and he almost fancied that he preserved the imprint of it, on his skin, and he anxiously waited for this short omnibus ride, all the rest of the time, while Sundays seemed to him heart-breaking days. However, there was no doubt that she loved him, for one Saturday, in spring, she promised to go and lunch with him at Maisons–Laffitte the next day.
现在，每天早上她都和他握手。他把那种触碰的感觉和纤纤玉指轻压的记忆一直保存到第二天，他几乎幻想着把它印到自己的皮肤里面。除了星期日，其他时间他都焦急地等待着这个公共马车的短途旅程，在他看来，星期日成了伤心难过的日子。然而毫无疑问，她爱上了他，因为在春天的某个星期六，她承诺次日和他一起到迈松拉菲特去吃午餐。

II

二

She was at the railway station first, which surprised him, but she said: "Before going, I want to speak to you. We have twenty minutes, and that is more than I shall take for what I have to say." 
那天，她第一个到了火车站，这令他很惊讶，但是她说：“在出发前，我有话要跟你说。我们有二十分钟，这足够我把话说完。”
She trembled as she hung onto his arm, and she looked down, while her cheeks were pale, but she continued: "I do not want to be deceived in you, and I shall not go there with you, unless you promise, unless you swear ... not to do ... not to do anything ... that is at all improper ..." 
她靠着他的胳膊，浑身发抖，而且脸色苍白，双眼看看地。但她接着说：“我不是你想象的那样，除非你保证，你发誓……不做……不做任何……不规矩的事，那么我才和你到那儿去。”
She had suddenly become as red as a poppy, and said no more. He did not know what to reply, for he was happy and disappointed at the same time. At the bottom of his heart, he perhaps preferred that it should be so, and yet ... yet during the night he had indulged in anticipations that sent the hot blood flowing through his veins. He should love her less, certainly, if he knew that her conduct was light, but then it would be so charming, so delicious for him! And he made all a man's usual selfish calculations in love affairs.
她的脸色突然间变得像罂粟花一样红，之后再没说话了。他不知道该答复什么，因为他既高兴又失望。在他内心深处，也许他宁愿她如此，然而……然而在夜间，他却沉溺于那种期待，这令他脉管里热血沸腾。如果他知道她举止轻浮，他一定不会这样爱她，不过对他来说，那又是多么令人着迷、令人愉快！于是，他打着在恋爱中男人常有的小算盘。
As he did not say anything, she began to speak again in an agitated voice, and with tears in her eyes. "If you do not promise to respect me altogether, I shall return home." And so he squeezed her arm tenderly and replied: "I promise, you shall only do what you like." She appeared relieved in mind, and asked with a smile: "Do you really mean it?" And he looked into her eyes and replied: "I swear it." "Now you may take the tickets." she said.
因为他一个字也没说，所以她眼睛里噙着泪水，开始用激动的声音继续说道：“如果你不保证完全尊重我，我就回家去。”因此，他温情地紧握着她的胳膊答道：“我保证，你只做你喜欢的事情。”　她表现出心里放心的样子，微笑着问道：“真是这样吗？”他看着她的双眼，回答道：“我向你发誓。”“那你现在去买车票吧。”她说。
During the journey they could hardly speak, as the carriage was full, and when they got to Maison–Laffitte they went towards the Seine. The sun, which shone full onto the river, onto the leaves and onto the turf seemed to be reflected in them in his brightness, and they went, hand in hand, along the bank, looking at the shoals of little fish swimming near the bank, and they went on brimming over with happiness, as if they were raised from the earth in their lightness of heart.
他们一路上几乎不能说话，因为车厢里载满了人。当他们到达迈松拉菲特时，便向塞纳河走去。太阳光满照在河面上、树叶上和草地上，仿佛在它们上面反射着光芒。他们一面手挽着手沿着河岸走，一面看着成群的小鱼靠着河岸游动。他们走着，心中充满了幸福，仿佛怀着轻松喜悦的心情脱离了尘世。
At last she said: "How foolish you must think me!" 
最后她说道：“你肯定认为我很傻！”
"Why?" he asked. "To come out like this, all alone with you?" "Certainly not; it is quite natural." "No, no; it is not natural for me—because I do not wish to commit a fault, and yet this is how girls fall. But if you only knew how wretched it is, every day the same thing, every day in the month, and every month in the year. I live quite alone with Mamma, and as she has had a great deal of trouble, she is not very cheerful. I do the best I can, and try to laugh in spite of everything, but I do not always succeed. But all the same, it was wrong in me to come, though you, at any rate, will not be sorry.” 
“为什么？”他问道。“像这样单独和你出来难道不傻吗？”“当然不，这是很自然的事。”“不，不。对我来说，这并不自然，因为我不希望犯错，然而女人就是这样陷进去的。但是如果你知道，每天、每月、每年都经历着同样的事情是多么不幸。我单独和母亲生活在一起，因为她有许多伤心事，过得不是很快乐。我尽我所能，无论如何都努力去笑，但不是每次都能办到。今天也是一样的，到这里来是我不对，但至少你不会后悔吧。”
By way of an answer he kissed her ardently on her ear that was nearest him, but she moved from him with an abrupt movement, and getting suddenly angry, she exclaimed: "Oh! Monsieur François, after what you swore to me!” And they went back to Maison–Laffitte.
他在她离他最近的耳上深情地吻了一下作为回答。但是她突然一动远离了他，她变得很生气，喊道：“喂！弗朗索瓦先生，您是跟我发过誓的！”于是他们回到了迈松拉菲特。
They had lunch at the Petit–Havre , a low house, buried under four enormous poplar trees, by the side of the river. The air, the heat, the light wine, and the sensation of being so close together, made them red and silent, with a feeling of oppression, but after the coffee, they regained all their high spirits, and having crossed the Seine, they started off along the bank, towards the village of La Frette, and suddenly he asked: "What is your name?" "Louise." "Louise." he repeated, and said nothing more.
他们在珀蒂阿弗尔的一个矮房子里吃午饭，房子靠近河边，被四棵大白杨树遮着。当时的空气、湿热的温度、淡淡的葡萄酒和两个人靠拢坐着的感觉使他们脸红、沉默，有一种压抑感。但是喝过了咖啡后，他们又恢复了高涨的情绪。跨过了塞纳河后，他们开始沿着河岸向拉弗雷特走去。他突然问道：“你叫什么？”“路易丝。”“路易丝。”他跟着说了一遍，之后再没有多说什么。
The river, which described a long curve, bathed a row of white houses in the distance, which were reflected in the water. The girl picked the daisies and made them into a great bunch, whilst he sang vigorously, as intoxicated as a colt that has been turned into a meadow. On their left, a vine-covered slope followed the river, but suddenly François stopped motionless with astonishment: "Oh! look there!" he said.
河身勾勒出一条长长的曲线，沐浴着远处一行白色的房子，房子倒映在水面上。路易丝采了些雏菊，把它们扎成了一个大花束；而他则放声歌唱，兴奋得像一匹到了草地上的小马驹一样。他们的左边是一个沿着河的藤蔓丛生的斜坡。弗朗索瓦突然停下来，惊奇得一动不动地说道：“啊！看那儿！”
The vines had come to an end, and the whole slope was covered with lilac bushes in flower. It was a violet colored wood! A kind of great carpet stretched over the earth, reaching as far as the village, more than two miles off. She also stood, surprised and delighted, and murmured: "Oh! how pretty!" And crossing a meadow they ran towards that curious low hill, which every year furnishes all the lilac which is drawn through Paris on the carts of the street sellers.
藤蔓已经长到终点了，整个斜坡都被丁香花丛所覆盖。这是一片紫色的树林！一张大毯子铺在地上，一直延伸到那边两公里之外的村子里。她也惊喜得站住了，喃喃地说：“啊！多么美丽呀！”他们穿过草地，朝那座特别的小山丘跑去，那儿就是每年遍布巴黎街头的那些小贩车子上所载着的丁香花的出产地。
A narrow path went beneath the trees, so they took it, and when they came to a small clearing, they sat down.
树下有一条狭窄的小道，他们走了进去，走到一块小空地上时，他们便坐下了。
Swarms of flies were buzzing around them and making a continuous, gentle sound, and the sun, the bright sun of a perfectly still day, shone over the bright slopes, and from that wood of flowers, a powerful aroma was borne towards them, a breath of perfume, of that sweat of the flowers.
成群的蜜蜂在他们身旁旋绕，发出一种轻轻的、持续不断的嗡嗡声。阳光，极度平静的一天的明亮阳光照在繁花似锦的小山坡上。花丛树木中散发出的一股浓郁的香味、一阵芬芳的气息，还有花儿分泌的水珠的香气向他们飘来。
A church clock struck in the distance, and they embraced gently, then clasped each other close, lying on the grass, without the knowledge of anything except of that kiss. She had closed her eyes and held him in her arms, pressing him to her closely, without a thought, with her reason bewildered, and from head to foot in passionate expectation. And she surrendered herself altogether, without knowing that she had given herself to him. But she soon came to herself with the feeling of a great misfortune, and she began to cry and sob with grief, with her face buried in her hands.
远处一座教堂的钟声敲响了，他们轻轻地相拥，之后紧紧地抱在一起，躺在草地上，除了接吻以外，什么都不想。她闭着眼睛，双臂抱着他，不知不觉紧紧地拥着他，头脑迷迷糊糊的，从头到脚都沉醉在一种热情的期待中。于是她完全妥协了，不知道自己已经委身于他了。但她很快便在一种极为不幸的感觉中清醒过来。于是她两手遮面，开始痛哭、抽噎起来。
He tried to console her, but she wanted to start, to return, and to go home immediately, and she kept saying as she walked along quickly: "Good heavens! good heavens!" He said to her: "Louise! Louise! Please let us stop here." But now her cheeks were red and her eyes hollow, and as soon as they got to the railway station in Paris, she left him, without even saying good-bye.
他试着安慰她，但是她要走，想立刻起身回家去。她一边快速向前走，一边不停地说道：“天哪！天哪！”他对她说：“路易丝！路易丝！我们停到这儿好吗？”但是她现在两颊通红，眼神空洞。他们一到达巴黎的火车站，她没有跟他道别就离开了。

III

三

When he met her in the omnibus next day, she appeared to him to be changed and thinner, and she said to him: "I want to speak to you; we will get down at the Boulevard." 
次日，当他在公共马车上遇见她时，他觉得她变了，瘦了。她对他说：“我想和你讲几句话，我们就在大街上下车吧！”
As soon as they were on the pavement, she said: "We must bid each other good-bye; I cannot meet you again after what has happened.” "But why?" he asked. "Because I cannot; I have been culpable, and I will not be so again." 
一到大街的人行道上，她便说：“我们必须分手了，因为过去发生的事，我以后不能再和你见面了。”“但是为什么？”他问道。“因为我不能。我已经有罪了，我不想再犯罪。”
Then he implored her, tortured by desire, maddened by the wish of having her entirely, in the absolute freedom of nights of love, but she replied firmly: "No, I cannot, I cannot." He, however, only grew all the more excited, and promised to marry her, but she said again: "No." And left him.
于是他哀求她，在这绝对自由的爱之夜，他完全被一心想占有她的欲望折磨得发狂。但她坚定地回答道：“不行，我不能，我不能！”然而，他反而变得更加激动了，并许诺与她结婚。但她仍说：“不行。”于是她离开了他。
For a week he did not see her. He could not manage to meet her, and as he did not know her address, he thought that he had lost her altogether. On the ninth day, however, there was a ring at his bell, and when he opened it, she was there. She threw herself into his arms, and did not resist any longer, and for three months she was his mistress. He was beginning to grow tired of her, when she told him she was pregnant, and then he had one idea and wish: To break with her at any price. As, however, he could not do that, not knowing how to begin or what to say, full of anxiety through the fear of that child which was growing, he took a decisive step: One night he changed his lodgings, and disappeared.
他已经有一个星期没见到她了。他无法找到她，而且因为不知道她的住址，他以为自己完全失去了她。然而第九天，他的门铃响了。他打开门，是她站在那儿。她扑到他怀里，不再抵抗。这三个月里，她成了他的情妇。当他得知她怀有身孕的时候，他便开始厌倦她了，头脑里只有一个念头和愿望，那就是不惜任何代价和她分手。然而，由于他不能那样做，不知道怎么开口，也不知道讲什么，害怕这个慢慢长大的孩子，他焦急万分，于是他采取了果断的一步：在一个夜晚搬了家，消失了。
The blow was so heavy that she did not look for the man who had abandoned her, but threw herself at her mother's knees and confessed her misfortune, and some months after, she gave birth to a boy.
这个打击太沉重了，以至于她都没有去寻找这个抛弃她的人，而是扑到她母亲的膝前，坦白了自己的不幸。几个月后，她生下了一个男孩。

IV

四

Years passed, and François Tessier grew old without there having been any alteration in his life. He led the dull, monotonous life of bureaucrates, without hopes and without expectations. Every day he got up at the same time, went through the same streets, went through the same door, passed the same porter, went into the same office, sat in the same chair, and did the same work. He was alone in the world, alone, during the day in the midst of his colleagues, and alone at night in his bachelor's lodgings, and he laid by a hundred francs a month, against old age.
岁月流逝，弗朗索瓦·泰西耶渐渐地老去，但是他的生活却没有丝毫改变。作为一位办事员，他过着无趣、单调的生活，既没有希冀也没有期盼。他每天在同一时间起床，穿过同样的街道，走过同一扇门，经过同一个看门人，走进同一个办公室，坐在同一把椅子上，做着同样的工作。他是世界上孤独的人，孤零零的，白天和同事处在一起，夜晚独自一人在自己的单身宿舍里，他每月积攒一百法郎，以备养老。
Every Sunday he went to the Champs–Elysées, to watch the elegant people, the carriages and the pretty women, and the next day he used to say to one of his colleagues: "The return of the carriages from the Bois de Boulogne was very brilliant yesterday." One fine Sunday morning, however, he went into the Parc Monceau, where the mothers and nurses, sitting on the sides of the walks, watched the children playing, and suddenly François Tessier started. A woman passed by, holding two children by the hand; a little boy of about ten and a little girl of four. It was she.
每逢星期日，他都去香榭丽舍大道散步，看那些举止优雅的人、马车，还有漂亮的女人们。次日，他就会对他其中的一个同事说：“昨天从布洛涅公园回来的车很抢眼啊。”然而，一个星期日的早上，他走进蒙索公园，母亲们和保姆们都在路旁坐着，看着孩子们玩耍。突然，弗朗索瓦·泰西耶吓了一跳。一个妇女牵着两个小孩从他面前经过。小男孩大约十来岁，小女孩大约四岁。是她。
He walked another hundred yards, and then fell into a chair, choking with emotion. She had not recognized him, and so he came back, wishing to see her again. She was sitting down now, and the boy was standing by her side very quietly, while the little girl was making sand castles. It was she, it was certainly she, but she had the serious looks of a lady, was dressed simply, and looked self-possessed and dignified. He looked at her from a distance, for he did not venture to go near, but the little boy raised his head, and François Tessier felt himself tremble. It was his own son, there could be no doubt of that. And as he looked at him, he thought he could recognize himself as he appeared in an old photograph taken years ago. He remained hidden behind a tree, waiting for her to go, that he might follow her.
他又走了一百来码，便倒在了一把椅子上，激动得说不出话来。她没有认出他来，于是他便返回去想再看看她。她现在坐下了，小男孩很安静地站在她旁边，小女孩在堆沙堡。是她，确实是她。但她却有着一副严肃女人的神情，衣着朴素，看起来端庄镇定。他远远地看着她，因为他不敢冒昧地走近。小男孩抬起头，弗朗索瓦·泰西耶感觉自己浑身发抖。毫无疑问，这是他的儿子。他看着他，觉得他能认出往日旧照片上的自己。他一直躲在一棵树后面，等着她离开，这样他可以跟着她走。
He did not sleep that night. The idea of the child especially harrassed him. His son!
那晚他彻夜难眠。对孩子的思念格外困扰着他。是他的儿子啊！
Oh! If he could only have known, have been sure? But what could he have done? However, he went to the house where she had lived, and asked about her. He was told that a neighbor, an honorable man of strict morals, had been touched by her distress, and had married her; he knew the fault she had committed and had married her, and had even recognized the child, his, François Tessier's child, as his own.
唉！如果他只是已经知道，已经确定呢？但是他能怎么办呢？于是，他去她曾经的住所打听她的情况。他得知她嫁给了一个邻居，一个被她的不幸所感动的道德高尚的、令人尊敬的男子。这个男子知道她犯的过错，并且娶了她，还认弗朗索瓦·泰西耶的儿子为自己的儿子。
He returned to the Parc Monceau every Sunday, for then he always saw her, and each time he was seized with a mad, an irresistible longing, to take his son into his arms, cover him with kisses and to steal him, to carry him off.
他每个星期日都到蒙索公园去，因为他总能看见她，并且他每次都产生一种疯狂的、无法抗拒的渴望，想把他的儿子抱在怀里，吻他、偷走他，带他离开。
He suffered horribly in his wretched isolation as an old bachelor, with nobody to care for him, and he also suffered atrocious mental torture, torn by paternal tenderness springing from remorse, longing and jealousy, and from that need of loving one's own children, which nature has implanted into all, and so at last he determined to make a despairing attempt, and going up to her, as she entered the park, he said, standing in the middle of the path, pale and with trembling lips: "You do not recognize me." She raised her eyes, looked at him, uttered an exclamation of horror, of terror, and, taking the two children by the hand she rushed away, dragging them after her, whilst he went home and wept, inconsolably.
作为一个老单身汉，他遭受着可怕的、不幸的孤独，没有任何人关心他。同时他还遭受着残忍的精神折磨，懊悔、想念、妒忌和自然赋予众生的爱子之需交织而成的父子之情，把他的心都撕碎了。最后，他决定孤注一掷。于是当她走进公园时，他走到她面前，站在路中间，脸色苍白，嘴唇颤抖着说：“你不认识我了。”她抬眼看着他，发出一声惊骇的、恐怖的叫喊，便拉上两个孩子，拖着他们跑开了。而他回到家伤心欲绝地哭了。
Months passed without his seeing her again, but he suffered, day and night, for he was a prey to his paternal love. He would gladly have died, if he could only have kissed his son, he would have committed murder, performed any task, braved any danger, ventured anything. He wrote to her, but she did not reply, and after writing her some twenty letters he saw that there was no hope of altering her determination, and then he formed the desperate resolution of writing to her husband, being quite prepared to receive a bullet from a revolver, if need be. His letter only consisted of a few lines, as follows: "Monsieur,  You must have a perfect horror of my name, but I am so miserable, so overcome by misery, that my only hope is in you, and therefore I venture to request you to grant me an interview of only five minutes.”  "I have the honor, etc."  The next day he received the reply: "Monsieur,  I shall expect you tomorrow, Tuesday, at five o'clock.” 
几个月过去了，他再也没有见到过她，但是他整日整夜都很苦恼，因为他是他们父子之情的牺牲品。倘若他能亲吻到自己的儿子，他可以死，可以杀人，可以执行任何任务，可以勇敢面对任何危险，可以冒险做任何事情。他给她写信，但是她不回复。写到大约二十封信的时候，他意识到没有改变她决心的希望了。于是，绝望中他决定写封信给她丈夫，并准备好在必要的时候被一支左轮枪的子弹打中。他的信只有几句话，如下：“先生，您肯定十分厌恶我的名字，但是我非常苦恼，简直被痛苦所压垮，唯一的希望只有您了，因而我冒昧地拜托您给我五分钟的谈话时间。”“我将十分荣幸。”第二天他便得到回复：“先生，“我们在星期二，就是明天的五点钟见面。”

As he went up the staircase, François Tessier's heart beat so violently that he had to stop several times. There was a dull and violent noise in his breast, the noise as of some animal galloping, and he could only breathe with difficulty, and had to hold on to the banisters in order not to fall.
弗朗索瓦·泰西耶走上楼梯，心跳得非常厉害，他不得不停下来好几次。他的胸口像有马在飞驰一样，声音低沉而强烈。他只能艰难地呼吸，为了不至于摔倒，他不得不扶着楼梯扶手。
He rang the bell on the third floor, and when a maidservant had opened the door, he asked "Does Monsieur Flamel live here?" "Yes. Monsieur. Kindly come in." 
到了第三层楼，他按响了门铃。一位女仆开了门，他问道：“弗拉梅勒先生住在这里吗？”“是的。先生。请进。”
He was shown into the drawing-room; he was alone and waited, feeling bewildered, as in the midst of a catastrophe, until a door opened and a man came in. He was tall, serious, and rather stout, and wore a black frock-coat, and pointed to a chair with his hand. François Tessier sat down, and then said, panting: "Monsieur ... Monsieur ... I do not know whether you know my name ... whether you know ..." 
于是他被领进客厅。他独自等着，不知所措，如同在灾难中一样，直到门打开，一个男子走进来。他身材高大，神情庄重，并且略微有点发胖，穿着黑色大礼服。他用手指着一把椅子。弗朗索瓦·泰西耶坐下了，喘着气说：“先生……先生……我不知道您是否知道我的名字……是否知道……”
Monsieur Flamel interrupted him. "You need not tell it me, Monsieur, I know it. My wife has spoken to me about you." He spoke in the dignified tone of voice of a good man who wishes to be severe, and with the common-place stateliness of an honorable man, and François Tessier continued: "Well, Monsieur, I want to say this: I am dying of grief, of remorse, of shame, and I would like once, only once to kiss ... the child ...” 
弗拉梅勒先生打断了他的话：“这不用说，先生，我知道。我的妻子跟我说过您。”他有一种善良人想要严肃说话时的庄重声调和中等社会端庄体面的人的仪态。弗朗索瓦·泰西耶接着说道：“是这样的，先生，我想说我痛苦、懊悔、羞愧得快要死掉了。我想亲一次那孩子，只要一次……”
Monsieur Flamel got up and rang the bell, and when the servant came in, he said: "Will you bring Louis here." When she had gone out, they remained face to face, without speaking, as they had nothing more to say to one another, and waited. Then, suddenly, a little boy of ten rushed into the room, and ran up to the man whom he believed to be his father, but he stopped when he saw a stranger, and Monsieur Flamel kissed him and said: "Now go and kiss that gentleman, my dear." And the child went up to him nicely, and looked at the stranger.
弗拉梅勒先生站起来，按了下门铃。当女仆走进来时，他说：“你把路易带过来。”女仆走出去后，他们俩面对面等着，什么也没说，因为没有别的什么相互要说的话了。之后，一个十来岁的小男孩突然冲进客厅，朝着那个他以为是他父亲的男人跑去。但是，当他看到有一个陌生人在时，便停下了。弗拉梅勒先生亲吻着他，说道：“现在去亲亲那位先生，我的宝贝。”于是，那孩子小心翼翼地走到他身边，看着这个陌生人。
François Tessier had risen, he let his hat fall, and was ready to fall himself as he looked at his son, while Monsieur Flamel had turned away, from a feeling of delicacy, and was looking out of the window.
弗朗索瓦·泰西耶站了起来，任凭自己的帽子掉下来。他看着自己的儿子，做好了要晕倒的准备。弗拉梅勒先生出于体贴，背过身去，看着窗外。
The child waited in surprise, but he picked up the hat and gave it to the stranger. Then François, taking the child up in his arms, began to kiss him wildly all over his face, on his eyes, his cheeks, on his mouth, on his hair, and the youngster, frightened at the shower of kisses tried to avoid them, turned away his head and pushed away the man's face with his little hands. But suddenly, François Tessier put him down, and cried: "Good-bye! Good-bye!” And he rushed out of the room as if he had been a thief.
孩子惊讶地等着，他捡起帽子，把它还给了这个陌生人。然后，弗朗索瓦把那个孩子抱进怀里，开始疯狂地满脸吻他，眼睛上、脸蛋上、嘴上、头发上都吻遍了。这个小家伙被这种阵雨似的吻弄得惊慌失措，试图躲开它们，于是他把头别过去，用他那双小手推开男人的脸。突然，弗朗索瓦·泰西耶把他放下来，喊道：“再见！再见！”之后他像个小偷似的从客厅里冲了出去。
