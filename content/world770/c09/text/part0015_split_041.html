ENVY.  

嫉妒  

Wednesday, 25th.

星期三，25日

The boy who wrote the best composition of all on our country was Derossi, as usual. And Votini, who thought himself sure of the first medal—I like Votini well enough, although he is rather vain and does polish himself up a trifle too much,—but it makes me scorn him, now that I am his neighbor on the bench, to see how envious he is of Derossi. He would like to vie with him; he studies hard, but he cannot do it by any possibility, for the other is ten times as strong as he is on every point; and Votini rails at him. Carlo Nobis envies him also; but he has so much pride in his body that, purely from pride, he does not allow it to be perceived. Votini, on the other hand, betrays himself: he complains of his difficulties at home, and says that the master is unjust to him; and when Derossi replies so promptly and so well to questions, as he always does, his face clouds over, he hangs his head, pretends not to hear, or tries to laugh, but he laughs awkwardly. And thus every one knows about it, so that when the master praises Derossi they all turn to look at Votini, who chews his venom, and the little mason makes a hare's face at him. Today, for instance, he was put to the torture. The head-master entered the school and announced the result of the examination,—"Derossi ten tenths and the first medal." 
关于我们的祖国的作文写得最好的是代洛西，就跟平常一样。华梯尼本以为他肯定是第一名的——虽然他相当自负，又有点儿太爱打扮自己了，我还是挺喜欢他的——但现在我和他同桌，看到他有多么嫉妒代洛西之后，我开始瞧不起他了。他总想和代洛西比；他用功学习，但是他不可能做到了，因为对手各方面都比他强十倍；于是华梯尼就奚落他。卡洛·诺琵斯也嫉妒代洛西；但他骨子里的骄傲，纯粹只是因为骄傲，使得他不表现出来。而另一方面，华梯尼出卖了他自己：他总是抱怨他在家时的难处，说老师对他不公平；每当代洛西跟平常一样迅速准确地回答出问题，他就沉着脸、低着头，假装没听见，或者试图大笑，但他笑得很不自然。结果所有人都知道了，只要老师一表扬代洛西，他们就都看向面带怨恨的华梯尼，而小石匠则冲他做兔子鬼脸。比如说今天，他就受尽了折磨。校长走进学校，宣布考试结果——“代洛西满分，第一名。”
Votini gave a huge sneeze. The master looked at him: it was not hard to understand the matter. "Votini," he said, "do not let the serpent of envy enter your body; it is a serpent which gnaws at the brain and corrupts the heart." 
华梯尼打了一个大喷嚏。老师看了看他，不难理解他的所作所为。“华梯尼，”他说，“不要让嫉妒这条蛇钻进你的身体，那是条啃噬大脑、腐化心灵的大蛇。”
Every one stared at him except Derossi. Votini tried to make some answer, but could not; he sat there as though turned to stone, and with a white face. Then, while the master was conducting the lesson, he began to write in large characters on a sheet of paper, "I am not envious of those who gain the first medal through favoritism and injustice." It was a note which he meant to send to Derossi. But, in the meantime, I perceived that Derossi's neighbors were plotting among themselves, and whispering in each other's ears, and one cut with penknife from paper a big medal on which they had drawn a black serpent. But Votini did not notice this. The master went out for a few moments. All at once Derossi's neighbors rose and left their seats, for the purpose of coming and solemnly presenting the paper medal to Votini. The whole class was prepared for a scene. Votini had already begun to quiver all over. Derossi exclaimed:— 
每个人都盯着他看，除了代洛西。华梯尼想反驳，但没话说。他像变成了石头一样，面色苍白地坐在那里。然后，在老师上课的时候，他开始在一张纸上用很大的字写道：“我不会嫉妒那些利用偏心和不公平得到第一名的家伙。”这是他写给代洛西的字条。但是，与此同时，我看到代洛西周围的人交头接耳地策划着什么，一个人在一张画着一条黑蛇的纸上用折叠小刀割出一块大大的奖牌。但华梯尼并没有注意到。老师出去了一会儿。突然，代洛西周围的人站起来离开座位，准备走过去把这张纸奖牌郑重地交给华梯尼。整个班的人都等着看好戏。华梯尼已经开始浑身发抖了。代洛西大声喊道：
"Give that to me!" 
“把它给我！”
"So much the better," they replied; "you are the one who ought to carry it." 
“那样更好。”他们回答说，“你才应该得到它。”
Derossi took the medal and tore it into bits. At that moment the master returned, and resumed the lesson. I kept my eye on Votini. He had turned as red as a coal. He took his sheet of paper very, very quietly, as though in absence of mind, rolled it into a ball, on the sly, put it into his mouth, chewed it a little, and then spit it out under the bench. When school broke up, Votini, who was a little confused, let fall his blotting-paper, as he passed Derossi. Derossi politely picked it up, put it in his satchel, and helped him to buckle the straps. Votini dared not raise his eyes.
代洛西拿过奖牌，把它撕成碎片。这时老师回来了，继续上课。我一直看着华梯尼。他的脸红得像烧着的煤炭。他非常非常安静地把那张字条揉成纸球，好像心不在焉似的，悄悄地把它放到嘴里，嚼了一下，然后吐在凳子底下。放学的时候，脑袋有点儿混乱的华梯尼走过代洛西身边时，把他的吸水纸弄掉了。代洛西礼貌地捡起来，把纸放进他的书包里，还帮他扣好背包带。华梯尼头也不敢抬。
