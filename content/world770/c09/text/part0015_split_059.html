THE BOYS' PARENTS.  

男孩们的父母  

Monday, 6th.

星期一，6日

This morning big Stardi, the father, came to wait for his son, fearing lest he should again encounter Franti. But they say that Franti will not be seen again, because he will be put in the penitentiary. There were a great many parents there this morning. Among the rest there was the retail wood-dealer, the father of Coretti, the perfect image of his son, slender, brisk, with his mustache brought to a point, and a ribbon of two colors in the button-hole of his jacket. I know nearly all the parents of the boys, through constantly seeing them there. There is one crooked grandmother, with her white cap, who comes four times a day, whether it rains or snows or storms, to accompany and to get her little grandson, of the upper primary; and she takes off his little cloak and puts it on for him, adjusts his necktie, brushes off the dust, polishes him up, and takes care of the copy-books. It is evident that she has no other thought, that she sees nothing in the world more beautiful. The captain of artillery also comes frequently, the father of Robetti, the lad with the crutches, who saved a child from the omnibus, and as all his son's companions bestow a caress on him in passing, he returns a caress or a salute to every one, and he never forgets any one; he bends over all, and the poorer and more badly dressed they are, the more pleased he seems to be, and he thanks them.
今天早上，大斯带地，那位父亲，来等着接他的儿子，怕他再碰到弗兰蒂。但是他们说弗兰蒂不会再出现了，因为他被送进教养所了。今天早上很多家长都来了。其中有可莱谛的父亲，那个木材零售商。他跟儿子长得很像，瘦高、很活泼，胡子根根直立，夹克的纽扣孔上别着一条双色的缎带。我几乎认识所有孩子的家长，因为经常在那里看到他们。还有一位驼背的祖母，她戴着白色的帽子，她一天来学校四次，接送她那上小学高级班的孙子，不管是下雨、下雪还是暴风雨天；她给他穿脱小披风，调整他的领结，扫掉灰尘，给他整理仪容，并保管着字帖。很明显，她没有别的心思，也不觉得世上有更美的东西。炮兵上尉也常来，他是洛佩谛——那个从公共马车下救下一个小孩、拄着拐杖的少年——的父亲。所有他儿子的伙伴走过时都会抚摸他一下，而他也会还他们每个人一个爱抚或敬礼，并且从不会忘记任何一个人；他会向所有人俯下身子，他们越是穷、穿得越破，他似乎越高兴，并且感谢他们。
At times, however, sad sights are to be seen. A gentleman who had not come for a month because one of his sons had died, and who had sent a maidservant for the other, on returning yesterday and beholding the class, the comrades of his little dead boy, retired into a corner and burst into sobs, with both hands before his face, and the head-master took him by the arm and led him to his office.
但有时也会看到悲伤的情景。一位绅士有一个月没来了，因为他的一个儿子去世了，于是让女佣来接另一个。昨天他又来了，看着他死去的小儿子班上的同学们，就走到一个角落里，双手捂着脸哭了起来，而校长拉着他的胳膊，把他带到了办公室。
There are fathers and mothers who know all their sons' companions by name. There are girls from the neighboring schoolhouse, and scholars in the gymnasium, who come to wait for their brothers. There is one old gentleman who was a colonel formerly, and who, when a boy drops a copy-book or a pen, picks it up for him. There are also to be seen well-dressed men, who discuss school matters with others, who have kerchiefs on their heads, and baskets on their arm, and who say:— 
有些父母能记住他们儿子们的所有伙伴的名字。那里有一些隔壁学校的女孩，还有体育学校的学生，他们是来等他们的兄弟们的。有一位老绅士，他以前是上校，有孩子掉了字帖或钢笔时，他就帮着捡起来。还能见到一些衣着考究的人，他们手里拿着方巾，胳膊上挎着篮子，在跟别人讨论学校的课程，说：
"Oh! the problem has been a difficult one this time."— "That grammar lesson will never come to an end this morning!" 
“哦！这次的作业很难。”“今天上午的语法课没完没了！”
And when there is a sick boy in the class, they all know it; when a sick boy is convalescent, they all rejoice. And this morning there were eight or ten gentlemen and workingmen standing around Crossi's mother, the vegetable-vender, making inquiries about a poor baby in my brother's class, who lives in her court, and who is in danger of his life. The school seems to make them all equals and friends.
班上有孩子生病时，他们都知道；当一个生病的孩子康复时，他们都很高兴。今天早上有八到十个绅士和工人围站在克洛西卖菜的母亲的周围，询问我弟弟班上的一个可怜的小孩的情况，他住在她家的院子里，生命垂危了。学校似乎使他们变得平等，并变成了朋友。
