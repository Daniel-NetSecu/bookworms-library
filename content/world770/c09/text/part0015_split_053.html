THE LAST DAY OF THE CARNIVAL.  

狂欢节的最后一天  

Tuesday, 21st.

星期二，21日

What a sad scene was that which we witnessed Today at the procession of the masks! It ended well; but it might have resulted in a great misfortune. In the San Carlo Square, all decorated with red, white, and yellow festoons, a vast multitude had assembled; masks of every hue were flitting about; cars, gilded and adorned, in the shape of pavilions; little theatres, barks filled with harlequins and warriors, cooks, sailors, and shepherdesses; there was such a confusion that one knew not where to look; a tremendous clash of trumpets, horns, and cymbals lacerated the ears; and the masks on the chariots drank and sang, as they apostrophized the people in the streets and at the windows, who retorted at the top of their lungs, and hurled oranges and sugar-plums at each other vigorously; and above the chariots and the throng, as far as the eye could reach, one could see banners fluttering, helmets gleaming, plumes waving, gigantic pasteboard heads moving, huge head-dresses, enormous trumpets, fantastic arms, little drums, castanets, red caps, and bottles;—all the world seemed to have gone mad. When our carriage entered the square, a magnificent chariot was driving in front of us, drawn by four horses covered with trappings embroidered in gold, and all wreathed in artificial roses, upon which there were fourteen or fifteen gentlemen masquerading as gentlemen at the court of France, all glittering with silk, with huge white wigs, a plumed hat, under the arm a small-sword, and a tuft of ribbons and laces on the breast. They were very gorgeous. They were singing a French canzonette in concert and throwing sweetmeats to the people, and the people clapped their hands and shouted. Suddenly, on our left, we saw a man lift a child of five or six above the heads of the crowd,—a poor little creature, who wept piteously, and flung her arms about as though in a fit of convulsions. The man made his way to the gentlemen's chariot; one of the latter bent down, and the other said aloud:— 
我们在今天的面具游行上目睹的那一幕真是悲惨！它的结局不错，但是差一点儿就造成了巨大的不幸。圣卡洛广场上，一大群全身装饰着红色、白色和黄色花彩的人聚集在一起；各种颜色的面具晃来晃去；涂成金色的汽车被装饰成亭阁的样子；小剧场和游船里满是小丑、武士、厨师、水手和牧羊女；场面很混乱，大家都不知道该看哪里了；巨大的喇叭声、号角声和钹声震耳欲聋；敞篷马车上戴面具的人边喝酒边唱歌，还招呼着街头和窗边的人们，人们扯着嗓子回应着，还使劲地互相扔橘子和糖果；在马车和人群的上方，只要是目所能及的地方，人们就能看见飘扬的旗帜、闪亮的头盔、飞舞的羽毛、摇摇晃晃的纸糊的巨大头部、巨大的头饰、巨大的喇叭、奇特的胳膊、小小的鼓、响板、红色的帽子，还有瓶子——整个世界似乎都疯狂了。当我们的马车进入广场时，我们前面行驶着一辆四匹马拉的华丽的敞篷马车，马披着绣了金花的马饰，整辆车都被玫瑰绢花围了起来，上面有十四五位装扮成法国宫廷绅士的男人，都穿着闪闪发光的丝绸，戴着巨大的白色假发和饰有羽毛的帽子，胳膊下挂着一柄短剑，胸前戴着一簇缎带和蕾丝。他们非常华美。他们正合唱着一首法国小调，并向人们抛撒蜜饯，人群鼓掌欢呼。突然，在我们左边，我们看到一个人把一个五六岁的小孩举过人群头顶——那个可怜的小东西凄惨地哭着，她的胳膊像抽搐似的一阵乱舞。那个人走到绅士们的马车前，看到其中一个绅士弯下腰，他就大声说道：
"Take this child; she has lost her mother in the crowd; hold her in your arms; the mother may not be far off, and she will catch sight of her: there is no other way." 
“接着这个孩子，她在人群中跟母亲走散了，把她抱在你怀里，她的母亲可能就在这附近，会看见她的，没有别的办法了。”
The gentleman took the child in his arms: all the rest stopped singing; the child screamed and struggled; the gentleman removed his mask; the chariot continued to move slowly onwards. Meanwhile, as we were afterwards informed, at the opposite extremity of the square a poor woman, half crazed with despair, was forcing her way through the crowd, by dint of shoves and elbowing, and shrieking:— 
绅士把小孩抱在怀里，其他人也都停下来不再唱歌；小孩尖叫着、挣扎着；绅士摘掉面具，马车继续缓慢地前行。我们是后来听说的——与此同时，在广场另一端，一个绝望到快发疯的可怜的妇人拼命在人群中用手肘推来挤去，尖叫着：
"Maria! Maria! Maria! I have lost my little daughter! She has been stolen from me! They have suffocated my child!" And for a quarter of an hour she raved and expressed her despair in this manner, straying now a little way in this direction, and then a little way in that, crushed by the throng through which she strove to force her way.
“玛丽亚！玛丽亚！玛丽亚！我弄丢了我的小女儿！她被人拐走了！他们把我的孩子踩死了！”足有一刻钟的时间，她就这样绝望地胡言乱语，努力在人群中挤出一条路，却被人群挤得东倒西歪。
The gentleman on the car was meanwhile holding the child pressed against the ribbons and laces on his breast, casting glances over the square, and trying to calm the poor creature, who covered her face with her hands, not knowing where she was, and sobbed as though she would break her heart. The gentleman was touched: it was evident that these screams went to his soul. All the others offered the child oranges and sugar-plums; but she repulsed them all, and grew constantly more convulsed and frightened.
同时，车上的绅士把孩子紧紧抱在饰有缎带和蕾丝的胸前，张望着广场，并试着让这个可怜的小家伙镇定下来，而她用双手捂着脸，不知道自己在哪里，伤心地啜泣着。绅士被触动了，很明显，哭喊声打动了他的心。其他人给小孩拿来橘子和糖果，但她推开了他们所有人，变得更害怕，抽搐得更厉害了。
"Find her mother!" shouted the gentleman to the crowd; "seek her mother!" And every one turned to the right and the left; but the mother was not to be found. Finally, a few paces from the place where the Via Roma enters the square, a woman was seen to rush towards the chariot. Ah, I shall never forget that! She no longer seemed a human creature: her hair was streaming, her face distorted, her garments torn; she hurled herself forward with a rattle in her throat,—one knew not whether to attribute it to either joy, anguish, or rage,—and darted out her hands like two claws to snatch her child. The chariot halted.
“找找她的母亲！”绅士对人群喊道，“去找找她的母亲！”然后所有人都环顾四周，但是她的母亲还是没找到。最后，在距离维亚罗马街与广场交界处几步远的地方，人们看到一个妇人冲向了马车。啊，我永远也不会忘了那一幕！她已经不成人样了：她的头发一绺一绺的，脸部扭曲着，衣服也破了；她喉咙里叫了一声，往前冲过去——没有人知道这是出于喜悦、痛苦，还是盛怒——并伸出爪子一样的两手去夺她的孩子。马车停了下来。
"Here she is," said the gentleman, reaching out the child after kissing it; and he placed her in her mother's arms, who pressed her to her breast like a fury. But one of the tiny hands rested a second longer in the hands of the gentleman; and the latter, pulling off of his right hand a gold ring set with a large diamond, and slipping it with a rapid movement upon the finger of the little girl, said:— 
“给你。”绅士说着，亲了亲孩子然后递了过去，把孩子放在她母亲怀里，而那母亲像在生气似的一把把她紧搂在胸前。但一只小手在绅士掌中多留了一秒；后者取下他右手上一只镶着一颗大钻石的金戒指，迅速把它戴在小女孩的指头上，说：
"Take this; it shall be your marriage dowry." 
“拿着，当你的嫁妆吧。”
The mother stood rooted to the spot, as though enchanted; the crowd broke into applause; the gentleman put on his mask again, his companions resumed their song, and the chariot started on again slowly, amid a tempest of hand-clapping and hurrahs.
那位母亲像被施了魔法一样，一动不动地站在原地；人群爆发出一阵掌声；绅士又戴上了面具，他的伙伴们继续唱他们的歌，马车在一阵暴风雨般的掌声和叫好声中又开始慢慢前行。
