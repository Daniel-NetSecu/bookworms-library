Jane Austen (1775—1817) was born in Steventon, Hampshire, England. Her education was mainly conducted within her family. She wrote six novels in her life. They center on the lives of young women of the middle class, and every novel ends with a happy marriage or two. As an unmarried woman of very modest financial means, Jane Austen understood the hopes and fears of women who had to rely on marriage and family connections to provide them with a home and means to live.
