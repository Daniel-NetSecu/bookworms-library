M-O

A tiny, obsessive-compulsive maintenance robot with a bristle-brush head who kept Axiom clean. When M-O met WALL·E and saw how filthy he was, he deviated from his normal routine and followed WALL·E, cleaning up behind him and accidentally saving him and EVE.


莫是一个有强迫症的微型维护机器人。他的脑袋是一把鬃刷，能让“功理”号保持清洁。当他遇上瓦力并发现他实在太脏，莫偏离了他的常规工作路线，一路跟着瓦力打扫他留下的印迹，机缘巧合之下还救了瓦力和伊芙。
