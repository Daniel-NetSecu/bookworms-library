The Cat and the Cock

A Cat caught a Cock, and pondered how he might find a reasonable excuse for eating him. He accused him of being nuisance to men by crowing in the night time and not per-mitting them to sleep. The Cock defended himself by saying that he did this for the benefit of men that they might rise in time for their labours. The Cat replied, ‘Although you abound in specious apologies, I shall not remain supperless’; and he made a meal of him.
