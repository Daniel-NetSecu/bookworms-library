MY FATHER.  

我的父亲  

Saturday, 17th.

星期六，17日

Surely, neither your comrade Coretti nor Garrone would ever have answered their fathers as you answered yours this afternoon. Enrico! How is it possible? You must promise me solemnly that this shall never happen again so long as I live. Every time that an impertinent reply flies to your lips at a reproof from your father, think of that day which will infallibly come when he will call you to his bedside to tell you, "Enrico, I am about to leave you." Oh, my son, when you hear his voice for the last time, and for a long while afterwards, when you weep alone in his deserted room, in the midst of those books which he will never open again, then, on recalling that you have at times been wanting in respect to him, you, too, will ask yourself, "How is it possible?" Then you will understand that he has always been your best friend, that when he was constrained to punish you, it caused him more suffering than it did you, and that he never made you weep except for the sake of doing you good; and then you will repent, and you will kiss with tears that desk at which he worked so much, at which he wore out his life for his children. You do not understand now; he hides from you all of himself except his kindness and his love. You do not know that he is sometimes so broken down with toil that he thinks he has only a few more days to live, and that at such moments he talks only of you; he has in his heart no other trouble than that of leaving you poor and without protection.
无疑，无论是你的朋友可莱谛还是卡隆，都绝不会像你今天下午那样回答父亲的话。安利柯！怎么会这样？你必须郑重地向我许诺，在我有生之年不会再发生这样的事情。每当你要对父亲的责备进行无礼反驳时，想想将来终有那么一天，你的父亲会把你叫到床前，对你说：“安利柯，我就要离开你了。”啊，我的儿子，在你最后一次聆听他的声音时，当你在他去世许久之后独自在他空荡的房间里哭泣，在那些他再也不会翻开的书籍中哭泣时，回想以前对父亲不敬，你也会质问自己：“怎么会这样？”那时你会明白父亲一直是自己最好的朋友，当他被迫惩罚你时，他比你还痛苦，要不是为了你好，他从不会使你哭泣。到那时，你会感到悔恨，会含泪亲吻父亲的书桌。在那张书桌边上，他曾伏案辛勤工作，曾为子女耗尽精力。你现在不会明白，他把自己的一切都隐藏起来，只留给你他的善良和爱。你不知道，他有时因为劳累过度，以为自己时日无多。每当此时，他只谈论你，他的心里只担心留你一个人在世上贫穷困苦、无依无靠。
And how often, when meditating on this, does he enter your chamber while you are asleep, and stand there, lamp in hand, gazing at you; and then he makes an effort, and weary and sad as he is, he returns to his labor; and neither do you know that he often seeks you and remains with you because he has a bitterness in his heart, sorrows which attack all men in the world, and he seeks you as a friend, to obtain consolation himself and forgetfulness, and he feels the need of taking refuge in your affection, to recover his serenity and his courage: think, then, what must be his sorrow, when instead of finding in you affection, he finds coldness and disrespect! Never again stain yourself with this horrible ingratitude! Reflect, that were you as good as a saint, you could never repay him sufficiently for what he has done and for what he is constantly doing for you. And reflect, also, we cannot count on life; a misfortune might remove your father while you are still a boy,—in two years, in three months, Tomorrow.
每每想到这里，他就会在你睡着时走进你的房间，手中提着灯，站在那里注视着你。尽管疲惫而伤心，他还是努力回去继续工作。你也不知道，父亲经常寻找你并呆在你的身边是因为他心中有着所有世人都会遭受的痛苦。他把你当成一个朋友，在你那里寻求安慰和遗忘，从你的爱中获得庇护，重获平静和勇气。想一想，如果父亲在你身上看不到爱，只看到冰冷和不敬，他该有多么悲伤啊！再也不要让这可怕的忘恩负义玷污了你自己！想一想，即便你像个圣人一样高尚，你也永远无法还清父亲一直以来不断的付出。再想一想，人生多变，不幸可能会在你还是孩子的时候就夺走你的父亲——也有可能是两年以后、三个月以后，或是明天。
Ah, my poor Enrico, when you see all about you changing, how empty, how desolate the house will appear, with your poor mother clothed in black! Go, my son, go to your father; he is in his room at work; go on tiptoe, so that he may not hear you enter; go and lay your forehead on his knees, and beseech him to pardon and to bless you.
啊，我可怜的安利柯，你会看见自己周围的一切都发生了改变，你可怜的母亲身着黑衣为你的父亲哀悼，家中将会变得多么空旷和凄凉！去吧，我的儿子，去父亲那里。他正在自己的房间里工作。踮起脚尖轻轻地走，这样他就听不到你进去的声音。去把头靠在父亲的膝上，请求他的原谅和祝福吧。
Thy Mother.
你的母亲
