Sadness

● Sadness has always been a “glass half-empty” kind of emotion. She’d love to be more optimistic, but it’s hard to stay positive when the world is so full of misery: she’s seen the deaths of three pet goldfish and the loss of a beloved purple troll doll; and she’s seen ice cream scoops fall to the ground for no good reason... Sometimes, Riley’s life becomes so grim, she has no choice but to collapse into a puddle of sorrow. It’s a painful job, but Sadness perseveres.
