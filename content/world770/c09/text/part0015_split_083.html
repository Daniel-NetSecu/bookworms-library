SUMMER.  

夏  

Wednesday, 24th.

星期三，24日

Marco, the Genoese, is the last little hero but one whose acquaintance we shall make this year; only one remains for the month of June. There are only two more monthly examinations, twenty-six days of lessons, six Thursdays, and five Sundays. The air of the end of the year is already perceptible. The trees of the garden, leafy and in blossom, cast a fine shade on the gymnastic apparatus. The scholars are already dressed in summer clothes. And it is beautiful, at the close of school and the exit of the classes, to see how different everything is from what it was in the months that are past. The long locks which touched the shoulders have disappeared; all heads are closely shorn; bare legs and throats are to be seen; little straw hats of every shape, with ribbons that descend even on the backs of the wearers; shirts and neckties of every hue; all the little children with something red or blue about them, a facing, a border, a tassel, a scrap of some vivid color tacked on somewhere by the mother, so that even the poorest may make a good figure; and many come to school without any hats, as though they had run away from home. Some wear the white gymnasium suit. There is one of Schoolmistress Delcati's boys who is red from head to foot, like a boiled crab. Several are dressed like sailors.
热那亚的马可是我们本学年要学习的最后一位小英雄，之后只剩下六月的每月故事。还有两次月考，二十六天课，六个星期四和五个星期天。人们已经可以感觉到一学年即将结束的气氛。庭院里的树枝叶茂盛、欣欣向荣，在体操器械上投下大块阴凉。学生们已经穿上了夏装。放学和课间时，可以看见一切事物都与过去几个月里不同，变得非常美好。垂在肩上的长发不见了，大家都把头发剪短，露出了腿和脖子，头戴各式各样的麦秸帽子，帽子后面还飘着长及背部的丝带。大家身穿五颜六色的衬衫，系着各种颜色的领带。小孩子身上都有些红色或蓝色的东西，或是领章，或是饰边，或是流苏，或是母亲钉在某处的什么色彩鲜艳的东西，即使是贫家的孩子看起来也很漂亮。许多孩子不戴帽子来上学，就像是从家里逃出来的一样。有些孩子穿着白色的体操服。代尔卡谛老师班上的一个孩子从头到脚穿了一身红，就像个煮熟的螃蟹。还有几个孩子穿着水手服。
But the finest of all is the little mason, who has donned a big straw hat, which gives him the appearance of a half-candle with a shade over it; and it is ridiculous to see him make his hare's face beneath it. Coretti, too, has abandoned his catskin cap, and wears an old travelling-cap of gray silk. Votini has a sort of Scotch dress, all decorated; Crossi displays his bare breast; Precossi is lost inside of a blue blouse belonging to the blacksmith-ironmonger.
但众人之中，要数小石匠穿得最精细了。他戴着一顶大草帽，就像是顶着笠罩的半截蜡烛，在帽子下面做着兔脸，看起来可笑极了。可莱谛也摘下了猫皮帽子，换上一顶灰色丝绸的旧旅行帽。华梯尼身着一种带有许多装饰物的苏格兰服饰，克洛西裸露着胸膛，泼来可西则穿着一件非常大的铁匠的蓝色的上衣。
And Garoffi? Now that he has been obliged to discard the cloak beneath which he concealed his wares, all his pockets are visible, bulging with all sorts of huckster's trifles, and the lists of his lotteries force themselves out. Now all his pockets allow their contents to be seen,—fans made of half a newspaper, knobs of canes, darts to fire at birds, herbs, and maybugs which creep out of his pockets and crawl gradually over the jackets.
那么，卡洛斐呢？现在他不得不脱下那什么都可以藏的斗篷，将所有口袋都露在了外面，口袋里鼓鼓地装满了各种小贩的东西，还露出半截彩票单子。如今从外面就可以看出他口袋里装的东西——半张报纸做的扇子、手杖柄、打鸟的镖、各种药草，甚至还有金甲虫从口袋里爬出来，慢慢地爬过他的夹克衫。
Many of the little fellows carry bunches of flowers to the mistresses. The mistresses are dressed in summer garments also, of cheerful tints; all except the "little nun," who is always in black; and the mistress with the red feather still has her red feather, and a knot of red ribbon at her neck, all tumbled with the little paws of her scholars, who always make her laugh and flee.
许多低年级的小孩子送花束给女老师们。她们也都穿上了颜色艳丽的夏装，只有那个总是身着黑衣的“小修女”是个例外。戴红羽毛的老师依然戴着红羽毛，脖子上还打着一个红丝带结，羽毛和丝带结被孩子们的小手扯得歪歪扭扭，她总是笑着躲开他们。
It is the season, too, of cherry-trees, of butterflies, of music in the streets, and of rambles in the country; many of the fourth grade run away to bathe in the Po; all have their hearts already set on the vacation; each day they issue forth from school more impatient and content than the day before. Only it pains me to see Garrone in mourning, and my poor mistress of the primary, who is thinner and whiter than ever, and who coughs with ever-increasing violence. She walks all bent over now, and salutes me so sadly!
现在也是樱桃、蝴蝶、街边乐队和乡下漫步的季节。许多五年级的孩子到波河里洗澡，他们一心想着假期，每天放学时都变得比前一天更加急切和满足。只是看见正在服丧的卡隆和我那可怜的二年级女老师令我很伤心。她日渐消瘦而苍白，咳嗽愈发严重了。现在她完全弓着腰走路，悲伤地同我打招呼！
