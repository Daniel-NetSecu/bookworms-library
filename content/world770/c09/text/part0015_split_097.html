THE EXAMINATIONS.  

考试  

Tuesday, 4th.

星期二，4日

Here are the examinations at last! Nothing else is to be heard under discussion, in the streets in the vicinity of the school, from boys, fathers, mothers, and even tutors; examinations, points, themes, averages, dismissals, promotions: all utter the same words. Yesterday morning there was composition; this morning there is arithmetic. It was touching to see all the parents, as they conducted their sons to school, giving them their last advice in the street, and many mothers accompanied their sons to their seats, to see whether the inkstand was filled, and to try their pens, and they still continued to hover round the entrance, and to say: 
考试终于来了。在学校附近的街上，学生、家长，甚至是老师都不谈论别的话题。考试、分数、题目、平均分、留级和升级：这些是他们讨论的全部。昨天早上考过作文，今早考算术。看见这情景真令人感动：家长们送孩子去学校，在街上最后嘱咐着他们；许多母亲陪着孩子走到座位上，去检查墨水瓶是否灌满，还试验钢笔是否好用，之后仍然继续在门口徘徊，说：
"Courage! Attention! I entreat you." 
“加油！要仔细！拜托你要用心啊！”
Our assistant-master was Coatti, the one with the black beard, who mimics the voice of a lion, and never punishes any one. There were boys who were white with fear. When the master broke the seal of the letter from the town-hall, and drew out the problem, not a breath was audible. He announced the problem loudly, staring now at one, now at another, with terrible eyes; but we understood that had he been able to announce the answer also, so that we might all get promoted, he would have been delighted.
留着黑色胡须的科伊蒂先生是我们的监考。他有着雄狮一般的声音，却从不惩罚任何人。有的孩子紧张得脸色苍白。当老师把市政厅送来的信封拆开并抽出题目时，全场都屏住了呼吸。他大声地宣读题目，用严厉的眼神一会儿看看这个，一会儿看看那个。但我们知道，要是他可以将答案一起念出，让大家都能升级，他会很高兴那么做的。
After an hour of work many began to grow weary, for the problem was difficult. One cried. Crossi dealt himself blows on the head. And many of them are not to blame, poor boys, for not knowing, for they have not had much time to study, and have been neglected by their parents. But Providence was at hand. You should have seen Derossi, and what trouble he took to help them; how ingenious he was in getting a figure passed on, and in suggesting an operation, without allowing himself to be caught; so anxious for all that he appeared to be our teacher himself. Garrone, too, who is strong in arithmetic, helped all he could; and he even assisted Nobis, who, finding himself in a quandary, was quite gentle.
一个小时之后，许多孩子开始感到疲倦，因为题目很难。一个孩子哭了。克洛西敲着头。有很多人做不出来是正常的，可怜的孩子们，因为他们受教时间短，父母也未曾教导过他们。好在天无绝人之路。你应该看看代洛西是费了多大的力气来帮助他们的。他背着老师机敏地传递数字、提示和运算。他是如此地紧张，仿佛他就是我们的老师一样。擅长算术的卡隆也尽力帮忙，他甚至帮助了骄傲的诺琵斯，后者今天也陷入窘境，变得非常温和。
Stardi remained motionless for more than an hour, with his eyes on the problem, and his fists on his temples, and then he finished the whole thing in five minutes. The master made his round among the benches, saying:— 
斯带地一动不动地呆了一个多小时，眼睛注视着题目，拳头抵着太阳穴，之后用了五分钟就完成了所有题目。老师在桌间巡视，说：
"Be calm! Be calm! I advise you to be calm!" 
“镇定！镇定！我建议大家镇定！”
And when he saw that any one was discouraged, he opened his mouth, as though about to devour him, in imitation of a lion, in order to make him laugh and inspire him with courage. Toward eleven o'clock, peeping down through the blinds, I perceived many parents pacing the street in their impatience. There was Precossi's father, in his blue blouse, who had deserted his shop, with his face still quite black. There was Crossi's mother, the vegetable-vender; and Nelli's mother, dressed in black, who could not stand still.
每当看到有学生气馁，他就像狮子一样张开嘴，仿佛要吞掉孩子似的，好使孩子发笑并重拾勇气。时近十一点钟，我透过百叶窗看见许多家长焦急地在街上走来走去。泼来可西的父亲丢下店铺赶了过来，穿着蓝色的工作服，脸上仍旧黑黑的。克洛西那卖菜的母亲也在。还有内利的母亲，她身着黑衣，不停地徘徊着。
A little before mid-day, my father arrived and raised his eyes to my window; my dear father! At noon we had all finished. And it was a sight at the close of school! Every one ran to meet the boys, to ask questions, to turn over the leaves of the copy-books to compare them with the work of their comrades.
将近正午的时候，我父亲也来了。他抬眼看着我教室的窗户，我亲爱的父亲！考试于正午结束。放学时的情景真是好看！家长们都跑到自己孩子那里，询问着情况，翻看着字帖，将他们的功课同其他孩子的作比较。
"How many operations? What is the total? And subtraction? And the answer? And the punctuation of decimals?" 
“一共有几个问题？加法有多少？减法有多少？答案是什么？小数点呢？”
All the masters were running about hither and thither, summoned in a hundred directions.
老师们被一大群人呼唤着，跑来跑去。
My father instantly took from my hand the rough copy, looked at it, and said, "That's well." 
父亲立刻从我手中拿过草稿看了看，说：“不错。”
Beside us was the blacksmith, Precossi, who was also inspecting his son's work, but rather uneasily, and not comprehending it. He turned to my father:— 
铁匠泼来可西在我们的旁边，也检查着儿子的笔记，但他非常焦急，似乎有不明白的地方。他转向父亲：
"Will you do me the favor to tell me the total?" 
“能帮忙告诉我这个总和是多少吗？”
My father read the number. The other gazed and reckoned. "Brave little one!" he exclaimed, in perfect content. And my father and he gazed at each other for a moment with a kindly smile, like two friends. My father offered his hand, and the other shook it; and they parted, saying, "Farewell until the oral examination." 
父亲念出数字。铁匠看着儿子的答案核对着。“好样的小家伙！”他喊道，非常满足。父亲和他相互对视了一下，像朋友一样友好地互相微笑。父亲伸出手去，铁匠握了父亲的手。两人分别时说：“口试再见。”
"Until the oral examination." 
“口试再见。”
After proceeding a few paces, we heard a falsetto voice which made us turn our heads. It was the blacksmith-ironmonger singing.
我们刚往前走了几步，就听见一声男高音，于是回头去看。原来是铁匠在唱歌。
