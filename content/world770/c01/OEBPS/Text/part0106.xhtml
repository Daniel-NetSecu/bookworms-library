LESSON 101

rain outside often pitte
say window sound patter
drops sometimes only music

I wish, Mamma you would tell me where the rain comes from. Does it come from the sky? And when the little drops pitter-patter on the window do you think they are playing with me? I can not work or read, for I love to listen to them. I often think their sound is pretty music. But the rain keeps children at home and sometimes I do not like that, then.

The little raindrops only say,“Pit, pitter, patter, pat;While we play on the outside,Why can't you play on that?”

【中文阅读】

妈妈，我希望你能告诉我雨是从哪里来的。是来自天空吗? 当小雨滴噼哒啪嗒地敲打窗户时，你认为他们是在跟我玩吗？我无法学习，无法看书，因为我喜欢听雨滴声。我经常认为，他们的声音非常像音乐。但是雨也将孩子们留在家中，有时候我也不喜欢。

小雨滴只说道：“噼哒噼哒，啪嗒啪嗒；我们在外面玩，你为什么不能在外面玩？”
