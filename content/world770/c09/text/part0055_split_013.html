CHAPTER XIV  

第十四章  

We must, however, say a few words about Sanin himself.
然而，关于萨宁本人，我们也该说上几句。
In the first place, he was very, very good-looking. A handsome, graceful figure, agreeable, rather unformed features, kindly bluish eyes, golden hair, a clear white and red skin, and, above all, that peculiar, naively-cheerful, confiding, open, at the first glance, somewhat foolish expression, by which in former days one could recognise directly the children of steady-going, noble families, "sons of their father" fine young landowners, born and reared in our open, half-wild country parts,—a hesitating gait, a voice with a lisp, a smile like a child's the minute you looked at him... lastly, freshness, health, softness, softness, softness—there you have the whole of Sanin. And secondly, he was not stupid and had picked up a fair amount of knowledge. Fresh he had remained, for all his foreign tour; the disturbing emotions in which the greater part of the young people of that day were tempest-tossed were very little known to him.
首先，他长得非常非常帅气。他相貌堂堂，体态优雅，让人喜爱。脸部轮廓不大分明，有一双温柔的浅蓝色眼睛，还有金黄色的头发，肤色白里透红。更值得一提的是：他给人的第一印象是有着特别的、纯真、快乐、自信而坦率的性格，还带点儿傻气。从前凭这种神态就不难认出，这一定是门第高贵的贵族公子、“世家”子弟，或是在俄国广阔的半草原地带长大的少爷。他走起路来步态迟疑，说话有点口齿不清，你只要看他一眼，他便会报以孩童般纯真的微笑……总之，他富有朝气，身体健康，温和、安静而温柔——这就是您所看到的萨宁。其次，他并不愚笨，还有些学问。在国外的游历让他精神饱满，在他的身上一点儿也找不到当时大部分青年的那种忧国忧民的心情。
Of late years, in response to the assiduous search for "new types", young men have begun to appear in our literature, determined at all hazards to be "fresh"... as fresh as Flensburg oysters, when they reach Petersburg... Sanin was not like them. Since we have had recourse already to simile, he rather recalled a young, leafy, freshly-grafted apple-tree in one of our fertile orchards—or better still, a well-groomed, sleek, sturdy-limbed, tender young three-year-old in some old-fashioned seignorial stud stable, a young horse that they have hardly begun to break in to the traces... Those who came across Sanin in later years, when life had knocked him about a good deal, and the sleekness and plumpness of youth had long vanished, saw in him a totally different man.
近些年来，文学界在努力搜寻一些“新形象”，新青年们已经开始在我们的文学作品中出现，不惜一切地想要“新鲜”……就像从弗伦斯堡运往彼得堡的牡蛎那样新鲜……萨宁和他们不一样。打个比方，他能让我们想起肥沃的果园里不久前嫁接过的一株枝叶繁茂的苹果树，甚至更恰当点，像那“贵族老爷”的养马场里精心洗刷过、毛色光滑、四腿粗壮、刚开始套上调马索调教的娇嫩的三岁小马驹……那些后来见过萨宁的人，都感觉他与当初是判若两人。因为经过了生活的磨砺，青年人的圆润与丰满已经消失殆尽了。
Next day Sanin was still in bed when Emil, in his best clothes, with a cane in his hand and much pomade on his head, burst into his room, announcing that Herr Kléber would be here directly with the carriage, that the weather promised to be exquisite, that they had everything ready by now, but that mamma was not going, as her head was bad again. He began to hurry Sanin, telling him that there was not a minute to lose... And Herr Kléber did, in fact, find Sanin still at his toilet. He knocked at the door, came in, bowed with a bend from the waist, expressed his readiness to wait as long as might be desired, and sat down, his hat balanced elegantly on his knees. The handsome shop-manager had got himself up and perfumed himself to excess: his every action was accompanied by a powerful whiff of the most refined aroma. He arrived in a comfortable open carriage—one of the kind called landau—drawn by two tall and powerful but not well-shaped horses. A quarter of an hour later Sanin, Klüber, and Emil, in this same carriage, drew up triumphantly at the steps of the confectioner's shop. Madame Roselli resolutely refused to join the party; Gemma wanted to stay with her mother; but she simply turned her out.
第二天，萨宁还躺在床上时，埃米尔就穿着最新的衣服，手里拿着一根手杖，头发上擦了很多润发油，突然跑进来，说克吕贝尔先生雇了辆马车马上就要来了。天气格外晴朗，他们已经准备好了一切，但是他妈妈不去了，因为头疼病又犯了。他开始催促萨宁，说一分钟都不能耽搁了……果然，当克吕贝尔来时，萨宁还在换衣服。他敲了敲门走了进来，弯着腰鞠了个躬，表示等多久他都乐意，然后坐了下来，优雅地把帽子搁在膝盖上。这个仪表堂堂的店主精心打扮了一番，身上喷足了香水，因此他的每一个动作都会散发出一阵阵浓郁的精品香水味。他坐了一辆敞篷式大马车——所谓的活顶四轮马车，拉车的那两匹马虽说不上漂亮，却高大而健壮。一刻钟后，萨宁、克吕贝尔和埃米尔坐在这辆敞篷大车里，兴高采烈地来到了糖果店的台阶前。罗塞利太太坚持不去郊游；吉玛想和她妈妈一起呆着，但是她母亲还是把她撵出来了。
"I don't want any one," she declared; "I shall go to sleep. I would send Pantaleone with you too, only there would be no one to mind the shop." 
“我不用任何人陪我，”她坚决地说道，“我想睡觉。我本想让潘塔莱昂纳跟你们一起去的，可是，店里没人照料。”
"May we take Tartaglia?" asked Emil.
“我们能带上塔尔塔利阿吗？”埃米尔问道。
"Of course you may." 
“你们当然可以带它一起去。”
Tartaglia immediately scrambled, with delighted struggles, on to the box and sat there, licking himself; it was obviously a thing he was accustomed to. Gemma put on a large straw hat with brown ribbons; the hat was bent down in front, so as to shade almost the whole of her face from the sun. The line of shadow stopped just at her lips; they wore a tender maiden flush, like the petals of a centifoil rose, and her teeth gleamed stealthily—innocently too, as when children smile. Gemma sat facing the horses, with Sanin; Kléber and Emil sat opposite. The pale face of Frau Lenore appeared at the window; Gemma waved her handkerchief to her, and the horses started.
塔尔塔利阿立刻爬上了马车前面的箱子——有点儿费劲但是很欢快，坐在那儿，舔起自己来，显然这是它惯常的动作。吉玛戴着一顶褐色绸带的大草帽，草帽的前帽檐稍微垂下，几乎遮住了照在她脸上的阳光。帽檐的阴影正好落在了她的唇上。她的唇有少女的红润，像百叶玫瑰的花瓣；她的牙齿，就像小孩子微笑时一样，也隐隐地闪着微光，那么天真。吉玛和萨宁面朝着马坐着，克吕贝尔和埃米尔坐他们对面。莱诺雷太太面色苍白地出现在窗子上，吉玛向她妈妈挥挥手帕，马就跑了起来。
