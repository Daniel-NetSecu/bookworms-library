The Awakening  

觉醒  

During the three years that she had been married, she had not left the Val de Ciré, where her husband possessed two cotton-mills. She led a quiet life, and although she had no children, she was quite happy in her house among the trees, which the work-people called the chateau.
在婚后的这三年里，她从没离开过西雷山谷。在那儿，他的丈夫拥有两家棉纺厂。她虽然没有孩子，却过着平静的日子。住在那座掩映在树林里，被工人们称为“城堡”的房子里，她过得十分幸福。
Although Monsieur Vasseur was considerably older than she was, he was very kind. She loved him, and no guilty thought had ever entered her mind.
瓦瑟尔先生的年纪比她大得多，但是却非常的善良。她爱他，心中从未产生过任何邪念。
Her mother came and spent every summer at Ciré, and then returned to Paris for the winter, as soon as the leaves began to fall.
她母亲每年都会来西雷度过夏天，到树木开始落叶的时候再返回巴黎过冬。
Jeanne coughed a little every autumn, for the narrow valley through which the river wound, grew foggy for five months. First of all, slight mists hung over the meadows, making all the low-lying ground look like a large pond, out of which the roof of the houses rose.
让娜每年秋天总会有点咳嗽，因为狭窄的山谷里有一条弯弯曲曲的小河，这使得秋天里连续五个月都雾气弥漫。首先，薄雾漂浮在草地上，使得所有洼地看上去像一片大池塘，一个个屋顶从中冒了出来。
Then that white vapor, which rose like a tide, enveloped everything, and turned the valley into a land of phantoms, through which men moved about like ghosts, without recognizing each other ten yards off, and the trees, wreathed in mist, and dripping with moisture, rose up through it.
随后这片白色的水汽像潮汐似的升腾起来，把一切都笼罩住，把山谷变成了鬼怪的世界。人们像幽灵似地穿梭在雾气中，相隔十码也互相认不出对方；树木被雾气环绕着，潮湿得快要滴水，穿过雾气向上生长。
But the people who went along the neighboring hills, and who looked down upon the deep, white depression of the valley, saw the two huge chimneys of Monsieur Vasseur's factories, rising above the mist below. Day and night they vomited forth two long trails of black smoke, and that alone indicated that people were living in that hollow, which looked as if it were filled with a cloud of cotton.
可是那些从附近山坡上经过，低头看向这个深深的白茫茫的山谷的人们，都会看到瓦瑟尔先生工厂里的两支巨大的烟囱高耸在薄雾之上。日日夜夜，这两支烟囱喷吐着两条黑色的烟龙。只有这说明了这个仿佛堆满了云团的山谷里有人居住。
That year, when October came, the medical men advised the young woman to go and spend the winter in Paris with her mother, as the air of the valley was dangerous for her weak chest, and she went. For a month or so, she thought continually of the house which she had left, to which she seemed rooted, and whose well-known furniture and quiet ways she loved so much, but by degrees she grew accustomed to her new life, and got to liking entertainments, dinners and evening parties, and balls.
那一年，当十月来临的时候，医生建议这位少妇去巴黎和她母亲一起过冬，因为山谷的空气对她原本虚弱的肺很危险了，于是她去了巴黎。开始的一个月左右，她不停地思念着那个她离开的家，那里好似有她的根，有她熟悉的家具和她喜欢的平静的节奏。但是渐渐地，她适应了新的生活，开始喜欢上了各种娱乐活动、晚宴、晚会和舞会。
Till then, she had retained her girlish manners, she had been undecided and rather sluggish; she walked languidly, and had a tired smile, but now she became animated and merry, and was always ready for pleasure. Men paid her marked attentions, and she was amused at their talk, and made fun of their gallantries, as she felt sure that she could resist them, for she was rather disgusted with love, from what she had learned of it in marriage.
在那以前，她一直保持着她少女般的举止态度。她曾经是那么的犹豫不决甚至是反应迟钝，走起路来疲倦无力，就连笑容都充满着倦意。但是现在她变得鲜活起来而且精神愉悦，随时随地准备好参加各种娱乐活动。男人们向她献殷勤，她被他们的谈话偷笑，拿他们的甜言蜜语开玩笑，因为她深信自己能抵制住他们的诱惑，因为她对爱情感到反感，这一点是她从婚后生活中学到的。
The idea of giving up her body to the coarse caresses of such bearded creatures, made her laugh with pity, and shudder a little with ignorance.
一种把自己的身子交给那些满脸大胡子的人，被他们粗鲁地爱抚的想法使得她由于怜悯而情不自禁地笑了出来，并且因感到无知而微微颤抖。
She asked herself how women could consent to those degrading contacts with strangers, as they were already obliged to endure them with their legitimate husbands. She would have loved her husband much more if they had lived together like two friends, and had restricted themselves to chaste kisses, which are the caresses of the soul.
她问自己，女人怎么会同意和陌生人进行这些可耻的接触，因为这些接触只能产生在她和她的合法的丈夫之间。如果她和她的丈夫就像朋友那样生活在一起，仅限于一些抚慰灵魂的纯洁的吻，那么她也许会更爱她的丈夫。
But she was much amused by their compliments, by the desire which showed itself in their eyes, and which she did not share, by their declarations of love, which they whispered into her ear as they were returning to the drawing-room after some grand dinner, by their words, which were murmured so low that she almost had to guess them, and which left her blood quite cool, and her heart untouched, while they gratified her unconscious coquetry, while they kindled a flame of pleasure within her, and while they made her lips open, her eyes glow bright, and her woman's heart, to which homage was due, quiver with delight.
但是她对恭维的话，对他们眼中流露出的而她却没有的欲望；对当他们吃过精美的晚餐回到客厅时到她耳边小声说出的爱情表白；对他们含含糊糊、声音轻得要靠猜测其意思的话语都感到非常有趣。这些话语不会使她皮肤发热、心跳加快，而是迎合了她没有觉察到的征服感，点燃了她心中快乐的火花，使得她的嘴角浮现笑意；使得她的眼睛熠熠生辉；使得她那颗受到爱慕的女人的心开心得微微颤抖。
She was fond of those tête-à-têtes when it was getting dusk, when a man grows pressing, stammers, trembles and falls on his knees. It was a delicious and new pleasure to her to know that they felt that passion which left her quite unmoved, to say no, by a shake of the head, and with her lips, to withdraw her hands, to get up and calmly ring for lights, and to see the man who had been trembling at her feet, get up, confused and furious when he heard the footman coming.
她喜欢天色暗下来后客厅火炉旁的单独谈话，这时那个男人变得很紧张、讲话结结巴巴，全身哆嗦然后跪倒在地上。她不会被这种感觉触动。她会摇头，用嘴的动作表示拒绝，缩回双手、站起来、平静地按铃吩咐点灯，看到那个在她脚下发抖的男人在听到仆人走来时满脸疑惑、气急败坏地站起来，这对她来说是一种新的有趣的事。
She often had a hard laugh, which froze the most burning words, and said harsh things, which fell like a jet of icy water on the most ardent protestations, while the intonations of her voice were enough to make any man who really loved her, kill himself, and there were two especially who made obstinate love to her, although they did not at all resemble one another.
她经常发出一种干笑声，这种笑声能冻结火热的情话；她经常说一些无情的话，这些话会像冰水一样喷到炙热保证上；她说话的语调足以使那些真正爱着她的男人去寻死。有两个年轻人执着地追求她，虽然他们是完全不一样的人。
One of them, Paul Péronel, was a tall man of the world, gallant and enterprising, a man who was accustomed to successful love affairs, and who knew how to wait, and when to seize his opportunity.
其中一位是保罗·佩罗内尔先生，他是个高个子的青年，英勇帅气、有上进心，在情场上很得意，懂得如何等待，懂得何时把握时机。
The other, Monsieur d'Avancelle, quivered when he came near her, scarcely ventured to express his love, but followed her like a shadow, and gave utterance to his hopeless desire by distracted looks, and the assiduity of his attentions to her, and she made him a kind of slave who followed her steps, and whom she treated as if he had been her servant.
另一位是德·阿旺塞先生。这个人在靠近她时便会瑟缩发抖，几乎不敢冒险让她知道他心中的爱情，可是却像个影子一样追随着她，用他那心烦意乱的目光和对她时时刻刻的关心来诉说他心中怀有的那一线几乎没有可能的渴望。她把他变成了她的奴隶，像仆人一样地使唤他。
She would have been much amused if anybody had told her that she would love him, and yet she did love him, after a singular fashion. As she saw him continually, she had grown accustomed to his voice, to his gestures, and to his manner, as one grows accustomed to those with whom one meets continually. Often his face haunted her in her dreams, and she saw him as he really was; gentle, delicate in all his actions, humble, but passionately in love, and she awoke full of those dreams, fancying that she still heard him, and felt him near her, until one night (most likely she was feverish), she saw herself alone with him in a small wood, where they were both of them sitting on the grass. He was saying charming things to her, while he pressed and kissed her hands.
如果有人告诉她也许她会爱上他，她一定会觉得很好笑。可是她的确爱上了他，在唯一一次时装会后。因为她不断地看到他，对他的声音、姿态和行为举止都已经习惯了，就像人们会习惯始终见面的人一样。他的脸经常出现在她的梦中。她见到的脸就像现实生活中的那张脸一样，温柔、清秀、谦卑、不失热情。她醒来后满脑子还是这些梦境，想象着她听到了他的声音，感觉他就在她身边。直到一天夜里（很可能她在发烧），她看到自己和他两个人，一起坐在小树林的草地上。他对她说着迷人的事情，一面紧握着她的手吻着。
She could feel the warmth of his skin and of his breath, and she was stroking his hair, in a very natural manner.
她能感受到他肌肤的温度和他呼出的气息，她则很自然地抚摸着他的头发。
We are quite different in our dreams to what we are in real life. She felt full of love for him, full of calm and deep love, and was happy in stroking his forehead and in holding him against her. Gradually he put his arms round her, kissed her eyes and her cheeks without her attempting to get away from him; their lips met, and she yielded.
我们在梦中和现实生活中是完全不一样的。她感到自己对他的满满的爱意，是一种宁静而深厚的爱意。当她触摸他的前额并抱着他的时候她感到很幸福。渐渐地，他抱着她，吻她的眼和脸颊，而她也毫不挣扎，他们的嘴唇贴在了一起，她完全被征服了。
When she saw him again, unconscious of the agitation that he had caused her, she felt that she grew red, and while he was telling her of his love, she was continually recalling to mind their previous meeting, without being able to get rid of the recollection.
当她再次见到他，她感到自己的脸红了，没有意识到他已经扰乱了她平静的内心。当他对她吐露爱慕之情的时候，她不断地回忆起他们之前的约会，怎么也甩不了这些念头。
She loved him, loved him with refined tenderness, which arose chiefly from the remembrance of her dream, although she dreaded the accomplishment of the desires which had arisen in her mind.
她爱他，怀着一种优雅的柔情，这种情感主要源自于她对自己梦境的回忆，尽管她很害怕在她头脑中已经产生的渴望变成现实。
At last, he perceived it, and then she told him everything, even to the dread of his kisses, and she made him swear that he would respect her, and he did so. They spent long hours of transcendental love together, during which their souls alone embraced, and when they separated, they were enervated, weak and feverish.
他最终还是发现了她的变化。于是她把一切都告诉了他，甚至连她害怕他的吻也告诉了他。她要他发誓会尊重她，而且他也的确很尊重她。唯有心灵结合的超乎一般的爱情使他们久久的呆在一起。当分别的时候，他们都感到神经衰弱，肢体疲惫和心情激动。
Sometimes their lips met, and with closed eyes they reveled in that long, yet chaste caress; she felt, however, that she could not resist much longer, and as she did not wish to yield, she wrote and told her husband that she wanted to come to him, and to return to her tranquil, solitary life. But in reply, he wrote her a very kind letter, and strongly advised her not to return in the middle of the winter, and so expose herself to a sudden change of climate, and to the icy mists of the valley, and she was thunderstruck, and angry with that confiding man, who did not guess, who did not understand, the struggles of her heart.
有时候他们的嘴唇合在一起，他们闭上双眼，陶醉在这种久久的，但很纯洁简单的爱抚之中。然而，她感到她也许再也坚持不了多久了。因为她不希望失足，所以她便写信告诉她丈夫，说她想回到他身边，回到她曾经平静和孤独的生活中去。但是他回了一封非常善意的信，强烈地建议她不要在这严寒的冬季突然改变生活环境而返回山谷，回到冰冷的浓雾中。她吓了一跳，对这个容易相信人的男人很生气，因为他不懂猜测，也不懂她内心的挣扎。
February was a warm, bright month, and although she now avoided being alone with Monsieur Avancelle, she sometimes accepted his invitation to drive round the lake in the Bois de Boulogne with him, when it was dusk.
二月是个气候温和天气晴朗的月份，尽管她现在避免和阿旺塞先生单独相处，她有时候还是会同意在傍晚时分，和他一起乘车绕着布洛涅树林中的湖游玩。
On one of those evenings, it was so warm that it seemed as if the sap in every tree and plant were rising. Their cab was going at a walk; it was growing dusk, and they were sitting close together, holding each others' hands, and she said to herself: 
有一天晚上，天气特别暖和，似乎所有的树木和植物都觉醒了，充满了生命的气息。他们的马车向前行驶着，天色渐渐变暗，他们坐得很近，紧紧地握着对方的手，她对自己说：
"It is all over, I am lost!" for she felt her desires rising in her again, the imperious want for that supreme embrace, which she had undergone in her dream. Every moment their lips sought each other, clung together and separated, only to meet again immediately.
“一切都完了，我已经迷失了自我！”因为她感受到内心再一次升起了一股渴望，对那种欲仙欲死的拥抱的强烈的渴望，这种拥抱她已经在梦中感受过了。他们的嘴无时无刻不在寻找着对方，合在一起又分离开，然后又马上合在一起。
He did not venture to go into the house with her, but left her at her door, more in love with him than ever, and half fainting.
他不敢随她一起进她的家，只是送到她家门口，她比以前更爱他了，快要神魂颠倒了。
Monsieur Paul Péronel was waiting for her in the little drawing-room, without a light, and when he shook hands with her, he felt how feverish she was. He began to talk in a low, tender voice, lulling her worn-out mind with the charm of amorous words.
保罗·佩罗内尔先生在没有电灯的客厅里正等着她。当他触摸到她的手，他感到她的手是如此的烫。他开始轻声讲话，声音十分温柔，用充满诱惑的绵绵情话来抚慰她衰竭的心灵。
She listened to him without replying, for she was thinking of the other; she thought she was listening to the other, and thought she felt him leaning against her, in a kind of hallucination. She saw only him, and did not remember that any other man existed on earth, and when her ears trembled at those three syllables: "I love you," it was he, the other man, who uttered them, who kissed her hands, who strained her to his breast, like the other had done shortly before in the cab. It was he who pressed victorious kisses on her lips, it was his lips, it was he whom she held in her arms and embraced, whom she was calling to, with all the longings of her heart, with all the over-wrought ardor of her body.
她听他讲，却不回答，因为她心里想着另一个人。她以为她听到的是另一个人的声音；她以为在某种幻觉当中另一个人紧挨着自己。她的眼中只有他，再也记不起世界上还存在着其他的男人。当她的耳朵因听到“我爱你”三个字而哆嗦时，其实是另一个男人说的，那个男人吻着她的手，强行将他搂入自己的胸怀，就像刚才马车里的那个人一样。是他向她的唇上投下这胜利的吻，那是他的唇，她拥抱的、她紧搂着的、她以激动的心情和全身的热情呼唤的都是他。
When she awoke from her dream, she uttered a terrible cry. Captain Fracasse was kneeling by her, and thanking her, passionately, while he covered her disheveled hair with kisses, and she almost screamed out: 
当她从梦中醒来，她发出了一声恐怖的叫喊。弗拉格斯上尉跪在她旁边，一面吻着她蓬乱的头发，一面热情地感谢她。而她几乎尖声吼道：
"Go away! go away! go away!
“滚出去！滚！滚！
And as he did not understand what she meant, and tried to put his arm round her waist again, she writhed, as she stammered out: 
因为他不懂她是怎么了，还试图想再次搂她的腰，她挣脱出来，结结巴巴地说：
"You are a wretch, and I hate you! Go away! go away!" And he got up in great surprise, took up his hat, and went.
“你这个无赖，我恨你！滚开！滚！”于是他十分诧异地站了起来，拿起帽子走了。
The next day she returned to Val de Ciré, and her husband, who had not expected her for some time, blamed her for a freak.
第二天，她便回西雷山谷了。她的丈夫没有料到她会突然回来，埋怨她不该如此任性。
"I could not live away from you any longer," she said.
她回答说：“离开你我再也无法活下去了。”
He found her altered in character, and sadder than formerly, but when he said to her: 
他发现她性格变了，比以前更加忧郁。但是当他问她：
"What is the matter with you? You seem unhappy. What do you want?" she replied: 
“你怎么了？你看起来不开心。你想要什么吗？”她回答说：
"Nothing. Happiness exists only in our dreams, in this world." 
“我什么也不需要。这个世上只有梦中才有快乐。”
Avancelle came to see her the next summer, and she received him without any emotion, and without regret, for she suddenly perceived that she had never loved him, except in a dream, from which Paul Péronel had brutally roused her.
第二年夏天，阿旺塞来拜访她。她接待了他，内心很平静，也无半点悔恨。她突然之间明白了她从没有爱过他，除了在被保罗·佩罗内尔粗鲁地唤醒的梦中。
But the young man, who still adored her, thought as he returned to Paris: 
但是这个始终爱着她的年轻人在回到巴黎后心里想的是：
"Women are really very strange, complicated and inexplicable beings." 
“女人真的是一种很奇怪很复杂又令人费解的生物啊！”
