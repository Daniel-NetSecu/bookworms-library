THE FLY

Little Fly

Thy summer’s play

My thoughtless hand

Has brush’d away.


Am not I

A fly like thee?

Or art not thou

A man like me?


For I dance

And drink & sing:

Till some blind hand

Shall brush my wing.


If thought is life

And strength & breath:

And the want

Of thought is death;


Then am I

A happy fly,

If I live,

Or if I die.
