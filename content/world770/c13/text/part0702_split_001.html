Lightning McQueen

Lightning McQueen is a red racer car sponsored by Rust-eze Medicated Bumper Ointment. He was at first obnoxious, noxious and rude to everyone. One day on the way to California for the rematch, he got lost and ended up in Radiator Springs where he accidentally tore up the main road and was arrested by the Sheriff. His stay in Radiator Springs fixing the road helped him make friends with town folks. Lightning began to care about others.When he returned to the match, he gave up the Piston Cup and helped The King finish the race.
