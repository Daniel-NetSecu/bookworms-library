THANKS.  

感谢  

Wednesday, 28th.

星期三，28日

My poor schoolmistress wanted to finish her year of school: she departed only three days before the end of the lessons.
我可怜的女老师本想教完这个学年，但她在课程结束的三天前去世了。
Day after Tomorrow we go once more to the schoolroom to hear the reading of the monthly story, Shipwreck, and then—it is over.
后天，我们会去学校听最后一次的每月故事《遇难船》，之后——这个学年就结束了。
On Saturday, the first of July, the examinations begin. And then another year, the fourth, is past! And if my mistress had not died, it would have passed well.
七月一日，星期六，考试即将开始。然后，再过一年，五年级也会成为过去！假如我的女老师没有死，这一学年本会过得很好。
I thought over all that I had known on the preceding October, and it seems to me that I know a good deal more: I have so many new things in my mind; I can say and write what I think better than I could then; I can also do the sums of many grown-up men who know nothing about it, and help them in their affairs; and I understand much more: I understand nearly everything that I read. I am satisfied. But how many people have urged me on and helped me to learn, one in one way, and another in another, at home, at school, in the street,—everywhere where I have been and where I have seen anything! And now, I thank you all.
与去年十月相比，我觉得自己学到了更多知识。我的头脑里装满了许多新事物，我能够比那时更好地说出和写出自己的想法，可以做一些大人都不会的算术，帮他们做事。我的理解能力也大有长进，我阅读的所有东西我几乎都能理解。我对自己很满意。但是有很多人用不同的方式勉励和帮助过我学习，在家里、在学校或是在街上——在我去过或有所见闻的任何地方！现在，我感谢大家。
I thank you first, my good teacher, for having been so indulgent and affectionate with me; for you every new acquisition of mine was a labor, for which I now rejoice and of which I am proud.
首先，我要感谢你，我的好老师，感谢你这么容忍我，这样爱护我，费心教授我新知识。这些知识现在让我又欣喜又自豪。
I thank you, Derossi, my admirable companion, for your prompt and kind explanations, for you have made me understand many of the most difficult things, and overcome stumbling-blocks at examinations; and you, too, Stardi, you brave and strong boy, who have showed me how a will of iron succeeds in everything; and you, kind, generous Garrone, who make all those who know you kind and generous too; and you too, Precossi and Coretti, who have given me an example of courage in suffering, and of serenity in toil, I render thanks to you: I render thanks to all the rest.
我要感谢你，代洛西，我令人钦佩的朋友，感谢你及时、亲切的讲解，感谢你让我明白了很多最难懂的知识，在考试中战胜了许多障碍。还有你，斯带地，你这个勇敢而又强壮的男孩。你使我知道，钢铁般的意志可以战胜任何困难。还有你，善良、慷慨的卡隆，你使认识你的人也变得善良和慷慨。还有泼来可西和可莱谛，你们勇敢地面对痛苦，平静地经历艰辛，为我树立了榜样，我向你们致谢。我向其他所有人致谢。
But above all, I thank thee, my father, thee, my first teacher, my first friend, who hast given me so many wise counsels, and hast taught me so many things, whilst thou wert working for me, always concealing thy sadness from me, and seeking in all ways to render study easy, and life beautiful to me; and thee, sweet mother, my beloved and blessed guardian angel, who hast tasted all my joys, and suffered all my bitternesses, who hast studied, worked, and wept with me, with one hand caressing my brow, and with the other pointing me to heaven.
但我特别要感谢你，我的父亲，我的第一个老师，第一个朋友，你给我种种明智的忠告，教我种种事情，平日为我辛劳，总是向我隐瞒你的痛苦，用各种办法使我能轻松地学习、愉快地生活。还有你，我亲爱的母亲，我心爱的、神圣的守护天使，你与我一同快乐，一同悲伤，一同学习，一同工作，一同哭泣。你用一只手爱抚我的额头，用另一只手指引我走向天堂。
I kneel before you, as when I was a little child; I thank you for all the tenderness which you have instilled into my mind through twelve years of sacrifices and of love.
我像儿时一样跪在你的面前，感谢你在过去的十二年里通过牺牲与爱，将温柔注入我的心里。
