ITALY.  

意大利  

Tuesday, 14th.

星期二，14日

Salute your country thus, on days of festival: "Italy, my country, dear and noble land, where my father and my mother were born, and where they will be buried, where I hope to live and die, where my children will grow up and die; beautiful Italy, great and glorious for many centuries, united and free for a few years; thou who didst disseminate so great a light of intellect divine over the world, and for whom so many valiant men have died on the battle-field, and so many heroes on the gallows; august mother of three hundred cities, and thirty millions of sons; I, a child, who do not understand thee as yet, and who do not know thee in thy entirety, I venerate and love thee with all my soul, and I am proud of having been born of thee, and of calling myself thy son.
在国庆日，你应该这样祝福你的祖国：“意大利，我的祖国，亲爱且神圣的土地，我的父母出生并将埋葬于这里，我自己也希望能在这里生活、安葬，我的孩子们也将在这片土地上成长、死亡。美丽的意大利，尽管获得统一和自由只有数年，你的光荣与伟大却贯穿了数个世纪。你将伟大神圣的智慧之光传给世界，为了你，无数勇士战死沙场，许多英雄命丧断头台。你是三百城池和三千万子女威严的母亲。我作为一个孩子，虽然不能知道你、了解你的全部，却全心全意地崇敬并热爱着你，以生在你的怀抱、做你的儿子为荣。
I love thy splendid seas and thy sublime mountains; I love thy solemn monuments and thy immortal memories; I love thy glory and thy beauty; I love and venerate the whole of thee as that beloved portion of thee where I, for the first time, beheld the light and heard thy name.
我爱你壮丽的海洋和壮美的山脉，我爱你庄严的古迹和不朽的历史，我爱你的荣耀和美丽。自从我第一次从你那里看见了光、听见了你的名字，我就热爱和崇敬着你的全部。
I love the whole of thee, with a single affection and with equal gratitude,—Turin the valiant, Genoa the superb, Bologna the learned, Venice the enchanting, Milan the mighty; I love you with the uniform reverence of a son, gentle Florence and terrible Palermo, immense and beautiful Naples, marvellous and eternal Rome.
我始终一心一意、满怀感激地爱着你的全部——英勇的都灵、华丽的热那亚、睿智的博洛尼亚、神秘的威尼斯、伟大的米兰。我像儿子爱父亲一样敬爱着你，爱温柔的佛罗伦萨，爱威严的巴勒莫，爱宏大而美丽的那不勒斯，爱美妙而不朽的罗马。
I love thee, my sacred country!
我爱你，我神圣的祖国！
And I swear that I will love all thy sons like brothers; that I will always honor in my heart thy great men, living and dead; that I will be an industrious and honest citizen, constantly intent on ennobling myself, in order to render myself worthy of thee, to assist with my small powers in causing misery, ignorance, injustice, crime, to disappear one day from thy face, so that thou mayest live and expand tranquilly in the majesty of thy right and of thy strength.
我发誓，我会爱你所有的儿子，如同爱我的兄弟一样；我会从心中敬仰你所生的伟人，无论是活着的还是死去的；我会成为一个勤勉而诚实的公民，不懈地提高自己的品德，好让自己不辱你名，尽我绵薄之力使不幸、无知、不公和罪恶终有一天在你的面前消失，你便可以在威严的权利和力量之下宁静地生活和舒展。
I swear that I will serve thee, as it may be granted to me, with my mind, with my arm, with my heart, humbly, ardently; and that, if the day should dawn in which I should be called on to give my blood for thee and my life, I will give my blood, and I will die, crying thy holy name to heaven, and wafting my last kiss to thy blessed banner." 
我发誓，如同你赋予我的神圣使命一样，用我的学识和身心谦卑地、热心地尽忠于你。如有一天需要我把鲜血和生命奉献给你，我会仰天呼唤你神圣的名字，亲吻你祝福过的旗帜，洒下我的鲜血，献出我的生命。”
Thy Father.
你的父亲
