CHAPTER XXVI  

第二十六章  

At eight o'clock next morning, Emil arrived at Sanin's hotel leading Tartaglia by a string. Had he sprung of German parentage, he could not have shown greater practicality. He had told a lie at home; he had said he was going for a walk with Sanin till lunch-time, and then going to the shop. While Sanin was dressing, Emil began to talk to him, rather hesitatingly, it is true, about Gemma, about her rupture with Herr Kléber; but Sanin preserved an austere silence in reply, and Emil, looking as though he understood why so serious a matter should not be touched on lightly, did not return to the subject, and only assumed from time to time an intense and even severe expression.
第二天早晨八点钟，埃米尔用一条皮带牵着塔尔塔利阿来到萨宁住的旅馆。即使他是德国血统，也不会比现在更加讲求实际。他给家里人撒了个谎：他说自己要跟萨宁去散步到午餐时间，然后去店里。当萨宁还在穿衣服的时候，埃米尔就有点犹豫地对他说起吉玛来，说她跟克吕贝尔先生断绝关系的事情；但是萨宁以严肃的沉默作为回答，于是，埃米尔看起来好像明白了，如此严肃的事情是不该这么轻描淡写的，他再也没有说起这件事，只是时不时地露出紧张甚至严肃的表情。
After drinking coffee, the two friends set off together—on foot, of course—to Hausen, a little village lying a short distance from Frankfort, and surrounded by woods. The whole chain of the Taunus mountains could be seen clearly from there. The weather was lovely; the sunshine was bright and warm, but not blazing hot; a fresh wind rustled briskly among the green leaves; the shadows of high, round clouds glided swiftly and smoothly in small patches over the earth. The two young people soon got out of the town, and stepped out boldly and gaily along the well-kept road. They reached the woods, and wandered about there a long time; then they lunched very heartily at a country inn; then climbed on to the mountains, admired the views, rolled stones down and clapped their hands, watching the queer droll way in which the stones hopped along like rabbits, till a man passing below, unseen by them, began abusing them in a loud ringing voice. Then they lay full length on the short dry moss of yellowish-violet colour; then they drank beer at another inn; ran races, and tried for a wager which could jump farthest. They discovered an echo, and began to call to it; sang songs, hallooed, wrestled, broke up dry twigs, decked their hats with fern, and even danced. Tartaglia, as far as he could, shared in all these pastimes; he did not throw stones, it is true, but he rolled head over heels after them; he howled when they were singing, and even drank beer, though with evident aversion; he had been trained in this art by a student to whom he had once belonged. But he was not prompt in obeying Emil—not as he was with his master Pantaleone—and when Emil ordered him to "speak" or to "sneeze", he only wagged his tail and thrust out his tongue like a pipe.
喝完咖啡，这朋友俩一起出发——当然是步行——去哈泽。哈泽是个小村庄，离法兰克福只有一小段距离，周围全是树林。在那儿，整个陶努斯山脉都一览无余。天气宜人，阳光温暖而明媚，并不刺眼灼热；凉爽的风轻快地拨动着绿叶，弄得绿叶沙沙作响；高空中一团团云彩投下的斑驳的影子，在大地上轻快而平顺地滑动着。两个年轻人一会儿就出了城，大胆地、快乐地走在打扫得干干净净的马路上。他们到了树林里，在那里逛了很久；然后在一家乡村小饭馆饱饱地吃了一顿午餐；接着又爬上山，欣赏周围的风景，从山上往下滚石头，鼓着掌看着滚下的石头像兔子一样奇怪而有趣地蹦下山，直到山下出现一个他们看不见的过路人大声骂他们。后来，他们躺在一块紫黄色的、干枯短小的苔藓上，舒展着四肢；接而他们在另一家小饭馆里喝了啤酒，一起赛跑，还打赌看谁跳得最远。他们发现了回声，便开始喊它，唱着歌，叫喊着，摔着跤，折干树枝，把羊齿草插在帽子上，还一起跳起了舞。塔尔塔利阿也尽情地一起分享着这份悠闲。当然，它不会扔石头，但它翻滚着跟在石头后面；他们唱歌的时候它就嚎叫着，甚至还喝了啤酒，虽然它显然不喜欢喝——这是它以前的主人，一名学生教给它的本领。可是他对埃米尔的话反应不快——不像和它的主人潘塔莱昂纳一起时——当埃米尔让它“讲话”或者“打喷嚏”时，它只是摇摇尾巴，或者伸出像管子一样的舌头。
The young people talked, too. At the beginning of the walk, Sanin, as the elder, and so more reflective, turned the conversation on fate and predestination, and the nature and meaning of man's destiny; but the conversation quickly took a less serious turn. Emil began to question his friend and patron about Russia, how duels were fought there, and whether the women there were beautiful, and whether one could learn Russian quickly, and what he had felt when the officer took aim at him. Sanin, on his side, questioned Emil about his father, his mother, and in general about their family affairs, trying every time not to mention Gemma's name—and thinking only of her. To speak more precisely, it was not of her he was thinking, but of the morrow, the mysterious morrow which was to bring him new, unknown happiness! It was as though a veil, a delicate, bright veil, hung faintly fluttering before his mental vision; and behind this veil he felt... felt the presence of a youthful, motionless, divine image, with a tender smile on its lips, and eyelids severely—with affected severly—downcast. And this image was not the face of Gemma, it was the face of happiness itself! For, behold, at last his hour had come, the veil had vanished, the lips were parting, the eyelashes are raised—his divinity has looked upon him—and at once light as from the sun, and joy and bliss unending! He dreamed of this morrow—and his soul thrilled with joy again in the melting torture of ever-growing expectation!
两个年轻人也互相交谈。刚出来漫步时，萨宁作为大哥，提的话题比较成熟，谈起了命运和宿命以及人类命运的意义和本质，但是谈话一会儿便转移到比较轻松的话题上了。埃米尔向自己的朋友和保护人问起了俄罗斯的情况：他那里是怎样进行决斗的；俄罗斯的女孩是不是很漂亮；俄语是不是很快就能学会；还有，那个军官拿枪对着他的时候，他是什么样的感受。而萨宁也问埃米尔关于他的父亲、母亲以及家里的大概的情况，每次都竭力不提及吉玛的名字——心里却只想着她。更确切地说，他那会儿想的不是她，而是想着明天，想着那将会给他带来全新的、未知的幸福的神秘的明天！他心里仿佛挂着帷幕，一帘精致、明亮的帷幕，在他脑海里轻轻地摇晃着。在这层帷幕后面，他感觉到……感觉到了那年轻的、静止的、神圣的面容，嘴唇上挂着温柔的微笑，而睫毛严肃地——假装严肃地——下垂着。而这面容却不是吉玛的面容，难道是幸福本身的面容？他看到，最后的时刻就要到来了，这层面纱已经消失了，嘴唇张开了，睫毛抬起了——他的女神看着他——顷刻间，来自太阳的光亮、无尽的欢乐和喜悦向他袭来。他梦想着他的明天——他的心灵又一次受着兴奋与渐增的期望融合为一体后带来的煎熬！
And this expectation, this torture, hindered nothing. It accompanied every action, and did not prevent anything. It did not prevent him from dining capitally at a third inn with Emil; and only occasionally, like a brief flash of lightning, the thought shot across him, What if any one in the world knew? This suspense did not prevent him from playing leap-frog with Emil after dinner. The game took place on an open green lawn. And the confusion, the stupefaction of Sanin may be imagined! At the very moment when, accompanied by a sharp bark from Tartaglia, he was flying like a bird, with his legs outspread over Emil, who was bent double, he suddenly saw on the farthest border of the lawn two officers, in whom he recognised at once his adversary and his second, Herr von Dénhof and Herr von Richter! Each of them had stuck an eyeglass in his eye, and was staring at him, chuckling!... Sanin got on his feet, turned away hurriedly, put on the coat he had flung down, jerked out a word to Emil; the latter, too, put on his jacket, and they both immediately made off.
这种期望、这种折磨，却没有带来什么阻碍。虽然这种情绪伴随着他的每一个行动，但一点儿都不碍事。这种情绪没有妨碍他与埃米尔在第三家餐馆里尽情地用餐。只是一个想法偶尔像一道短暂的闪电一样，在他的脑海里闪过——如果世上有其他人知道呢？这种焦虑也没有影响他与埃米尔饭后玩跳背游戏。他们在一块开阔的绿草坪上玩着。可以想象，萨宁是既有些迷惑又有些麻木！就在这个时候，在塔尔塔利阿的狂吠声中，他叉开双腿，像鸟儿一般从埃米尔弯着的腰上跃过时，他突然看到，草坪的另一边远远站着两个军官，他一眼认出，原来是他的对手和他的副手，冯·登霍夫先生和冯·里希特先生。他们都戴着眼镜，望着他吃吃地笑着……萨宁双脚一落地，就匆忙转过身去，穿上了脱下的外套，突然和埃米尔说了句话；埃米尔也穿上了外衣，两个人立刻离开了。
It was late when they got back to Frankfort. "They'll scold me," Emil said to Sanin as he said good-bye to him. "Well, what does it matter? I've had such a splendid, splendid day!" 
他们回到法兰克福时已经很晚了。“他们会责备我的，”埃米尔跟萨宁道别时说道，“唉，这又有什么关系？我今天玩得这么痛快，真是痛快的一天！”
When he got home to his hotel, Sanin found a note there from Gemma. She fixed a meeting with him for next day, at seven o'clock in the morning, in one of the public gardens which surround Frankfort on all sides.
当萨宁回到他住的旅馆时，发现吉玛留了一张便条。她约他第二天早晨七点，在法兰克福附近的一个公共公园里见面。
How his heart throbbed! How glad he was that he had obeyed her so unconditionally! And, my God, what was promised... what was not promised, by that unknown, unique, impossible, and undubitably certain morrow!
他的心颤动得多么厉害啊！他多么乐意这样无条件地听她的话！上帝啊，这未知的、独特的、绝无可能而又确切无疑的明天到底预示着什么？还有什么没预示的呢？
He feasted his eyes on Gemma's note. The long, elegant tail of the letter G, the first letter of her name, which stood at the bottom of the sheet, reminded him of her lovely fingers, her hand... He thought that he had not once touched that hand with his lips... "Italian women," he mused, "in spite of what's said of them, are modest and severe… And Gemma above all! Queen... goddess... pure, virginal marble…” 
他盯着吉玛的便条看着。便条末尾签名的首字母G，拖着一条纤长而秀美的尾巴，这让他又想起了她那可爱的手指，她的手……他心里想，他还没有用自己的嘴唇吻过那双手……“意大利女人，”他沉思着，“不管别人怎么说，她们是谦虚而严肃的……吉玛就更不用说了！她是女王……女神……一尊纯洁无暇的大理石像……”
"But the time will come; and it is not far off... There was that night in Frankfort one happy man…” 
“可是日子总会到来的，这一天已经不远了……那天夜里，法兰克福有一个幸福的人……”
"I sleep... but my watchful heart sleeps not." 
“我睡了……但是我那敏感的心却醒着。”
And it fluttered as lightly as a butterfly flutters his wings, as he stoops over the flowers in the summer sunshine.
他的心跳得那么轻盈，宛如一只在夏天阳光下停息在花上的小蝴蝶颤动着翅膀。
