Elsa　艾莎

Personality　性格

The perfect heir to the throne, Elsa is a natural leader and loved by her Kingdom. Regal and graceful, Elsa is everything a queen should be. But she harbors a secret — she has the power to control ice and snow.

作为最佳的王位继承人，艾莎是天生的领导者，并深受其国民的爱戴。艾莎既庄严又优雅，具备了女王该有的所有气质。但是她有一个秘密，即她拥有控制冰雪的魔力。

Strengths　优点

Natural leader, controlled, regal, graceful, can control ice and snow

天生的领导者，克制，庄严，优雅，能控制冰雪

Weakness　缺点

Doesn’t trust others, secretive, guarded, emotionally closed off, emotionally cold

不相信别人，非常神秘，谨慎，不与人沟通，情感冷漠
