139. When Life Is Quite Through With

——E. E. Cummings

When life is quite through with
And leaves say alas,
Much is to do
For the swallow, that closes
A flight in the blue;

When love's had his tears out,
Perhaps shall pass
A million years.
(While a bee dozes
On the poppies, the dears;

When all's done and said, and
Under the grass
Lies her head
By oaks and roses
Deliberated.)
