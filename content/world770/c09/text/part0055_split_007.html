CHAPTER VIII  

第八章  

He had not finished dressing, when a waiter announced the arrival of two gentlemen. One of them turned out to be Emil; the other, a good-looking and well-grown young man, with a handsome face, was Herr Karl Kléber, the betrothed of the lovely Gemma.
他还没穿好衣服，服务员就进来了，说外面来了两位先生要见他。其中一个原来是埃米尔，另外一个仪表堂堂、穿着得体、五官端正，便是卡尔• 克吕贝尔先生，可爱的吉玛的未婚夫。
One may safely assume that at that time in all Frankfort, there was not in a single shop a manager as civil, as decorous, as dignified, and as affable as Herr Klé The irreproachable perfection of his get-up was on a level with the dignity of his deportment, with the elegance—a little affected and stiff, it is true, in the English style (he had spent two years in England)—but still fascinating, elegance of his manners! It was clear from the first glance that this handsome, rather severe, excellently brought-up and superbly washed young man was accustomed to obey his superior and to command his inferior, and that behind the counter of his shop he must infallibly inspire respect even in his customers! Of his supernatural honesty there could never be a particle of doubt: one had but to look at his stiffly starched collars! And his voice, it appeared, was just what one would expect; deep, and of a self-confident richness, but not too loud, with positively a certain caressing note in its timbre. Such a voice was peculiarly fitted to give orders to assistants under his control: "Show the crimson Lyons velvet!" or, "Hand the lady a chair!" 
可以这么说，那时在整个法兰克福，不论哪家商店里都找不出一个像克吕贝尔先生那么礼貌有加、举止得体、和蔼可亲又不失威严的老板。他的穿着和他那不失威严的举止一样优雅得体，无可指摘，只是稍显古板，带点儿英国人的派头（他在英国呆过两年）——但是仍然优雅而有风度，令人着迷。第一眼就能看得清清楚楚，这个潇洒英俊、极其严肃、颇有教养、干净整洁的年轻人惯于对上司唯命是从，对下属颐指气使。他站在店面的柜台后面必然会赢得人们的尊重，甚至是顾客。他那异乎寻常的诚实是不容置疑的：只要看看他那直挺挺的衣领就知道了。他的嗓音和想象中一样，听起来醇厚洪亮而充满自信，声音不是很高，音色中明显有某种亲切的味道。这样的嗓音极适合对他的店员发号施令：“把那件深红色的里昂天鹅绒拿出来瞧瞧！”或者是，“给这位女士拿一把椅子来！”
Herr Klüber began with introducing himself; as he did so, he bowed with such loftiness, moved his legs with such an agreeable air, and drew his heels together with such polished courtesy that no one could fail to feel, "that man has both linen and moral principles of the first quality!" The finish of his bare right hand—(the left, in a suede glove, held a hat shining like a looking-glass, with the right glove placed within it)—the finish of the right hand, proffered modestly but resolutely to Sanin, surpassed all belief; each finger-nail was a perfection in its own way! Then he proceeded to explain in the choicest German that he was anxious to express his respect and his indebtedness to the foreign gentleman who had performed so signal a service to his future kinsman, the brother of his betrothed; as he spoke, he waved his left hand with the hat in it in the direction of Emil, who seemed bashful and turning away to the window, put his finger in his mouth. Herr Kléber added that he should esteem himself happy should he be able in return to do anything for the foreign gentleman. Sanin, with some difficulty, replied, also in German, that he was delighted... that the service was not worth speaking of... and he begged his guests to sit down. Herr Klüber thanked him, and lifting his coat-tails, sat down on a chair; but he perched there so lightly and with such a transitory air that no one could fail to realise, "this man is sitting down from politeness, and will fly up again in an instant." And he did in fact fly up again quickly, and advancing with two discreet little dance-steps, he announced that to his regret he was unable to stay any longer, as he had to hasten to his shop—business before everything! But as the next day was Sunday, he had, with the consent of Frau Lenore and Fréulein Gemma, arranged a holiday excursion to Soden, to which he had the honour of inviting the foreign gentleman, and he cherished the hope that he would not refuse to grace the party with his presence. Sanin did not refuse so to grace it; and Herr Kléber repeating once more his complimentary sentiments, took leave, his pea-green trousers making a spot of cheerful colour, and his brand-new boots squeaking cheerfully as he moved.
克吕贝尔先生开始介绍他自己，同时还是那么绅士地弯一下腰，两腿自然合拢，毕恭毕敬地碰了一下脚跟。这给每个人的感觉是：“这个年轻人衣着得体，品德高尚！”没戴手套的右手是修剪过的（左手戴着绒面革手套，拿着一顶光滑如镜的帽子，右手放在了帽子里面），就是那只修剪过的右手，出人意料地谦恭而坚毅地伸向萨宁，萨宁看到每个指甲都修剪得近乎完美。然后，他开始挑德语里最好听的句子说道，他很想对这位外国的先生表达自己的敬意和感激，因为这位先生对他未来的亲戚——他未婚妻的弟弟——出过这么大的力。说着，他向萨宁挥挥拿着帽子的左手，指指埃米尔，埃米尔看起来仿佛有点害臊，扭过脸去，把一个手指放在了嘴里，望着窗外。克吕贝尔先生还补充道，如果也能为这位外国先生做些什么的话，他将深感荣幸。萨宁一边用磕磕巴巴的德语回答——他很乐意……这点小事不足挂齿，一边请客人们入座。克吕贝尔先生道了谢，撩开燕尾服的后襟坐在了椅子上。但是他只是轻轻地坐下，都没完全坐下去，使人不难看出：“这个人只是出于礼貌才坐下的，随即就会起来。”果然他很快就站起来了，迈舞步似的谨慎地向前走了两小步，说他很抱歉，不能再呆在这儿了，他还要赶去店里面——生意最要紧啊！不过因为明天是星期日，他已经征得了莱诺雷太太和吉玛小姐的同意，安排到索登去游玩。他也想邀请这位外国的先生一起去，希望他会赏光，不要拒绝。萨宁没有拒绝，一口答应了。克吕贝尔先生又诉说了一番他的感激之情，就告辞了。他那豆绿色的裤子闪着令人愉悦的光彩，崭新的靴子也随着他的脚步发出悦耳的嘎吱声。
