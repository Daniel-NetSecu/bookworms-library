I can wade grief

I can wade grief,

Whole pools of it, —

I’m used to that.

But the least push of joy

Breaks up my feet,

And I tip — drunken.

Let no pebble smile,

’Twas the new liquor, —

That was all!


Power is only pain,

Stranded, through discipline,

Till weights will hang.

Give balm to giants,

And they’ll wilt, like men.

Give Himmaleh, —

They’ll carry him!
