JUNE.  

六月  

GARIBALDI.

加里波第

June 3d.

六月三日

Tomorrow is the National Festival Day.
明天是国庆节。
Today is a day of national mourning. Garibaldi died last night. Do you know who he is? He is the man who liberated ten millions of Italians from the tyranny of the Bourbons. He died at the age of seventy-five. He was born at Nice, the son of a ship captain. At eight years of age, he saved a woman's life; at thirteen, he dragged into safety a boat-load of his companions who were shipwrecked; at twenty-seven, he rescued from the water at Marseilles a drowning youth; at forty-one, he saved a ship from burning on the ocean. He fought for ten years in America for the liberty of a strange people; he fought in three wars against the Austrians, for the liberation of Lombardy and Trentino; he defended Rome from the French in 1849; he delivered Naples and Palermo in 1860; he fought again for Rome in 1867; he combated with the Germans in defence of France in 1870. He was possessed of the flame of heroism and the genius of war. He was engaged in forty battles, and won thirty-seven of them.
今日是举国哀悼的日子。加里波第昨夜逝世了。你知道他是谁吗？他是把千万意大利人民从波旁暴政之下解救出来的人。他享年七十五岁。他生于尼斯，父亲是位船长。八岁那年，他救了一个女人的性命；十三岁那年，他使一艘遇险船只上的伙伴们转危为安；二十七岁那年，他在马赛救起一个溺水的年轻人；四十一岁那年，他使海上一艘船免遭火灾。为了其他民族的自由，他在美洲斗争了十年；为了解放伦巴第和特伦蒂诺，他曾与奥军三次交战；一八四九年他为守卫罗马而抵御法军；一八六零年他解放了那不勒斯和巴勒莫；一八六七年他再次为罗马而战；一八七零年他同德军联合抵御法军。他拥有英雄的热情和作战的天赋。他参加过四十场战争，得胜三十七场。
When he was not fighting, he was laboring for his living, or he shut himself up in a solitary island, and tilled the soil. He was teacher, sailor, workman, trader, soldier, general, dictator. He was simple, great, and good. He hated all oppressors, he loved all peoples, he protected all the weak; he had no other aspiration than good, he refused honors, he scorned death, he adored Italy. When he uttered his war-cry, legions of valorous men hastened to him from all quarters; gentlemen left their palaces, workmen their ships, youths their schools, to go and fight in the sunshine of his glory. In time of war he wore a red shirt. He was strong, blond, and handsome. On the field of battle he was a thunder-bolt, in his affections he was a child, in affliction a saint. Thousands of Italians have died for their country, happy, if, when dying, they saw him pass victorious in the distance; thousands would have allowed themselves to be killed for him; millions have blessed and will bless him.
不打仗时，他靠自己劳动过活，或者隐居孤岛，耕田为生。他曾经当过老师、海员、工人、商人、士兵、将军和指挥官。他质朴、伟大而又善良。他痛恨一切压迫者，热爱所有人民，保护所有弱者。他一心只想行善，他拒绝荣誉，他不畏死亡，他热爱意大利。他一发出作战号令，众多勇士们就赶忙从四面聚集到他面前。绅士们离开邸宅，海员们离开船舶，年轻人们离开学校，纷纷投奔到他的光辉之旗下作战。打仗的时候，他常穿一件红衣。他身强力壮、金发碧眼、相貌英俊。战场上的他雷厉风行，感情中的他像个孩子，痛苦中的他犹如圣者。成千上万的意大利士兵为国捐躯，如果能在临死之时望见威风凛凛的将军从远处凯旋，他们就会感到幸福。成千上万的人们愿意为他献出生命，数百万的人们祝福过他，并会一直祝福他。
He is dead. The whole world mourns him. You do not understand him now. But you will read of his deeds, you will constantly hear him spoken of in the course of your life; and gradually, as you grow up, his image will grow before you; when you become a man, you will behold him as a giant; and when you are no longer in the world, when your sons' sons and those who shall be born from them are no longer among the living, the generations will still behold on high his luminous head as a redeemer of the peoples, crowned by the names of his victories as with a circlet of stars; and the brow and the soul of every Italian will beam when he utters his name.
现在他死了。整个世界都为他哀悼。你现在还不了解他。但你会读到他的事迹，会在你的人生历程中时常听到人们提及他。随着你逐渐长大，将军的形象会在你面前越来越清晰。成年以后，你就会将他看作一个伟人。到你和你的子孙后代都去世时，世世代代的人们依旧会仰望这位人民的救世主。他的头上将环绕着胜利的名字，犹如一顶点缀着星星的王冠。一提到他的名字，每个意大利人的额头和灵魂都会闪烁着荣光。
Thy Father.
