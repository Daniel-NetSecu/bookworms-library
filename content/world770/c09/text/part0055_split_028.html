CHAPTER XXIXI  

第二十九章  

If Gemma had announced that she had brought with her cholera or death itself, one can hardly imagine that Frau Lenore could have received the news with greater despair. She immediately sat down in a corner, with her face to the wall, and burst into floods of tears, positively wailed, for all the world like a Russian peasant woman on the grave of her husband or her son. For the first minute Gemma was so taken aback that she did not even go up to her mother, but stood still like a statue in the middle of the room; while Sanin was utterly stupefied, to the point of almost bursting into tears himself! For a whole hour that inconsolable wail went on—a whole hour! Pantaleone thought it better to shut the outer door of the shop, so that no stranger should come; luckily, it was still early. The old man himself did not know what to think, and in any case, did not approve of the haste with which Gemma and Sanin had acted; he could not bring himself to blame them, and was prepared to give them his support in case of need: he greatly disliked Klé Emil regarded himself as the medium of communication between his friend and his sister, and almost prided himself on its all having turned out so splendidly! He was positively unable to conceive why Frau Lenore was so upset, and in his heart he decided on the spot that women, even the best of them, suffer from a lack of reasoning power! Sanin fared worst of all. Frau Lenore rose to a howl and waved him off with her hands, directly he approached her; and it was in vain that he attempted once or twice to shout aloud, standing at a distance, "I ask you for your daughter's hand!" Frau Lenore was particularly angry with herself. "How could she have been so blind—have seen nothing? Had my Giovann Battista been alive," she persisted through her tears, "nothing of this sort would have happened!" "Heavens, what's it all about?" thought Sanin; "why, it's positively senseless!" He did not dare to look at Gemma, nor could she pluck up courage to lift her eyes to him. She restricted herself to waiting patiently on her mother, who at first repelled even her...
几乎可以想象，即便吉玛说她带来了霍乱和死神，莱诺雷太太的失望也不过如此了。她立刻坐到一个角落里，脸朝着墙，嚎啕大哭起来，泪水奔涌而出，完全像一个俄国农妇在她丈夫或儿子的坟边。起先，吉玛很吃惊，甚至不敢走到她母亲跟前去，像个雕塑一样静静地站在屋子的中央。萨宁也完全吓呆了，仿佛感觉他自己也要哭出来了！这场极度沮丧的痛哭整整持续了一个小时——整整一个小时！潘塔莱昂纳觉得最好关上外面的店门，免得陌生人进来，不过好在时间还早。这个老人不知道想什么，无论如何，他不同意吉玛和萨宁这样草率行事。可他自己不忍心责备他们俩，必要时，他还准备帮他们一把：因为他极不喜欢克吕贝尔先生！埃米尔把自己当作他的朋友和姐姐交流的中间人，能有这样了不起的结果，让他甚是自豪！他肯定不能够理解为什么妈妈这样伤心，他心里当时就断定：女人，甚至是最好的女人，也会因缺少理智的力量而痛苦！萨宁最难堪。只要他一靠近莱诺雷太太，她就立刻嚎哭起来，并挥手让他走开；他远远地站着，一次次想大声呼喊：“我要向您的女儿求婚！”但都是徒劳。莱诺雷太太特别生自己的气。“她怎么这么没有眼光——什么都没有看出来呢？要是我的乔瓦尼·巴蒂斯塔还在，”她流着眼泪继续说，“这种事情是不会发生的！”“天啊！这是怎么了？”萨宁寻思着，“为什么，这确实挺愚蠢！”他不敢看吉玛，吉玛也没有勇气抬眼看他。起初她也被母亲推开，但她控制着自己，耐心地等待着母亲……
At last, by degrees, the storm abated. Frau Lenore gave over weeping, permitted Gemma to bring her out of the corner, where she sat huddled up, to put her into an arm-chair near the window, and to give her some orange-flower water to drink. She permitted Sanin—not to approach... oh, no!—but, at any rate, to remain in the room—she had kept clamouring for him to go away—and did not interrupt him when he spoke. Sanin immediately availed himself of the calm as it set in, and displayed an astounding eloquence. He could hardly have explained his intentions and emotions with more fire and persuasive force even to Gemma herself. Those emotions were of the sincerest, those intentions were of the purest, like Almaviva's in the Barber of Seville. He did not conceal from Frau Lenore nor from himself the disadvantageous side of those intentions; but the disadvantages were only apparent! It is true he was a foreigner; they had not known him long, they knew nothing positive about himself or his means; but he was prepared to bring forward all the necessary evidence that he was a respectable person and not poor; he would refer them to the most unimpeachable testimony of his fellow-countrymen! He hoped Gemma would be happy with him, and that he would be able to make up to her for the separation from her own people!.. The allusion to "separation"—the mere word "separation"—almost spoiled the whole business... Frau Lenore began to tremble all over and move about uneasily... Sanin hastened to observe that the separation would only be temporary, and that, in fact, possibly it would not take place at all!
最后，渐渐地，这场暴风雨平息了。莱诺雷太太也止住了哭泣，让吉玛把她从自己刚刚蜷缩过的角落里扶了出来，坐到了窗户旁的扶椅上，还让吉玛给她倒了杯香橙花茶。她让萨宁不要过来……哦，不要！但是，可以让他呆在屋里——本来她一直喊叫着让他离开——她现在不打断他说话了。萨宁立即利用这安静的时刻，表现了一下自己让人惊叹的雄辩之才。即使对吉玛本人，他也未必会这么热情洋溢、令人信服地表达自己的心思和感情了。这些感情是最真诚的，这些心思也是最纯洁的，就像《塞维勒的理发师》中的阿尔马维瓦一样。他没有向莱诺雷太太，也没有向自己隐藏这些心愿的不利之处。但是，这些不利之处也是显而易见的！诚然，他是一个外国人，他们认识他的时间也不长，对他的身份和工作都一无所知。但他已经准备好证明自己是个上流社会的人，不是穷人，他将提供同胞们的最无可置疑的证明！他希望吉玛跟他在一起时快乐，希望他能够弥补吉玛与亲人的离别之情……一提到“离别”——仅仅“离别”这一个词——就差点坏了所有的事……莱诺雷太太开始浑身发抖，不安地走来走去……萨宁急忙说明，离别是暂时的，事实上，有可能根本不会离别！
Sanin's eloquence was not thrown away. Frau Lenore began to glance at him, though still with bitterness and reproach, no longer with the same aversion and fury; then she suffered him to come near her, and even to sit down beside her (Gemma was sitting on the other side); then she fell to reproaching him,—not in looks only, but in words, which already indicated a certain softening of heart; she fell to complaining, and her complaints became quieter and gentler; they were interspersed with questions addressed at one time to her daughter, and at another to Sanin; then she suffered him to take her hand and did not at once pull it away... then she wept again, but her tears were now quite of another kind... Then she smiled mournfully, and lamented the absence of Giovanni Battista, but quite on different grounds from before... An instant more and the two criminals, Sanin and Gemma, were on their knees at her feet, and she was laying her hands on their heads in turn; another instant and they were embracing and kissing her, and Emil, his face beaming rapturously, ran into the room and added himself to the group so warmly united.
萨宁的善辩没有白费。莱诺雷太太开始打量起他来，尽管还有些伤心和责备，但先前的讨厌和恼怒已经没有了。然后她也让他靠近说话，甚至坐在她的旁边（吉玛坐在另一边）。接着，她又开始责备他——不仅是目光，还用言语，这些都表明她已经开始心软了；她又开始埋怨，但逐渐变得轻柔、温和了。后来，她一会儿问她的女儿，一会儿又问问萨宁。最后，她允许萨宁拉着她的手，没有立即缩回去……自己又哭了起来，但已经是另外一种不同的眼泪了……接着她又悲伤地笑了，替乔瓦尼·巴蒂斯塔的离去而惋惜，但与前面的缘由已经不一样了……片刻之后，两个罪人，萨宁和吉玛，跪在她脚边，她也把双手轮流放在他们头上。然后，他们拥抱着她，亲吻着她，而埃米尔眉开眼笑、兴高采烈地跑进屋子，自己也同这几个人温暖地抱在了一起。
Pantaleone peeped into the room, smiled and frowned at the same time, and going into the shop, opened the front door.
潘塔莱昂纳朝屋里瞅了一眼，笑了笑，同时也皱了皱眉，走进店铺，打开了前门。
