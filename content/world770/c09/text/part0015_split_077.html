GIUSEPPE MAZZINI.  

朱塞佩·马志尼  

Saturday, 29th.

星期六，29日

This morning, also, Garrone came to school with a pale face and his eyes swollen with weeping, and he hardly cast a glance at the little gifts which we had placed on his desk to console him. But the teacher had brought a page from a book to read to him in order to encourage him. He first informed us that we are to go Tomorrow at one o'clock to the town-hall to witness the award of the medal for civic valor to a boy who has saved a little child from the Po, and that on Monday he will dictate the description of the festival to us instead of the monthly story. Then turning to Garrone, who was standing with drooping head, he said to him:— 
今天早上，卡隆仍然是脸色苍白、眼睛红肿地来到学校。我们为了安慰他，在他桌上放了小礼物，可他几乎都没看一眼。但是老师从一本书中选了一篇文章，准备念给卡隆听，以此来鼓励他。老师先是通知我们明天下午一点钟去市政厅，参加给从波河里救起小孩的少年颁发市民英雄奖的仪式。星期一老师就不讲每月故事了，而是给我们听写关于颁奖仪式的描写。接着老师转向垂着头站着的卡隆，对他说道：
"Make an effort, Garrone, and write down what I dictate to you as well as the rest." 
“卡隆，努力和大家一同把我讲的话记下来。”
We all took our pens, and the teacher dictated.
我们都拿起笔，老师便开始口述。
"Giuseppe Mazzini, born in Genoa in 1805, died in Pisa in 1872, a grand, patriotic soul, the mind of a great writer, the first inspirer and apostle of the Italian Revolution; who, out of love for his country, lived for forty years poor, exiled, persecuted, a fugitive heroically steadfast in his principles and in his resolutions. Giuseppe Mazzini, who adored his mother, and who derived from her all that there was noblest and purest in her strong and gentle soul, wrote as follows to a faithful friend of his, to console him in the greatest of misfortunes. These are almost his exact words:— 
“朱塞佩·马志尼，一八零五年生于热那亚，一八七二年死于比萨，他是一个伟大的爱国者、大文豪，是意大利革命的启蒙者、先驱者。出于对祖国的热爱，四十年里他甘受贫穷、放逐和迫害，宁为逃亡者，也对自己的原则和决心坚定不移。朱塞佩·马志尼非常敬爱自己的母亲，他把自己的高尚纯洁归功于母亲坚强而又温柔的灵魂，他写了下面这封信去安慰他一位经历剧烈丧母之痛的好友。这些几乎是他的原话：
“‘My friend, thou wilt never more behold thy mother on this earth. That is the terrible truth. I do not attempt to see thee, because thine is one of those solemn and sacred sorrows which each must suffer and conquer for himself. Dost thou understand what I mean to convey by these words, It is necessary to conquer sorrow—to conquer the least sacred, the least purifying part of sorrow, that which, instead of rendering the soul better, weakens and debases it? But the other part of sorrow, the noble part—that which enlarges and elevates the soul—that must remain with thee and never leave thee more. Nothing here below can take the place of a good mother. In the griefs, in the consolations which life may still bring to thee, thou wilt never forget her. But thou must recall her, love her, mourn her death, in a manner which is worthy of her. O my friend, hearken to me! Death exists not; it is nothing. It cannot even be understood. Life is life, and it follows the law of life—progress. Yesterday thou hadst a mother on earth; Today thou hast an angel elsewhere. All that is good will survive the life of earth with increased power. Hence, also, the love of thy mother. She loves thee now more than ever. And thou art responsible for thy actions to her more, even, than before. It depends upon thee, upon thy actions, to meet her once more, to see her in another existence. Thou must, therefore, out of love and reverence for thy mother, grow better and cause her joy for thee. Henceforth thou must say to thyself at every act of thine, "Would my mother approve this?" Her transformation has placed a guardian angel in the world for thee, to whom thou must refer in all thy affairs, in everything that pertains to thee. Be strong and brave; fight against desperate and vulgar grief; have the tranquillity of great suffering in great souls; and that it is what she would have.'" 
“‘我的朋友，你再也无法在这世上见到你的母亲了。这是一个可怕的事实。我不忍见你，因为你现在所经历的，正是每个人都必须要经受的，而且必须独自战胜的那种庄严而神圣的不幸。你明白我这话的意思吗？必须要战胜悲伤——要战胜它不神圣、不纯洁的一面，因为它会使我们的灵魂变得柔弱和卑微，而不会使之变得高尚。但是悲伤的另一面，它高尚的那一面——那使我们精神高尚而伟大的一面——应该永远保存，不可遗弃。世上没有任何东西能取代一位好母亲。日后，生活可能还会再带给你悲伤和安慰，你却永远不会忘记你的母亲。但是你要用配得上母亲的方式怀念和热爱她，为她的死亡哀悼。啊，我的朋友，听我说！死亡并不存在，它不算什么。它甚至不可理喻。生活就是这样，它遵循着生命的法则——过程。昨日你在这世上有一位母亲，今日你在某处有一位天使。但凡善良的东西都会在这世上留存，并且它的力量会与日俱增。因此，你母亲的爱也是一样。她现在比以前更爱你。你为母亲做的事情承载着比以前更多的责任。能否再见到她，与她在另一个世界相聚，完全取决于你，取决于你的行为。因此，出于对她的爱和尊敬，你必须不断成长，让她为你高兴。以后无论做什么事，你都应该问自己：‘我的母亲会不会同意我这样？’她走了，化成了天使，在这世上守护你。你自己所有的事、每一件和你有关的事，你都要同这个天使商量。你要坚强、勇敢，同令人绝望的、粗俗的悲伤斗争，在剧痛中保持灵魂的宁静。这才是她所希望的。’”
"Garrone," added the teacher, "be strong and tranquil, for that is what she would have. Do you understand?" 
“卡隆，”老师补充道，“要坚强和平静，因为那是她所希望的。你明白吗？”
Garrone nodded assent, while great and fast-flowing tears streamed over his hands, his copy-book, and his desk.
卡隆同意地点点头，大颗大颗的泪珠簌簌地落下，流到他的手背、字帖和桌上。
